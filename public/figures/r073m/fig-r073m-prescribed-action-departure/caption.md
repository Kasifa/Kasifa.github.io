**Figure R0.73M. Finite prescribed-action recoding diagnostic.**
(a) The finite action-normalized gain
\(g^{(0)}_{N,\varepsilon}=G_{N,\varepsilon}
\exp(-A_{N,0}/\varepsilon)\) for three Fourier cutoffs; the vertical scale is
focused and the cutoff curves coincide to plotting precision. Here
\(A_{N,0}\) is the separately computed finite inviscid action proxy, not the
continuum action. (b) At \(N=64\), the registered coefficients
\(\lVert b_N\rVert/\varepsilon\) and
\(\lVert\Pi_{\pm1}c_N\rVert/\varepsilon^2\) remain order one over the sampled
finite grid. (c) The signed cubic alignments, divided by
\(\varepsilon^2\), are shown separately for the mean-mediated and doubled-
harmonic paths; the zero line preserves the sign information. (d) Within
each requested gate family, the largest numerical discrepancy is divided by
its frozen tolerance; the line at one is the fail threshold and the stems are
distance guides, not uncertainty intervals. The independent family includes
the second linear solver and the independently written vorticity-FFT
hierarchy calculation. All panels are finite binary64 diagnostics and do not
certify a continuum limit or any Navier--Stokes regularity claim.
