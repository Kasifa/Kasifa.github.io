classical type-I scale.** (a) After subtracting the common exponent
\(A=4\pi C_S^2V^2h\), the exact fractional-Volterra gain
\(E_{1/2}(\sqrt A)\) tends to the constant remainder \(\log2\).  The optimized
Bielecki certificate has the same exponent and retains only the polynomial
remainder \(\log(2eA)\).  (b) Failure of the R0.69D packet-stability gate
forces the shell variable \(x=2C_S\sqrt{\pi h}\,V\) above the certified
threshold \(x_\rho=0.0926155\) in limsup, equivalently
\(\limsup V_j\sqrt{h_j}\ge0.0261264/C_S\).  Standard \(L^\infty\) local
continuation already supplies a positive lower bound on every sufficiently
late shell.  The figure therefore records a no-go result for scalar
resolvent optimization, not a new singularity criterion.
