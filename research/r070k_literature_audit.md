# R0.70K bounded primary-literature audit

**Question.** Does the existing literature already provide a deterministic,
signed evolution estimate for a spatially cutoff, trace-normalized filtered
vorticity covariance, or for its adjacent-scale correlation with a resolved
symmetric trace-free source?

**Search protocol.** The search used two bounded passes. The first covered
exact filtering identities, smooth coarse-graining, filtered enstrophy, and
vortex stretching. The second filled only two gaps: vorticity-covariance
closure and the nonlocal pressure--strain mechanism. Search stopped after ten
high-signal primary sources because new hits had entered the same structural
categories. This is a bounded-search saturation statement, not a proof that
no other relevant paper exists.

**Finding.** None of the ten sources proves a deterministic fixed-center sign
for

\[
 B_k=\frac{\operatorname{dev}\int\chi_k
             W_k\otimes W_k,dx}
            {\int\chi_k|W_k|^2dx},
 \qquad
 q_k=\Sigma_{k-1}:B_k,
 \tag{A.1}
\]

or for an adjacent-scale sum of \(q_k\). The sources do provide exact
filtering ledgers, conditional scale-locality estimates, averaged flux
theorems, and statistical/model closures. Each requires a separate inference
before it can inform R0.70K.

## 1. Germano — filtering identity

**Primary source:** M. Germano, *Turbulence: the filtering approach*, Journal
of Fluid Mechanics 238 (1992),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022112092001733).

**Direct result.** For nested linear filters that preserve constants and
commute with the relevant spatial derivatives, the exact Germano identity
relates stresses at two filter levels:

\[
 L_{ij}=T_{ij}-\widehat{\tau}_{ij}
 =\widehat{\bar u_i\bar u_j}
  -\widehat{\bar u}_i\widehat{\bar u}_j.
 \tag{A.2}
\]

**Use in R0.70K.** This is an exact ledger for adjacent-filter covariance and
stress differences. It prevents a scale difference from being treated as an
independent error.

**Additional assumptions.** Filter linearity and normalization; commutation
with derivatives on the region where the differential form is used.

**Does not provide.** A sign, positivity, a scale-decay exponent, or a way to
remove boundary terms generated by multiplication with \(\chi_k\).

## 2. Eyink--Aluie — smooth coarse-graining and scale locality

**Primary source:** G. L. Eyink and H. Aluie, *Localness of energy cascade in
hydrodynamic turbulence. I. Smooth coarse graining*, Physics of Fluids 21
(2009), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.2386),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266883).

**Direct result.** The paper derives exact large-scale, small-scale, and
band-pass energy budgets under smooth coarse-graining and proves ultraviolet
and infrared locality from velocity-increment hypotheses of the form

\[
 \|\delta u(r)\|_p=O(r^{\sigma_p}),
 \qquad0<\sigma_p<1.
 \tag{A.3}
\]

**Use in R0.70K.** It justifies smooth hierarchical filters and shows how to
separate scale locality from sign.

**Additional assumptions.** Besov/structure-function control of increments,
not merely the Leray energy inequality.

**Does not provide.** A deterministic sign for one solution, center, cutoff,
and scale. Faster decay of signed averages than absolute values involves
cancellation and numerical evidence; it is not the locality theorem itself.

## 3. Cerutti--Meneveau--Knio — filtered vorticity and enstrophy ledger

**Primary source:** S. Cerutti, C. Meneveau, and O. M. Knio, *Spectral and
hyper eddy viscosity in high-Reynolds-number turbulence*, Journal of Fluid
Mechanics 421 (2000),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022112000001671).

**Direct result.** The filtered vorticity equation contains resolved
stretching, viscosity, forcing, and the curl of the SGS-stress divergence.
The associated filtered-enstrophy equation contains
\(\widetilde\omega_i\widetilde\omega_j\widetilde S_{ij}\) and an SGS
transfer. Under stationarity, homogeneity, and an inertial-range
approximation, the paper derives an averaged balance between those terms.

**Use in R0.70K.** Tensorizing and symmetrizing the vector equation yields the
raw \(W_k\otimes W_k\) ledger. Its trace must reproduce the published
filtered-enstrophy balance and serves as a derivation check.

**Additional assumptions.** Filter/derivative commutation for the exact local
equation; stationarity, homogeneity, and inertial-range assumptions for the
averaged equality.

**Does not provide.** The cutoff-localized deviatoric tensor equation, a
tensor sign, or a Leray-energy bound for its normalized positive part.

## 4. Dascaliuc--Grujić — conditional physical-space enstrophy cascade

**Primary source:** R. Dascaliuc and Z. Grujić, *Coherent vortex structures
and 3D enstrophy cascade*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 317 (2013),
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.0058),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-012-1595-8).

**Direct result.** With additional geometric coherence of the vorticity
direction, a modified Kraichnan-scale condition, localization/modulation
conditions, and ensemble averaging over covers, the paper proves positive
upper and lower bounds for physical-space enstrophy flux over a scale range.
It also emphasizes that vortex stretching is not itself a flux term and must
be controlled separately.

**Use in R0.70K.** This is a rigorous conditional precedent for obtaining a
positive cascade statement only after geometry and source control are added.

**Additional assumptions.** Directional coherence, macro-scale conditions,
cover averaging, and modulation beyond the bare Leray energy inequality.

**Does not provide.** A fixed-center, fixed-cutoff tensor correlation or an
unconditional sign for \(\Sigma_{k-1}:B_k\).

## 5. Betchov — homogeneous kinematic cancellation

**Primary source:** R. Betchov, *An inequality concerning the production of
vorticity in isotropic turbulence*, Journal of Fluid Mechanics 1 (1956),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022112056000317).

**Direct result.** For homogeneous incompressible flow, with boundary or
averaging conditions that eliminate total divergences, the Betchov relation
connects mean strain self-amplification and mean vortex stretching:

\[
 4\langle\operatorname{tr}S^3\rangle
 =-3\langle\omega\cdot S\omega\rangle.
 \tag{A.4}
\]

**Use in R0.70K.** It is a global consistency check in periodic or homogeneous
settings and illustrates an exact kinematic cancellation.

**Additional assumptions.** Homogeneous/periodic averaging or sufficient
decay so that total divergences vanish.

**Does not provide.** The same identity after multiplying by a physical
cutoff. Localization creates boundary terms; (A.4) has neither the adjacent
scale structure nor the pointwise sign required by (A.1).

## 6. Johnson — exact multiscale strain/vorticity decomposition

**Primary source:** P. L. Johnson, *Energy Transfer from Large to Small Scales
in Turbulence by Multiscale Nonlinear Strain and Vorticity Interactions*,
Physical Review Letters 124 (2020),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.104501).

**Direct result.** Using the Gaussian-filter semigroup, the paper gives an
exact integral representation of the SGS stress across filter scales and
decomposes local energy transfer into scale-local strain self-amplification,
scale-local vortex stretching, nonlocal strain, nonlocal vorticity, and mixed
cross-scale strain--vorticity covariance terms.

**Use in R0.70K.** This is the closest published exact structure to a
source-aware neighboring-scale correlation. It shows that the local term
\(S^\ell:\omega^\ell\otimes\omega^\ell\) cannot be isolated without the
mixed cross-scale contributions.

**Additional assumptions.** Gaussian semigroup filtering for the exact
decomposition. Reported term fractions use finite-Reynolds-number, forced,
periodic isotropic DNS.

**Does not provide.** A strict Littlewood--Paley annular identity, a physical
cutoff ledger, a deterministic positive sign, or a Leray-to-critical
estimate. DNS proportions are observations, not theorems.

## 7. Goto--Saito--Kawahara — empirical vortex-tube hierarchy

**Primary source:** S. Goto, Y. Saito, and G. Kawahara, *Hierarchy of
antiparallel vortex tubes in spatially periodic turbulence at high Reynolds
numbers*, Physical Review Fluids 2 (2017),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevFluids.2.064603).

**Direct result.** In forced periodic DNS, objectively identified vortex tubes
are tracked across time. Smaller tubes are observed to be stretched mainly by
structures roughly several times larger, while comparable or smaller
structures can weaken stretching; an inertial-range hierarchy is reported
across forcing choices.

**Use in R0.70K.** It motivates a directed adjacent-scale diagnostic that
keeps positive and negative stretching contributions separate.

**Additional assumptions.** Finite-Reynolds-number DNS, periodic forcing, and
a specific tube-identification/tracking procedure.

**Does not provide.** A theorem, exact cutoff tensor budget, universal scale
ratio, or deterministic Leray control.

## 8. Danish--Meneveau — filtered velocity-gradient evolution

**Primary source:** M. Danish and C. Meneveau, *Multiscale analysis of the
invariants of the velocity gradient tensor in isotropic turbulence*, Physical
Review Fluids 3 (2018),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevFluids.3.044604).

**Direct result.** For the filtered velocity gradient \(G=\nabla\widetilde u\),
the exact evolution separates the restricted-Euler quadratic term, the
deviatoric pressure Hessian, viscosity, and the trace-free Hessian of the SGS
stress. The paper derives the corresponding invariant equations and examines
their statistics in DNS.

**Use in R0.70K.** This is the correct exact starting point for the evolution
of an actual resolved source \(\Sigma_{k-1}\). It forces the pressure Hessian,
viscosity, and SGS Hessian to remain in the source ledger.

**Additional assumptions.** Filter/derivative commutation for the exact
equation; isotropic DNS for the statistical conclusions.

**Does not provide.** A sign for the pressure Hessian, a single-solution
cutoff estimate, or control of \(\Sigma_{k-1}:B_k\) from energy alone.

## 9. Hamlington--Dahm — nonlocal pressure--strain structure

**Primary source:** P. E. Hamlington and W. J. A. Dahm, *Nonlocal form of the
rapid pressure-strain correlation in turbulent flows*, Physical Review E 80
(2009), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.2755),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.80.046311).

**Direct result.** The work starts from a Green-function representation for
rapid pressure--strain correlation. Taylor expansion of a spatially varying
mean gradient plus a modeled homogeneous-isotropic two-point correlation
produces a series involving the mean strain and repeated Laplacians.

**Use in R0.70K.** It warns that pressure--strain influence is spatially
nonlocal and should not be silently replaced by a local eddy-viscosity term.

**Additional assumptions.** The initial Green representation is exact in its
RANS setting; the explicit series and closure use Taylor and statistical
model assumptions.

**Does not provide.** A deterministic filtered-vorticity covariance identity,
an adjacent-scale sign, or a regularity estimate. Reynolds-stress anisotropy
is not the tensor \(B_k\) in (A.1).

## 10. Bernard--Berger — vorticity-covariance closure hierarchy

**Primary source:** P. S. Bernard and B. S. Berger, *A Method for Computing
Three-Dimensional Turbulent Flows*, SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 42
(1982), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/0142033).

**Direct result.** Starting from equations for mean vorticity and its
second-order covariance, the paper exhibits the unclosed hierarchy and uses a
short-time Lagrangian expansion to construct a mean-vorticity-and-covariance
closure for turbulent-flow computation.

**Use in R0.70K.** It confirms historically that vorticity covariance is a
genuine tensor variable whose statistical evolution immediately encounters
higher correlations. It helps separate an exact identity from a closure
model.

**Additional assumptions.** Averaging, a short-time Lagrangian expansion, and
closure modeling.

**Does not provide.** A deterministic filtered identity, a Littlewood--Paley
or cutoff formulation, an energy estimate, or a signed theorem for (A.1).

## Remaining research gap

The exact R0.70K identity is

\[
 \dot q_k
 =\dot\Sigma_{k-1}:B_k
 +2\operatorname{tr}
  \left[R_k(\Sigma_{k-1}-q_kI)^2\right]
 +\Sigma_{k-1}:\mathcal T_{B_k}(F_{{\rm err},k}).
 \tag{A.5}
\]

The literature supplies individual equations underlying the first and third
terms, but no theorem showing that their scale/time sum compensates the
nonnegative middle term using only Leray-class information. That
source-evolution compensator is the precise open gate carried into R0.70L.

The audit does not claim that a deeper identity cannot exist. It records that
the claimed R0.70K novelty is not already supplied by the ten primary sources
reviewed here, and it prevents conditional locality, averaged DNS statistics,
or closure models from being elevated to deterministic regularity estimates.
