# R0.24 minimal-face sharpness audit

## Status

R0.23 showed that the first \(N=2\) labels from the R0.22 sharp family occur
in the Taylor field generated by the optimized R0.20 datum.  Their sharp
transverse projections are exactly nonzero, but their normalized interaction
is much weaker than the specially selected full-space polarizations.

This note extends that check to \(N=3\).  A boundary selection rule reduces
the exact calculation from the full three-dimensional cube support to a
closed two-dimensional face.  GMP rational arithmetic then reaches the
required input orders \(8,7\) and output order \(16\).

The \(N=3\) result strengthens the evidence for suppression: the actual
generated gain remains of order one while the R0.22 sharp benchmark grows.
It is still only a comparison of two finite values.  No all-\(N\) estimate is
claimed.

## A closed minimal-leaf face

Every signed input label is a vertex of the cube
\(\{\pm1\}^3\).  A sum of \(L\) input leaves therefore satisfies

\[
 -L\le m_j\le L,\qquad j=1,2,3.
\tag{2.1}
\]

The R0.22 labels are

\[
 a_N=(N,-3N,3N-2),
\qquad
 b_N=(-N+1,-3N+1,-3N+1).
\]

Their minimal leaf counts are

\[
 L_A=3N,\qquad L_B=3N-1,
\]

and both saturate the lower bound in the second coordinate:

\[
 (a_N)_2=-L_A,\qquad (b_N)_2=-L_B.
\tag{2.2}
\]

The output also saturates it:

\[
 a_N+b_N=(1,-6N+1,-1),
\qquad
 (a_N+b_N)_2=-(L_A+L_B).
\tag{2.3}
\]

Equality in (2.1) has an exact consequence.  Every contributing leaf must
have second coordinate \(-1\).  Moreover, if a tree root splits
\(L=L_1+L_2\), then

\[
 m_2=m^{(1)}_2+m^{(2)}_2=-L
\]

together with \(m^{(j)}_2\ge-L_j\) forces

\[
 m^{(1)}_2=-L_1,\qquad m^{(2)}_2=-L_2.
\tag{2.4}
\]

Thus the graded faces

\[
 \mathcal F_L=\{m:m_2=-L,\ |m_1|,|m_3|\le L\}
\]

form a closed recurrence for every minimal-leaf target on this face.  No
off-face Taylor coefficient can contribute.  The four allowed input
generators are

\[
 (1,-1,1),\quad(1,-1,-1),\quad(-1,-1,-1),\quad(-1,-1,1).
\]

The ambient support capacity falls from \((L+1)^3\) to \((L+1)^2\).  After
exact aggregation, the order-sixteen face contains 320 nonzero labels out of
the 324 possible face labels.

## Exact arithmetic and regression

The cone coefficient pair \((w,\ell)\) and the quadratic field
\(\mathbb Q(t)\) are the same as in R0.23:

\[
 t^2=\frac{x(1+p^2/12)}{4(1+q^2/3)}.
\]

The implementation uses gmpy2 2.3.1 over GMP 6.3.0.  Each completed order is
written as an atomic JSON checkpoint.  A restarted calculation verifies the
root-certificate hash, quadratic radicand, order, face constraint, and
divergence-free identity before resuming.

As an exact regression test, the restricted face recurrence was run through
order ten and compared with the full R0.23 certificate.  The quadratic-field
basis coordinates of

\[
 U_5(2,-6,4),\qquad
 U_4(-1,-5,-5),\qquad
 U_{10}(1,-11,-1)
\]

match the three full-field R0.23 coefficients exactly.  This verifies that
the face restriction removes no contributing tree histories.

## The \(N=3\) coefficients

For \(N=3\),

\[
 a_3=(3,-9,7),\qquad
 b_3=(-2,-8,-8),\qquad
 a_3+b_3=(1,-17,-1).
\]

They first occur at Taylor orders \(8,7,16\).  The exact quadratic-field
calculation gives:

1. \(U_8(a_3)\) and \(U_7(b_3)\) are nonzero.
2. Their projections on
   \(d\times\beta(a_3)\) and \(d\times\beta(b_3)\) are exactly nonzero.
3. Their longitudinal cone jets are exactly nonzero.
4. The symmetrized interaction
   \(\mathcal B(U_8(a_3),U_7(b_3))
   +\mathcal B(U_7(b_3),U_8(a_3))\)
   is exactly nonzero.

The real embeddings of the exact values are:

| quantity | \(U_8(a_3)\) | \(U_7(b_3)\) |
|---|---:|---:|
| squared cosine with the R0.22 sharp transverse direction | 0.912044 | 0.610429 |
| cone mode norm | \(2.63609\times10^7\) | \(4.79636\times10^5\) |
| longitudinal jet | \(-2.43956\times10^7\) | \(4.60799\times10^5\) |
| longitudinal/transverse ratio | 12.4137 | 24.4618 |

The sharp transverse components remain substantial.  The suppression is not
caused by an emerging orthogonality selection rule.  Both cone norms are
still dominated by their longitudinal jets.

## Comparing \(N=2\) and \(N=3\)

Define the generated symmetrized gain

\[
 G_N=
 \frac{
 \mathcal N_0(
 \mathcal B(A_N,B_N)+\mathcal B(B_N,A_N))}
 {\mathcal N_0(A_N)\mathcal N_0(B_N)},
\]

where \(A_N\) and \(B_N\) are the actual generated minimal-order
coefficients.  Let \(S_N\) be the same gain for the special normalized
zero-longitudinal polarizations from R0.22.

| quantity | \(N=2\) | \(N=3\) | ratio \(N=3/N=2\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| generated gain \(G_N\) | 0.304560 | 0.361556 | 1.18714 |
| R0.22 sharp benchmark \(S_N\) | 70.2619 | 184.774 | 2.62979 |
| \(G_N/S_N\) | 0.00433464 | 0.00195675 | 0.451421 |
| \(G_N/[r(a_N)r(b_N)]\) | 0.0101520 | 0.00502161 | 0.494642 |
| selected root share of full output mode norm | \(4.57375\times10^{-4}\) | \(7.71697\times10^{-7}\) | 0.00168723 |

From \(N=2\) to \(N=3\), the actual gain rises by about \(18.7\%\), not by
the factor \(2.63\) seen in the sharp benchmark.  Relative to the radius
product, it falls by about one half.  The selected root split becomes about
593 times smaller as a share of the complete output coefficient.

The root contribution and full \(N=3\) output are not nearly orthogonal:
their transverse squared cosine is \(0.891409\ldots\).  The very small share
is mainly a magnitude hierarchy among tree histories rather than a final
directional cancellation.

## Independent finite-shell check

An independent recurrence uses the finite-\(\delta\) wavevectors and Leray
projections directly, while retaining the exact face selection.  At shell
level 7, the three \(N=3\) target coefficients have the following relative
errors against the cone limit:

| coefficient | transverse error | longitudinal-jet error |
|---|---:|---:|
| \(U_8(a_3)\) | \(4.3745\times10^{-4}\) | \(6.1225\times10^{-6}\) |
| \(U_7(b_3)\) | \(8.6201\times10^{-4}\) | \(2.1142\times10^{-6}\) |
| \(U_{16}(a_3+b_3)\) | \(1.1627\times10^{-3}\) | \(6.5123\times10^{-6}\) |

The errors decrease from shell levels 3 to 5 to 7.  This checks the cone
limit, normalization, face restriction, Leray projection, and time-order
factors through order sixteen.

## Consequence and next test

R0.24 does not restore a one-radius analytic theorem.  Two values cannot
exclude later growth of \(G_N\), and the calculation is at one rational root
center rather than the full certified box.

It does change the working hypothesis.  The full-space \(N^2\) obstruction
is not reproduced at the first two generated points.  The data instead show:

- substantial sharp transverse projections;
- persistent, large longitudinal cone jets;
- an order-one generated gain;
- a decreasing gain after radius-product normalization;
- rapid dilution of the selected sharp root split inside the full output.

The next mathematical step should not be another isolated full coefficient.
The face recurrence exposes a smaller graded system on \(\mathbb Z^2\).
The useful target is now a structural estimate on that system: either prove
that \(G_N\) is bounded or grows subquadratically, or identify a later family
that reverses the observed suppression.  A first computational probe at
\(N=4\) would require orders \(11,10,22\), but it should be designed to test a
specific recurrence inequality rather than treated as a third unexplained
data point.
