# Figure contract — R0.69J harmonic pressure quadrupole

## Question

Does subtracting the leading harmonic Taylor polynomial make the complete
far-field pressure Hessian scale-subcritical on an observation ball?

## Supported claim

The nonconstant remainder gains one factor of \(r/R\), but the exact
zero-mass, zero-dipole four-source witness retains

\[
 Q_R=\frac{3}{2\pi R^3}\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0),\qquad
 \operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0):Q_R=\frac{3}{\pi R^3}\ne0.
\]

Thus harmonic Taylor subtraction alone transfers, rather than controls, the
leading trace-free quadrupole coefficient.

## Boundary

The source is a signed scalar pressure source, not yet a realization of
\(\operatorname{tr}((\nabla u)^2)\) generated by an incompressible velocity.
The figure neither proves Navier--Stokes regularity nor constructs a
singularity.

## Non-color encoding

Positive and negative sources use circles and squares; the quadrupole axes use
opposing arrowheads; the leading and remainder curves use dashed-square and
solid-circle encodings; the gap also carries hatching. These distinctions
remain visible in grayscale.
