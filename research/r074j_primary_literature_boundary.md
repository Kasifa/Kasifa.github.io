# R0.74J primary-literature boundary

## Boundary statement

R0.74J makes no novelty or priority claim.  The bounded primary-source check
was used to exclude two already-developed directions from the release claim
set and to isolate one internal exact-family theorem.

## Direct collisions

### Admissible trajectory comparison and tube containment

Jincheng Yang's skewed-cylinder paper defines admissibility using

\[
 \varepsilon^2\fint_{Q_\varepsilon}\mathcal M(|\nabla u|)<\eta.
\]

It proves quantitative comparison for trajectories at comparable
mollification scales.  Final Lemma 6 gives the trajectory comparison and
final Proposition 7 gives the factor-\(9\) containment for intersecting
admissible cylinders during their common lifespan.  In the same-centre
neighboring scale band, the proof of final Proposition 11 also gives a
\(3/4\)-containment under a stronger large-cylinder threshold.  The
corresponding arXiv v2 labels are Lemma 9, Proposition 10, and Proposition 14.

Primary source:
[Yang, 2022](https://arxiv.org/html/2008.05588v2);
[official journal record](https://ems.press/journals/aihpc/articles/4938796);
DOI [10.4171/AIHPC/20](https://doi.org/10.4171/AIHPC/20).

R0.74J therefore does not claim \(|X_R-X_S|\lesssim R\) or adjacent-scale
moving-tube containment as a new result.

### Suitable-weak skewed-cylinder regularity

Vasseur--Yang use mollified-flow recentering, a local vorticity regularity
theorem, and the skewed-cylinder maximal function to improve derivative
integrability for suitable weak three-dimensional Navier--Stokes solutions.

Primary source:
[Vasseur--Yang, 2021](https://arxiv.org/html/2009.14291v1);
DOI [10.1007/s00205-021-01661-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-021-01661-4).

R0.74J therefore does not claim to introduce moving mollified cylinders into
suitable-weak theory.

### One-scale pressure-free epsilon regularity

Wang--Wu--Zhou prove that, for every \(\delta>0\), sufficiently small

\[
 \iint_{Q(1)}|u|^{5/2+\delta}\,dx\,dt
\]

for a suitable weak solution implies \(u\in L^\infty(Q(1/16))\), without a
pressure-smallness norm in the hypothesis.  The exponent-\(3\) case used in
R0.74I is the specialization \(\delta=1/2\).

Primary source:
[Wang--Wu--Zhou, 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022039619302086);
DOI [10.1016/j.jde.2019.05.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2019.05.003).

R0.74J therefore does not claim a new velocity-only one-scale epsilon
criterion.

## Prescribed-point limitation

Yang proves eventual admissibility for almost every spacetime point.  That
does not guarantee the conclusion at a preassigned possible singular point.

Lei--Ren use nonoverlapping hollow dissipation layers to prove a logarithmic
improvement of the Caffarelli--Kohn--Nirenberg singular-set gauge.  Their
Theorem B gives a quantitative spatial interval and Proposition 9 gives a
regular time epoch; these are distinct conclusions.  Their Theorem D also
contains an axisymmetric one-point criterion under an additional weighted
dissipation hypothesis.

Primary source:
[Lei--Ren, 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.01783v1);
DOI [10.1016/j.aim.2024.109654](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2024.109654).

The checked literature does not supply

\[
 \text{finite total energy}
 \Longrightarrow
 \text{R0.74I-small moving core at a prescribed point}.
\]

This is a finite non-hit and a scope comparison, not a theorem of
impossibility.

## Selected noncolliding internal result

The R0.74J claim is instead:

\[
 P_{R_j}^M=P_{R_j}^F\asymp B_j^3R_j^3
\]

for the already frozen explicit R0.74F--H family analysed in R0.74I, proved
from the fifth-shell background-shear floor and the inherited R0.74G upper
bound.  The four-source literature search found no corresponding functional
or theorem, but that finite non-hit is not evidence of novelty or priority and
was not used to prove the analytic lower bound.
