**R0.73H exact doubled-row subcertificate and finite harmonic-feedback
diagnostics.** (A) Exact rational and analytic lower bounds used in the
continuum doubled-row energy estimate: the nine-mode low block, analytic tail,
Schur-combined $H_0$ bound, and its time-perturbed $H_d$ bound for
$0\le d\le1/450$.  The finite rational block is only one component of this
continuum subcertificate; it is not a Galerkin proof of the PDE.  (B) Natural
quadratic and target-cubic endpoint responses at $N=64$, with log--log slopes
recomputed on the frozen fit window.  These response ratios use $d=0.01$,
which is strictly outside the theorem window
$D\le1/450\approx0.002222$, and must not be read as values at $d=D$.
(C) The corresponding compensated
quadratic, target-cubic, and signed parallel cubic coefficients; the open star
is the preregistered holdout, and its signed total decomposes into mean-row and
doubled-row paths.  (D) Cutoff, step, independent alias-free FFT, and outer-mode
checks divided by their respective preregistered tolerances; the dotted line is
the acceptance gate, and ratios below $10^{-13}$ are placed at that explicitly
labelled display floor.  The independent check consists of four formal sentinels
plus one independently recomputed holdout.  Panels B--D are finite binary64
diagnostics.  The
negative projected cubic coefficient is not a continuum saturation theorem,
cutoff agreement is not a tail proof, and the figure makes no claim about
three-dimensional vortex stretching, natural-seed instability, global
regularity, or the Clay problem.
