**Figure R0.74S-18. Fixed deletion exposes a simultaneous-time quantifier gap in an exact abstract clock test.**

**(A)** Exact Step 18 relations.  Solid arrows are literal inequalities:
\(\mathfrak H^{\rm hyb}\le\mathfrak H^{\rm fix}\le
\mathfrak O^{F,+}\le\mathfrak H^F_1\) and
\(\mathfrak L^K\le\mathfrak M^K\).  Dashed links carry the displayed known
payments: \(\mathfrak H^{\rm fix}\le\mathfrak L^K+B_Q\),
\(\mathfrak L^K\le\Pi+6\mathfrak H^{\rm fix}\), and
\(\mathfrak M^K\le\mathfrak O^{F,+}+B_Q\).  Thus
\(\mathfrak H^{\rm fix}\) and \(\mathfrak L^K\) are equivalent only at the
target \(A_R\) scale after known payments, not literally equal.

**(B)** The exact disjoint triangular clocks (S.489) for
\(N=2,M=5,H=1\).  Their support interiors are pairwise disjoint, so at most
one clock is positive at any time.  The pale dashed curves merely mark one
admissible fixed two-coordinate deletion; the identities are independent of
which two coordinates are selected.

**(C)** Exact functional values for the same clocks:
\(\mathfrak H^{\rm hyb}=0\),
\(\mathfrak H^{\rm fix}=\mathfrak L^K=1\),
\(\mathfrak O^{F,+}=\mathfrak M^K=3\), and
\(\mathfrak H^F_1=6\).  Direct labels and hatch/marker changes duplicate the
color encoding.

**(D)** With fixed \(N=2\) and \(M=N+2=4\), the exact normalized height is
\(\mathfrak H^{\rm fix}/\mathcal P^{2/3}
=H^{1/3}/(2M)^{2/3}\).  Its log--log slope is exactly \(1/3\), so the ratio
diverges as \(H\to\infty\).  This shows only that the inherited abstract
linear ledger cannot imply the desired fixed-deletion \(2/3\)-power bound.

Panels B--D are **ABSTRACT CLOCK TEST / NOT PDE DATA**.  They are not
Navier--Stokes solutions or counterexamples, do not prove the open estimates
(S.486)--(S.487), and do not solve the Navier--Stokes Millennium problem.
