# Figure R0.70C — Parity obstruction and amplitude-order separation

**A,** Exact normalized vortex stretching of the inversion-even heat-layer
seed on the invariant plane \(z=y\):
\(e^{3\nu t}\omega\cdot S\omega=-3\sin x\sin^2y\).  Simultaneous inversion
\((x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)\) reverses its sign, so an even spatial window has zero
signed mean while retaining nonzero absolute activity.  **B,** The proved
small-amplitude orders on a fixed cylinder, shown with coefficients normalized
to one: absolute annular activity is \(A_I\varepsilon^3+O(\varepsilon^4)\),
whereas signed work is \(O(\varepsilon^4)\); hence their ratio tends to zero.
The panel is an asymptotic order diagram, not measured DNS data.  The figure
is explanatory and is not used as a numerical proof.
