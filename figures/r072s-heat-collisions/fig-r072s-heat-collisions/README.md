# R0.72S formal figure package

This package archives the journal figure for the exact higher incidence
strata and two heat-law critical-point collisions in R0.72S.

The plotted curves are direct samples of exact formulas.  They cannot replace
the continuous proof in `research/r072s_report-source.md`.  No PDE solve,
root fit, regression, or finite threshold inference is performed.

The formal build is fail closed.  It requires a clean source commit, the
sealed dual-route certificate in `research/certificates/r072s`, byte-stable
package sources, final-size and grayscale QA, and explicit visual inspection.
The public PDF, SVG, and 600 dpi PNG are byte-identical copies of the masters.

Panel A shows only the projected higher-stratum spine in incidence
parameters.  Panels B and C show exact critical branches for the unique
generic \(A_2\) fold and the real-even \(A_3\) collision.  The result does not
draw every noncolliding branch: Panel B omits the surviving simple pair and
Panel C omits the persistent simple \(\phi=\pi\) branch, while both remain
included in the annotated global counts.  The result does not
classify the complete four-dimensional caustic image or its self-intersections,
prove enhanced dissipation through either collision, treat arbitrary
time-dependent phases or a general third-carrier family, or imply general
three-dimensional Navier--Stokes regularity.  Arnol'd's prior caustic and
trigonometric-polynomial topology are not claimed as new.
