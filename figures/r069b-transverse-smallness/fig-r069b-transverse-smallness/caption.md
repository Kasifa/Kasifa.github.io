**Figure R0.69B.** Critical smallness for transverse perturbations of the
periodic invariant-shear packet. Panel (a) compares a certified lower bound
for the physical Fourier amplitude, which grows exponentially, with the
source-bound periodic (BMO^{-1}) upper envelope
\((6+4\sqrt{2})\rho^r\), which decays because
\(\rho<0.797586\). Panel (b) gives the first packet depth at which this upper
envelope lies below a chosen base budget \(\tau\). The marked depths
\(11,22,32,42,72\) are exact for budgets
\(1,10^{-1},10^{-2},10^{-3},10^{-6}\). Combining the envelope with the
standard periodic Koch--Tataru small-data theorem yields a fixed critical
ball of globally regular genuinely three-dimensional perturbations for all
sufficiently large depths. The theorem's universal threshold is existential
and is not assigned a numerical value. Order-one critical perturbations,
transverse instability, and singularity remain open.
