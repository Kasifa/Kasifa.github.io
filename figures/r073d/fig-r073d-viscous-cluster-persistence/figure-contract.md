# Figure contract

The figure must provide four visibly separate panels: exact operator
reduction, finite eigenvalue diagnostics, finite kinetic-projector
diagnostics, and a closed/open claim boundary.  The figure must label every
finite curve as diagnostic and must not display a rank-one, explicit-rate,
fast-time, nonlinear, or Clay conclusion.

Required masters:

- vector PDF;
- editable SVG;
- 600 dpi PNG at journal double-column width.

The PDF and final-size PNG must be rendered and visually inspected.  A
grayscale rendering must preserve distinctions between curves and status
blocks.
