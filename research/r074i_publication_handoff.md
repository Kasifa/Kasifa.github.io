## Literal mathematical result

For every periodic suitable weak solution in the scope of the note and every
admissible fixed scale (R), R0.74I proves

\[
 \boxed{
 X_R^M\le C\bigl[(P_R^M)^{2/3}+P_R^M\bigr].}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{
 P_R^M\le1
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 X_R^M\le C(P_R^M)^{2/3}.}
\]

There is a universal one-scale threshold such that

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal E^{M,R}(z_0,8R)\le\varepsilon_{\rm tube}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad z_0\text{ is regular},}
\]

and hence another threshold with

\[
 \boxed{
 P_R^M\le\varepsilon_P
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad z_0\text{ is regular}.}
\]

This is conditional on smallness at the given scale; the note does not
produce that smallness at a possible singular point.

On the exact R0.74F--H packet family, for both
(Y_j=X_j) and (Y_j=\mathfrak C_j),

\[
 \boxed{
 \liminf_{j\to\infty}
 \frac{Y_j}{P_j^{2/3}\sqrt{1+\log_+P_j}}>0.}
\]

Therefore every universal bound with
(P^{2/3}(1+\log_+P)^\gamma) fails for fixed
(\gamma<1/2), in both Version M and Version F.  At
(\gamma=1/2), the argument gives only a positive lower ratio; it does not
prove or refute the endpoint upper estimate.  Any such endpoint upper theorem
would force the still-open matching family bound

\[
 \boxed{P_j\gtrsim B_j^3R_j^3.}
\]
