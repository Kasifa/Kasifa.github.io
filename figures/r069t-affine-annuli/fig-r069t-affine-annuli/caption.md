**Figure R0.69T. Physical-space annular carrier of a compact affine core.**
(a) Signed contributions of the smooth dyadic physical annuli to the original
Biot--Savart boundary-carrier integral, estimated from 16 independently
scrambled Sobol replicates with \(2^{22}\) pairs each. Bars show replicate
means and whiskers show 95% scramble intervals. Production is concentrated at
\(j=-2,-1\). (b) An expanded view of the small tails shows that the outer
\(j=1\) annulus is the only statistically resolved negative contribution.
(c) Dot-and-interval estimates at three nested resolutions are compared with
the exact affine-core production \(8\pi/(3\sqrt6)\); the vertical scale is
focused and each whisker is a 95% scramble interval. At the finest resolution
the estimate is \(3.4190014\pm0.0005303\) (standard error) versus the exact
\(3.4201329\), while the exploratory core-carrier cancellation ratio is
\(0.996478\). These randomized quadrature results concern one explicit cutoff
and are not interval enclosures, a global annular depletion theorem, or a
Navier--Stokes regularity result.
