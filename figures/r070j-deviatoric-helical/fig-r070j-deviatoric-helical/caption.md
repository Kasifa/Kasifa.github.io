**Figure R0.70J — Helicity does not remove the deviatoric diagonal.**
**A,** For
$S_0=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/2,-1)$, both real pure-helicity modes have
$S_0:\operatorname{dev}(\omega_\sigma\otimes\omega_\sigma)=1$ at every
phase.  The signed control $2\cos(2\theta)$ has zero phase mean but is not a
pointwise null. **B,** The phase-averaged directional symbol
$K_{S_0}(\xi)=(3\xi_3^2-1)/2$ has zero complete-sphere signed mean, while
its normalized positive-part mean is $\sqrt{3}/9$. **C,** At amplitude
$a=1$, after removing the fixed profile, time-integral, and outer/core
factors, the critical source square norm scales as $r$, the core dual square
norm as $r^{-1}$, and both their normalized Cauchy product and the signed
source--core pairing are scale invariant. All values
are closed-form analytic evaluations. This is not DNS, not a simulated
Navier--Stokes trajectory, and not evidence for blow-up, regularity, or a
Millennium-problem solution.
