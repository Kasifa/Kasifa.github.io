## Gate decision

R0.72K closes the complex-target complete-root gate left open by R0.72J in
the perturbative common-frequency-band triangular class.  The closure is a
directional real-projection argument, not a complex derivative zero theorem.
It converts the already proved mixed-row and true-cubic estimates into a
complete root ledger and rules out the whole common-band ledger after the
physical normalization.  The next unresolved interfaces are multiscale heat
windows and strong coupling.
