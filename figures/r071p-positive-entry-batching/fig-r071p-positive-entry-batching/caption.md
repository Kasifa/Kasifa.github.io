**Figure R0.71P-1. Positive-entry spatial batching and temporal packing.**
The positive-entry measure is formed by taking soft positive parts component
by component before summing. It is generally not the positive Jordan part of
a signed shell-cell aggregate. (A) For unit trace amplitude, an odd first-order zero has
`A_+=1`, `A_-=0`, so the segmented/soft entry and ordinary hard positive jump
are both one. At an even second-order touch, `A_+=A_-=1`; the segmented/soft
entry remains one while the ordinary hard positive jump is zero. Their exact
difference is `min(A_+,A_-)`. (B) In the declared finite overlap-two model,
cellwise entries `(0.9,0,4.9)` are bounded by local support budgets
`(1,2.6,5)`, and the totals obey `5.8 <= 8.6 <= 12`. The theorem replaces
this finite ledger by the bounded-overlap and Littlewood-Paley square-sum
estimate at each common entry time. (C) For the abstract smooth Hilbert paths
`C_N=N^(-1) sin(Nt)e`, `F=e`, and `Y=1` on `[0,2*pi)`, the left endpoint is
included, the right endpoint is excluded, and there are exactly `N` positive
atoms. Thus the positive-entry mass is `N`, whereas
`integral 1 dt=2 pi`, `integral |C_t|^2 dt=pi`, and
`integral d dt=pi/N^2`. Thus ordinary time budgets do not replace the
distinct entry-time counting measure at the abstract functional level. This
is not a coupled NSE multiple-face example. (D) For
`u_0=(0,cos x_1,cos x_2)` and a real-even radial multiplier with `m(1)=0`
and `m(sqrt(2))=1`, exact Fourier convolution gives four target modes,
`Y(0)=1`, `||F(0)||_2^2=1/4`, `||c||_2^2=1`, and
`<F(0),c>=1/2`. Hence `A_+=1/4=||F||_2^2/Y`, saturating the one-cell
projection constant. This is a one-sided initial jet only, with no time
integration or internal or repeated NSE face theorem.
