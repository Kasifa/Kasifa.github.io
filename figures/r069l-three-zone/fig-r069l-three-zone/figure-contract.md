# Figure contract — R0.69L three-zone pressure budget

## Question

Can the sharp \(A^{-5}\) far-tail factor make the full pressure budget small
merely by increasing the separation ratio?

## Supported claim

For
\[
 B_M=\sum_{m=2}^{M-1}2^{-5m}e_m
     +2^{-5M}\sum_{m\geq M}e_m,
\]
successive separation choices move fixed shells from the lumped tail into
the transition sum, and
\[
 \inf_M B_M=\sum_{m\geq2}2^{-5m}e_m.
\]
The plotted finite unit-shell model is an exact illustration of this
identity.

## Boundary

The figure does not show that local-energy, Morrey, pressure, or geometric
hypotheses cannot control the positive floor. It neither proves
Navier--Stokes regularity nor constructs a singularity.
