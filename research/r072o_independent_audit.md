**Verdict:** PASS WITH QUALIFICATIONS
**Date:** 2026-08-27

The physical normalization, correction stability, and superposition
interface were rebuilt independently from the R0.72L definitions.

1. The R0.72N cubic is raw. In one-carrier variables,
   \(|\delta|dx=(\varepsilon/a)dy\) and
   \(hb=O(a^3)f_1(f_0-f_2)\), so it is exactly the
   \(\mathcal C_\times\) entering the unlifted root inequality.
2. Since
   \(\Theta a^2/D^{1/3}\asymp\varepsilon^{4/3}\), the
   \(O(a^2\varepsilon^{1/2})\) raw theorem produces
   \(U_{\rm ED}=\varepsilon^{11/6}\). Treating \(1/2\) as the final
   ledger exponent would omit the physical lift.
3. The R0.72L correction \(G+\zeta e_0\) breaks the special odd launch, so
   the \(u=f_1,\ v=f_0-f_2\) equality cannot be reused verbatim. Coble--He
   is an operator-norm estimate for arbitrary initial data. The corrected
   \(h\) and \(b\) remain fixed bounded coordinate functionals, giving
   \(|hb|\lesssim a^3\|f\|_2^2\). Since \(|\zeta|=O(c_*)\), the initial
   norm remains comparable with one.
4. Dividing \(U_{\rm ED}\) by the strong local floor
   \(Z\gtrsim\varepsilon^{4/3}R^{2/3}L_{R,\varepsilon}\) gives
   \(\sqrt\varepsilon/(R^{2/3}L_{R,\varepsilon})\). Hence the exact
   closure condition is the square-root window in the report, not merely
   raw sublinearity.
5. At fixed \(R\), the available floor yields only
   \(O(\sqrt\varepsilon/\log\varepsilon)\). This is a failure of the
   present proof payment, not a lower bound on the actual normalized
   ledger and not a counterexample to a sharper theorem.
6. In the multi-carrier problem, the bound
   \(|hb|\le\rho^2\|V\|E\) retains all cross terms. Under the stated
   integrated full-propagator estimate it gives
   \(a^2N^2\sqrt\varepsilon\) with no extra power of \(N\).
7. The general-\(p\) numerator and window are conditional on that full
   estimate. Only \(p=1\) has been proved from the one-carrier semigroup.
8. The two-carrier profile
   \(U_R=\sin(R\theta)-R(R+1)^{-1}\sin((R+1)\theta)\) has
   \(U_R'(0)=U_R''(0)=0\) and \(U_R'''(0)=R(2R+1)\). This exactly shows
   that common band plus comparable amplitudes does not supply Coble--He's
   nondegenerate-critical-point hypothesis.
9. The R0.72J triangle-rich family shows that coherent cross cubics can
   attain \(N^2\). It excludes a diagonal \(O(N)\) tensorization argument,
   but it does not refute a full-superposition IED theorem with \(N^2\)
   normalization.
10. The result remains inside a triangular 2.5D solution class and supplies
    no general three-dimensional continuation criterion.

No exponent, Jacobian, inequality-direction, or conditional/unconditional
