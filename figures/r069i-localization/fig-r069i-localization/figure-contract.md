# Figure contract — R0.69I localized strain commutators

## Question

Does multiplying the global strain identities by a spatial cutoff preserve
their cancellation or produce a scale-improving defect?

## Supported claim

The unweighted pressure and Betchov pairings vanish exactly, whereas the
certified weighted pairings are

\[
 \int\phi S:H=-\frac{676}{40425},\qquad
 \int\phi\operatorname{tr}(A^3)=\frac{228}{2695}.
\]

The two pressure boundary terms sum exactly to the first value. Every
localized term has Navier--Stokes scaling degree three, so bare cutoff
localization supplies neither a sign cancellation nor a lower scaling degree.

## Boundary

The figure closes only bare spatial localization as an automatic source of
gain. It does not rule out harmonic-tail, multiscale, Morrey, or geometric
control of the commutators, and it neither proves Navier--Stokes regularity
nor constructs a singularity.
