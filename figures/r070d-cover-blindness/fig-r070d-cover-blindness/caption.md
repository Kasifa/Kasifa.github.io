# Figure R0.70D — Fixed-scale positivity can hide order-one negative mass

**A,** Analytic scalar witness
\(f_{\delta,N}(x_1)=\delta+\sin(Nx_1)\) with \(\delta=0.12\) and \(N=18\).
The rust fill marks its negative part.  The blue curve is the exact convolution
with a translated wrapped-Gaussian observer of fixed width \(\sigma=0.20\);
its unresolved Fourier mode is attenuated by
\(\exp(-\sigma^2N^2/2)\), so every translated coarse observation remains
positive.  This observer is illustrative; Theorem 3.1 is uniform over the
locked cutoff class.  **B,** Exact normalized negative-to-signed ratio from
formula (3.4), together with its \(1/(\pi\delta)\) leading asymptote.  The
negative mass tends to \(1/\pi\) while every resolved signed observation tends
to zero from above.  This is an analytic scalar construction, not measured
DNS data and not a realization of Navier--Stokes flux or annular vortex
stretching.
