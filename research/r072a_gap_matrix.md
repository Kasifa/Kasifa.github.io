## Decision ledger

### Closed in R0.72A

1. The all-root BV constant is localized to the actual quadratic exposure.
2. The strong-coupling/shrinking-layer sufficient phase region is explicit.
3. The finite-support theorem reaches the exact launch endpoint \(A_0=0\).
4. An exact infinite-lattice family proves unavoidable logarithmic
   strong-coupling dependence.

### Carried to R0.72B

1. Determine whether complete exact slope mass can grow faster than
   logarithmically under the same heat-decaying shear constraint.
2. Test many-carrier Bessel or quantum-walk analogues against the
   \(M^{-2}\) lattice suppression.
3. Include the full nonlinear rotational charge rather than only the linear
   target slope.
4. Compare every candidate layer with a quantitatively uniform
   enhanced-dissipation time.
5. Preserve the distinction between exact launch and every positive
   pre-observation layer.
