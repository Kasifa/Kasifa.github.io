六十轮之后，
真正留下了什么

我没有解决三维 Navier–Stokes 千禧年问题，也没有得到任意大数据的全局先验估计或有限时奇点。真正留下来的是一张更精确的障碍地图：哪些临界传递确实可以饱和，哪些有限维接力会被外部能量压倒，哪些解析主级数可以严格闭合，以及为什么最新的高频包最终落入全局光滑的剪切类。这份回顾只记录能够被证明、复算或明确证伪的部分。

00 / Boundary first

先把最重要的否定句写在最前面

OPEN · CLAY PROBLEM没有解决

尚无任意光滑无散初值的全局正则证明，也没有构造有限时破裂。

OPEN · A PRIORI CONTROL没有一般大数据估计

现有闭合只发生在特殊频率模型、形式递推或对称剪切类中。

PROVED · STRUCTURE得到多项精确结构定理

包括临界核障碍、支撑排除、解析域和不变剪切约化。

CERTIFIED · COMPUTATION有限计算可复现

精确有理数、区间证明、资源日志和哈希只支持其声明范围。

因此，这不是“距离解决只差一步”的记录，而是一项把错误直觉逐层削掉的长期研究。负面结果只有在关闭了一个清晰、此前可信的机制时才有价值；计算结果只有在与解析定理的边界分开时才可信。

01 / Five-stage ledger

研究路线经历了五次实质转向

R0.00–R0.08从能量恒等式、临界缩放和 Fourier--Leray 三波核出发，构造近对角频率包、短时泄漏模型与最小非共面网络。结论是：临界大小的瞬时传递可以发生，但相位和支撑结构极易失稳。geometry
R0.09–R0.20尝试有限壳接力、五阶树与振幅—极化优化。目标通道可以被显著增强，但完整同阶外部能量仍占主导；局部最优不等于跨尺度级联，更不等于奇点。finite cascade
R0.21–R0.37转向解析主级数与尖锐边缘递推。有限电荷截断被证明不闭合；随后用无限电荷阶梯、显式主控函数和加权 Wiener 范数建立严格解析域与短延拓。majorant
R0.38–R0.54用尾部分离、荷—次数格点、端点凸性和乘积仿射权逐步优化同一约化系统的认证半径，最终把完整乘积仿射族压到严格窄区间。所得是约化递推的解析控制，不是一般三维解的正则性。certified radius
R0.55–R0.60重新回到完整 Fourier--Leray 算子：标量荷不能制造壳衰减，法向极化通道可达到常数一，Duhamel 热分母仍允许临界饱和，多输出也不能自动恢复衰减；最后发现 R0.59 构造严格属于全局正则剪切类。full operator

这五段不是一条不断接近奇点的直线。每次转向都源于上一阶段出现了一个可以严格表达的障碍：外部能量、导数损失、无限电荷尾、临界通道饱和，或隐藏的对称不变类。

02 / What survives

真正可以保留下来的，是四组可审计成果

一、完整 Fourier--Leray 核的临界障碍

R0.55–R0.59 给出一条连贯结论：尺度分离本身不产生统一小因子；面内通道可以衰减，而法向通道保留常数一障碍；即使加入有符号聚合、热积分、Rudin--Shapiro 相位压平和多目标平方函数，某些临界范数仍可保持饱和。这些结果告诉我，任何正则性机制都必须使用比“高频输入所以低频输出必然小”更深的结构。

二、有限级联模型的完整外部能量账本

R0.09–R0.20 没有产生自维持级联，却把失败原因从数值印象变成精确比值、全局优化和 Hessian 证书。目标输出可以优化，但非目标树与外部模态不能忽略。这个结论为以后设计有限维候选设置了更高的验收门槛。

三、无限荷解析主级数的严格闭合

R0.29–R0.54 建立了从非闭合有限电荷带到无限阶梯解析域、短延拓、精确尾界和权重优化的完整计算链。这里最可靠的贡献是证明工程：形式恒等式、全阶定理与有限回归分别归档；但它是否足以形成独立数学论文，仍取决于与真实 Navier--Stokes 系数系统的联系和现有文献的新颖性核查。

四、R0.59 候选的完整非线性结构判定

R0.60 证明该高频包保持 (u=(0,F(x_1,t),G(x_1,x_2,t))) 的平行剪切结构，完整 Picard 森林塌缩为单链，三次目标投影严格消失，三、五、七、九阶均不能触及目标平面。更重要的是，每个固定光滑频率包在该类内全局光滑，所以它不是候选奇点。

03 / Routes closed

被排除的路线本身构成了研究地图

原先可能的机制现在的判定关闭原因
有限壳三波接力自然形成级联未成立高阶外部树和非目标能量压倒指定通道。
固定有限电荷带可闭合全阶递推严格排除典范输运恒等式必然把电荷推向更高层。
尺度分离自动给 Fourier--Leray 小因子严格排除法向极化通道在任意壳比下保持常数阶。
热分母、多输出或平方函数恢复统一衰减反例排除相干包在临界热 Besov 与周期 (BMO^{-1}) 中继续饱和。
R0.59 三次回返可能启动非线性目标增长严格排除剪切单链的载频算术给出 (3H/4) 以上的三次间隙。
R0.59 高频包可能成为奇点候选严格排除完整动力学处在全局光滑的平行剪切不变类中。

“严格排除”只针对表中写明的机制和假设，不外推为对所有可能奇点机制的否定。

04 / Value assessment

对数学研究有价值；对 Clay 问题的直接推进仍低

我的当前判断。如果把“价值”理解为接近证明任意三维解全局正则或构造有限时奇点，那么现阶段价值偏低。如果把价值理解为找到精确障碍、证明若干自然方案不可能、建立可复算的临界反例族，并把下一次计算缩到唯一可证伪对象，那么价值是中等且真实的。

最重要的进步不是某个更大的数值增益，而是研究标准发生了变化：不再把第一 Picard 项当成完整解，不再把支撑可达当成系数非零，不再把有限阶回归当成全阶定理，也不再把特殊对称类的光滑性误写成一般三维结论。

距离 Clay 问题仍缺少核心桥梁：一个在真正三维、破坏剪切对称的演化中可持续的机制，以及能够控制全部高阶余项的临界或超临界估计。现有结果没有提供这座桥。

06 / R0.61 decision tree

下一步只有一个对象：四次目标系数

R0.60 已证明三次目标严格为零。令 (t_H=(\log2)/(2H^2))，R0.61 将沿唯一 Picard 链精确计算 (Pi_0G_4(t_H))，并与 R0.59 的二次目标比较。需要判断的不是“数值看起来大不大”，而是是否存在独立于 (M) 的 (C\varepsilon^2/L^2) 型相对上界。

  1. 若得到统一衰减：剪切频率包路线将被更深地关闭，研究优先级转向破坏不变类的真正三维扰动。
  2. 若得到随 (M) 增长的严格下界：继续证明五阶以上余项在同一时间尺度可控；没有余项定理，就仍不能升级为完整非线性结论。
  3. 若完整路径再次精确抵消：提出更高阶对称或守恒律，并优先证明结构定理，而不是继续盲目枚举。
停止条件。无论出现哪一条分支，只有当结果改变对一般三维扰动的判断时，这条路线才继续保有对 Clay 问题的优先级。

After sixty rounds,
what actually remains?

I have not solved the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem. I have neither obtained a global a priori estimate for arbitrary large data nor constructed a finite-time singularity. What remains is a sharper obstruction map: which critical transfers really saturate, which finite-dimensional relays are overwhelmed by exterior energy, which analytic majorants close rigorously, and why the latest high-frequency packet ultimately lies in a globally regular shear class. This recap records only statements that can be proved, recomputed, or clearly falsified.

00 / Boundary first

The most important negative statement comes first

OPEN · CLAY PROBLEMNot solved

There is no proof of global regularity for arbitrary smooth divergence-free data and no construction of finite-time breakdown.

OPEN · A PRIORI CONTROLNo general large-data estimate

Closure has occurred only in special frequency models, formal recurrences, or a symmetric shear class.

PROVED · STRUCTURESeveral exact structural theorems

These include critical-kernel obstructions, support exclusions, analytic domains, and invariant-shear reduction.

CERTIFIED · COMPUTATIONFinite computations are reproducible

Exact rationals, interval proofs, resource logs, and hashes support only their stated scope.

This is therefore not a record of being “one step from a solution.” It is a long-term attempt to remove false intuitions one layer at a time. A negative result matters only when it closes a clear and previously plausible mechanism; a computation is trustworthy only when its boundary is kept separate from the analytic theorem.

01 / Five-stage ledger

The programme changed direction five times

R0.00–R0.08I began with the energy identity, critical scaling, and Fourier–Leray triad kernel, then built near-diagonal packets, short-time leakage models, and minimal non-coplanar networks. Critical-size instantaneous transfer is possible, but phase and support coherence are fragile.geometry
R0.09–R0.20I tested finite-shell relays, fifth-order trees, and amplitude–polarisation optimisation. The target channel can be strengthened, but full same-order exterior energy remains dominant. A local optimum is not a cascade and is not a singularity.finite cascade
R0.21–R0.37I moved to analytic majorants and sharp edge recurrences. A finite charge cutoff was proved not to close. An infinite charge ladder, explicit majorants, and weighted Wiener norms then produced rigorous analytic domains and a short continuation step.majorant
R0.38–R0.54Tail separation, charge–degree lattices, endpoint convexity, and product-affine weights progressively improved the certified radius of the same reduced system. This controls a reduced recurrence, not the regularity of general three-dimensional solutions.certified radius
R0.55–R0.60I returned to the full Fourier–Leray operator. Scalar charges do not create shell decay; the normal polarisation channel attains the constant-one obstruction; Duhamel heat denominators and multiple outputs still permit critical saturation; the R0.59 packet was finally shown to lie in a globally regular shear class.full operator

These stages do not form a straight line towards a singularity. Each turn was forced by a precisely stated obstruction in the previous phase: exterior energy, derivative loss, an infinite charge tail, critical-channel saturation, or a hidden invariant symmetry.

02 / What survives

Four auditable groups of results remain

1. Critical obstructions in the full Fourier–Leray kernel

R0.55–R0.59 form a coherent conclusion. Scale separation alone supplies no uniform small factor. The in-plane channel may decay, while the normal channel retains a constant-one obstruction. Signed aggregation, heat integration, Rudin–Shapiro phase flattening, and multi-target square functions can all leave selected critical norms saturated. Any regularity mechanism must therefore use more than the slogan that high-frequency input should produce a small low-frequency output.

2. A complete exterior-energy ledger for finite cascade models

R0.09–R0.20 did not produce a self-sustaining cascade. They did turn the failure into exact ratios, global optimisation, and Hessian certificates. The target can be optimised, but non-target trees and exterior modes cannot be neglected. Future finite-dimensional candidates must meet this stronger acceptance test.

3. Rigorous closure of an infinite-charge analytic majorant

R0.29–R0.54 give a complete computational chain from the failure of a finite charge band to infinite-ladder analytic domains, continuation, exact tail bounds, and weight optimisation. Its most reliable contribution is proof engineering: formal identities, all-order theorems, and finite regression are archived separately. Independent publication still depends on the connection to the genuine Navier–Stokes coefficient system and on a novelty review.

4. A full nonlinear classification of the R0.59 candidate

R0.60 proves that the packet preserves the plane-parallel form (u=(0,F(x_1,t),G(x_1,x_2,t))). The full Picard forest collapses to a single chain; cubic target projection vanishes exactly; and orders three, five, seven, and nine cannot reach the target plane. More importantly, every fixed smooth packet is globally smooth in this class, so it is not a singularity candidate.

04 / Value assessment

Mathematically useful; still low in direct value for the Clay problem

My present assessment. If value means proximity to global regularity for arbitrary three-dimensional data or to a finite-time singularity, the present value is low. If it means identifying exact obstructions, proving several natural schemes impossible, building reproducible critical counterexample families, and reducing the next computation to one falsifiable object, the value is moderate and real.

The main improvement is not a larger numerical gain but a change in research standards. I no longer treat the first Picard term as the full solution, support accessibility as a nonzero coefficient, finite-order regression as an all-order theorem, or smoothness in a symmetric class as a general three-dimensional conclusion.

The central bridge to the Clay problem is still missing: a persistent mechanism in genuinely three-dimensional evolution that breaks shear symmetry, together with a critical or supercritical estimate controlling all higher-order remainders. None of the current results supplies that bridge.

05 / Publication value

The plausible papers are bounded technical studies, not a solution claim

CANDIDATE A · STRONGERA family of critical Fourier–Leray obstructions

Unify R0.55–R0.60 as kernel decomposition, coherent saturation, heat integration, multiple outputs, and the shear boundary. A systematic novelty review is required before submission.

CANDIDATE B · CONDITIONALExact analytic majorants and weight optimisation

Present R0.29–R0.54 as an all-order analysis of the reduced transport system, provided its independent mathematical object and non-decorative link to Navier–Stokes are made explicit.

SUPPLEMENT · REPRODUCIBILITYMachine-verifiable appendices

Exact rational arithmetic, interval certificates, resource logs, figure packages, and hashes are strong supplementary materials, but they do not replace mathematical novelty.

NOT READYA Millennium Problem claim

There is no basis for it. Any statement of a solution, major breakthrough, or proximity to a singularity would exceed the evidence.

06 / R0.61 decision tree

The next object is unique: the quartic target coefficient

R0.60 proves that the cubic target is exactly zero. At (t_H=(\log2)/(2H^2)), R0.61 will compute (Pi_0G_4(t_H)) along the unique Picard chain and compare it with the R0.59 quadratic target. The question is not whether a numerical sample looks large, but whether there is a relative bound of the form (C\varepsilon^2/L^2) independent of (M).

  1. If uniform decay holds: the shear-packet route is closed more deeply, and priority moves to genuinely three-dimensional perturbations that break the invariant class.
  2. If a rigorous lower bound grows with (M): the next task is to control every remainder of order five and above on the same time scale. Without a remainder theorem, there is still no full nonlinear conclusion.
  3. If the full path sum cancels again: formulate and prove a higher-order symmetry or conservation law instead of continuing blind enumeration.
Stopping rule. Whichever branch occurs, this route retains priority for the Clay problem only if it changes the assessment of genuinely three-dimensional perturbations.
