**Figure R0.69L | Increasing the separation ratio migrates the pressure
budget instead of eliminating it.** The illustration assigns unit normalized
energy to shells \(m=2,\ldots,12\). (a) As \(M\) increases, the lumped far
term \(2^{-5M}\sum_{m\geq M}e_m\) shrinks, but those shells enter the
transition sum. The total decreases to the strictly positive exact floor
\(\sum_{m\geq2}2^{-5m}e_m\). (b) A shell beyond the chosen split initially
shares the far-tail plateau; after the split passes it, the shell retains its
own \(2^{-5m}\) weight. The plot illustrates an exact parameter-migration
identity, not a regularity criterion.
