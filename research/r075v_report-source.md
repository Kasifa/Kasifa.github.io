- N. K. Singh and S. Sridhar, *Plane shearing waves of arbitrary form:
  exact solutions of the Navier--Stokes equations*, arXiv:1101.5507,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.5507>.

  shearing waves and explains when parallel Kelvin modes may be superposed

### Constant and variable shear mixing

- J. Bedrossian, V. Vicol, and F. Wang, *The Sobolev stability threshold for
  2D shear flows near Couette*, arXiv:1604.01831,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01831>.
- Relevance: this is primary neighboring context for shear-induced phase
  mixing and diffusion.
- Boundary: V uses exact finite Fourier phases and elementary time
  integration by parts.  It does not import a nonlinear Couette stability
  or enhanced-dissipation estimate.

### Torus spectral observability

- M. Egidi and I. Veselic, *Scale-free unique continuation estimates and
  Logvinenko--Sereda Theorems on the torus*, arXiv:1609.07020v6,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07020>.
- Relevance: the paper proves scale-free uncertainty and observability
  estimates for functions with Fourier support in finitely many
  parallelepipeds.
- Boundary: V uses the already frozen two-wave collar coercivity from T and
  proves a signed radial-flux identity.  No Logvinenko--Sereda constant or

### Official Millennium problem boundary

- Relevance: it fixes the global three-dimensional existence/smoothness or
  breakdown target.

  smooth two-harmonic shear subclass.  It proves neither global regularity
