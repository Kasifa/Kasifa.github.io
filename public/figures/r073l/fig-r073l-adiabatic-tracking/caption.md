**Figure R0.73L. Finite selected-orbit adiabatic diagnostic.**
(a) Action-normalized gain along the complete slow interval at cutoff
\(N=64\); the focused scale resolves deviations from the neutral value one.
(b) Terminal complementary leakage against \(\varepsilon\); the dashed
\(O(\varepsilon)\) reference is anchored at the smallest parameter rather than

to plotting precision. (c) Backward-action residual obtained solely by dividing
two points on the same forward orbit; no backward parabolic solve is used.
(Panel (c) reuses the line and marker encoding from panel (a).)
(d) Maximum numerical discrepancies divided by their frozen tolerances; the
line at one is the fail threshold, and the vertical segments are distance
guides rather than uncertainty intervals. Primary trajectories use DOP853, while the
independent reconstruction uses midpoint matrix-exponential products. All
panels are finite Fourier diagnostics and do not prove the continuum theorem.
