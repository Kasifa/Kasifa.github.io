# R0.23 generated-subspace sharpness audit

## Status

This note checks the first place where the sharp label family from R0.22 can
occur in the Taylor coefficients generated by the optimized R0.20 datum.  The
calculation is exact at the rational center of the certified R0.20 root box.

The result has two parts.  First, the actual generated coefficients do not
avoid the R0.22 sharp transverse directions: the relevant projections are
exactly nonzero.  Second, at the first encounter \(N=2\), their longitudinal
jets strongly suppress the normalized bilinear gain.  Thus a simple invariant
subspace exclusion is unavailable, but the full-space sharp example is also
not representative of this first generated pair.

This is a finite-order statement at one root center.  It is not uniform on the
root box and does not determine the behavior as \(N\to\infty\).

## Exact cone-limit recurrence

Let \(d=(1,1,1)\) and use the cube label \(m\in\Lambda\) from R0.21--R0.22:

\[
 K_\delta(m)=\delta^{-1}q(m)d+\beta(m),
 \qquad
 q(m)=\frac{3m_1-m_3}{12}.
\]

For a divergence-free coefficient \(u_{m,\delta}\), define its cone pair by

\[
 w_m=\lim_{\delta\to0}u_{m,\delta},
 \qquad
 \ell_m=\lim_{\delta\to0}\delta^{-1}d\cdot u_{m,\delta}.
\]

It obeys

\[
 d\cdot w_m=0,
 \qquad
 q(m)\ell_m+\beta(m)\cdot w_m=0.
\tag{2.1}
\]

For an ordered interaction \(m=a+b\), put

\[
 s(a,b)=\beta(b)\cdot w_a+q(b)\ell_a.
\]

If \(q(m)\ne0\), the cone-limit Leray projection gives

\[
 w_m^{\rm out}=s(a,b)w_b,
 \qquad
 \ell_m^{\rm out}=-\frac{\beta(m)\cdot w_m^{\rm out}}{q(m)}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

If \(q(m)=0\) and \(\beta(m)\ne0\), then

\[
 w_m^{\rm out}=s(a,b)P_{\beta(m)^\perp}w_b,
 \qquad
 \ell_m^{\rm out}=s(a,b)\ell_b.
\tag{2.3}
\]

The zero Fourier mode vanishes.  Equations (2.1)--(2.3) form a closed exact
bilinear system.

For the pure nonlinear Taylor coefficients,

\[
 (k+1)U_{k+1}=-\sum_{j=0}^{k}\mathcal B(U_j,U_{k-j}).
\tag{2.4}
\]

The audit aggregates every ordered interaction through time order ten.  The
order-ten field has 1720 exactly nonzero labels after aggregation.

## Why the arithmetic is algebraic, not floating point

At the R0.20 center, let \(p,q,x\in\mathbb Q\) be the certified decimal
coordinates.  The squared normalization factors for the pump and catalyst
polarization charts are

\[
 U=1+\frac{p^2}{12},
 \qquad
 V=1+\frac{q^2}{3}.
\]

After removing the pump scale common to every leaf at a fixed Taylor order,
the catalyst-to-pump weight is

\[
 t=\sqrt{\frac{xU}{4V}}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

The radicand in (3.1) is a positive rational number and is not a rational
square.  Hence every coefficient is represented uniquely as \(A+Bt\), with
\(A,B\in\mathbb Q\).  Exact nonvanishing is therefore decided by the pair
\((A,B)\), not by a numerical threshold.

As an independent implementation check, the full finite-\(\delta\) Leray
recurrence was evaluated at shell levels \(3,5,7\).  At level 7, the three
audited coefficients agree with the cone limit to relative transverse error
below \(8.3\times10^{-4}\); the longitudinal-jet errors are below
\(1.6\times10^{-6}\).

## First encounter with the R0.22 family

For \(N=2\), the sharp labels are

\[
 a=(2,-6,4),\qquad b=(-1,-5,-5),\qquad a+b=(1,-11,-1).
\]

Their minimal leaf counts are \(6,5,11\), so they first occur at Taylor orders
\(5,4,10\), respectively.  The exact recurrence gives the following facts.

1. Both input coefficients are nonzero.
2. Their projections on \(d\times\beta(a)\) and
   \(d\times\beta(b)\) are exactly nonzero.
3. Their longitudinal jets are also exactly nonzero.
4. The two ordered interactions
   \(\mathcal B(U_5(a),U_4(b))\) and
   \(\mathcal B(U_4(b),U_5(a))\) do not cancel exactly.

The numerical embeddings of the exact algebraic quantities are:

| quantity | left \(a\) | right \(b\) |
|---|---:|---:|
| squared cosine with the R0.22 sharp transverse direction | 0.298122 | 0.788361 |
| cone mode norm | \(2.63579\times10^5\) | \(7.80653\times10^3\) |
| longitudinal jet | \(-2.52529\times10^5\) | \(7.17024\times10^3\) |

The transverse projections are substantial, but neither coefficient is close
to the special zero-longitudinal polarization used to prove R0.22 sharpness.

## Size of the first sharp-label interaction

Use the cone mode norm

\[
 \mathcal N_0(w,\ell)=|w|+|\ell|.
\]

For the actual generated pair, the symmetrized operator gain is

\[
 \frac{\mathcal N_0(\mathcal B(U_5(a),U_4(b))
 +\mathcal B(U_4(b),U_5(a)))}
 {\mathcal N_0(U_5(a))\mathcal N_0(U_4(b))}
 =0.3045602145\ldots.
\tag{5.1}
\]

At the same labels, the special normalized R0.22 polarizations give

\[
 70.2619267363\ldots.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The ratio of (5.1) to (5.2) is

\[
 0.0043346408\ldots.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Thus the first generated pair realizes only about \(0.43\%\) of the
full-space sharp-polarization gain.  In the complete order-ten coefficient,
this one root split contributes about \(4.57\times10^{-4}\) of the full cone
mode norm.  Its transverse direction is nevertheless close to that of the
full output: their squared cosine is \(0.97339\ldots\).  The small share is
therefore mainly a magnitude effect, not a near-orthogonal cancellation.

## Consequence and boundary

The most optimistic structural escape from R0.22 would have been an exact
selection rule forcing every sharp transverse projection to vanish.  R0.23
rules out that simple mechanism already at \(N=2\).

The calculation also shows why this does not yet restore the negative result
from the full mode space.  The generated coefficients carry large
longitudinal jets, and the normalized sharp-label interaction is over two
orders of magnitude smaller than the specially chosen R0.22 pair.

There is no justified all-\(N\) conclusion from one value of \(N\).  The next
decisive calculation is \(N=3\): orders \(8,7,16\).  It should measure the
growth of

\[
 \frac{\mathcal N_0(\mathcal B(A_N,B_N)+\mathcal B(B_N,A_N))}
 {\mathcal N_0(A_N)\mathcal N_0(B_N)}
\]

and the longitudinal-to-transverse ratios.  Because exact Fraction arithmetic
already takes about five minutes at order ten, the order-sixteen run should
use GMP-backed rationals, target-aware convolution, checkpointed progress,
and an independent finite-shell check.
