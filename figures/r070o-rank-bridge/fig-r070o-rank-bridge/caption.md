strata.** **A**, for the scalar Bessel filter
\(m(k)=(1+|k|^2)^{-1}\), the squared response at frequency \(Ne_2\) is

the same observation requires the exact factor
\(A(N)^{-1}=(1+N^2)^2\sim N^4\), exposing the missing all-frequency lower
frame. **C**, writing \(x=\lambda_3/E\) and

\(0\le x\le1/3\), \(2x\le y\le(1+x)/2\). With
\(\delta=1/20\), the mutually exclusive partition marks coercive states

continuation, blow-up, global-regularity, or Millennium-problem result.
