# Figure R0.60-1 — Support thresholds in the invariant shear chain

**English.** The R0.59 packet lies in the exact invariant class
\(u=(0,F(x_1,t),G(x_1,x_2,t))\).  (a) The full binary Picard forest therefore
collapses to the ordered recurrence
\((\partial_t-\Delta_{12})G_n=-F_1\partial_2G_{n-1}\); every term after the
linear one is \(e_3\)-polarized and preserves the second Fourier frequency.
(b) For \(H=4N\), the exact distances from the target plane for orders
\(3,5,7,9\) are at least \(3N+1,2N+2,N+3,4\), respectively.  Hence the cubic
gap is strictly larger than \(3H/4\), while every displayed excluded odd
order remains strictly separated from zero.  At order eleven an explicit
path reaches zero for \(N\geq5\); this is support admissibility, not proof that
the complete coefficient is nonzero.  (c) The quadratic term reaches the
target plane, the cubic term can instead backtrack to the original high
\(V\)-support, and the first possible target correction is quartic.  The
cubic return and quadratic energy satisfy the exact order-four identity shown
below the panels.  The packet is globally smooth inside this symmetry class;
the result is not an arbitrary-data estimate or a solution of the
three-dimensional regularity problem.

**中文。** R0.59 高频包严格属于不变类
\(u=(0,F(x_1,t),G(x_1,x_2,t))\)。(a) 完整二叉 Picard 森林因而塌缩为单条
有序递推链；线性项之后的各阶均沿 \(e_3\) 极化，并保持第二傅里叶频率。
(b) 当 \(H=4N\) 时，三、五、七、九阶到目标平面 \(\xi_1=0\) 的精确距离
下界依次为 \(3N+1,2N+2,N+3,4\)，所以三次项具有严格大于 \(3H/4\) 的
频谱间隙。十一阶在 \(N\geq5\) 时存在一条到达零平面的显式支撑路径；这只
表示支撑允许，并不证明完整十一阶系数非零。(c) 二次项到达目标平面，三次
项则可回到原始高频 \(V\) 支撑，第一次可能修正低频目标的是四次项。三次
高频回返与二次能量满足图下方的四阶精确恒等式。该初值在此对称类内全局
光滑；这不是任意三维初值估计，也不是三维正则性问题的解答。
