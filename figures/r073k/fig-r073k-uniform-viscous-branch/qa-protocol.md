# R0.73K figure QA protocol

1. Bind every input to its repository-relative path, byte size, and SHA-256
   digest; require the primary, independent, and package validators to pass.
2. Reconstruct the 204 `N=160` core rows and nine cutoff summaries directly
   from the experiment JSON and compare every CSV field.
3. Check all 17 d nodes and 12 core viscosity levels, fixed-circle count one,
   numerical reality, the 952 cross-cutoff input rows, and all four adjacent
   cutoff pairs.
4. Verify the PDF and SVG remain vector outputs and that the PNG metadata and
   dimensions correspond to 178 mm by 118 mm at 600 dpi.
5. Inspect the final-size, grayscale, and independently rasterized PDF
   surfaces for clipping, label collisions, detached annotations, and loss of
   line or marker distinctions.
6. Fail closed if a figure claim exceeds `contract.json`. In particular, no
   finite cutoff result may be described as a continuum theorem or an
   explicit continuum viscosity threshold.
