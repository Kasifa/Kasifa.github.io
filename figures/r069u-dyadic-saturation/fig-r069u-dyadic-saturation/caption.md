**Figure R0.69U. Dyadic saturation of the affine-core boundary carrier.**
(a) Reported cancellation deficit for the core-restricted signed annular
carrier at seven dyadic cutoff radii.  Points are computed from 16 independent
scrambled Sobol replicates with \(2^{18}\) pairs per radius.  Values reported
as binary64 zero are displayed as open downward triangles at \(10^{-16}\);
this display floor is not a measured bound.  The shaded region \(R>40\) is
different: compact support of the analytic transition proves that only the
two principal annuli can occur there, so the exact theorem gives
\(\Gamma_{\rm core}=1\) for dyadic \(R\ge64\).  (b) The two principal annular
means, normalized by the exact core production \(8\pi/(3\sqrt6)\), approach
the positive analytic limits \(0.975495\) and \(0.024505\).  Whiskers show 95%
scramble intervals; dashed lines are limits computed from the fixed smooth
profile.  The rigorous construction gives the profile-independent safety
margin that the outer limiting share is at least \(1/42\).  (c) At every
radius, the difference between the QMC total and the exact core production is
less than \(1.1\) reported scramble standard errors.  Scramble errors are
diagnostics, not interval enclosures.  The exact saturation theorem concerns
the core-restricted carrier.  The full-space two-increment annular ratio is
unchanged by this self-similar dilation, so the figure is not evidence of
full-space saturation or a Navier--Stokes regularity theorem.
