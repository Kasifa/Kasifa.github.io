# R0.73E complement-transfer figure

This directory is the archival package for a four-panel, 178 mm journal figure.

- Panel A shows the six rightmost finite complementary eigenvalues after the
  leading finite cluster is removed.
- Panel B compares sampled maxima on three complementary-resolvent vertical
  lines across the stored viscosities.
- Panel C shows the intrinsic moving-complement semigroup norm and its finite
  spectral-abscissa normalization.
- Panel D compares a fixed inviscid complement with the moving viscous
  complement over the stored time grid.

The two JSON inputs are independently recomputed finite-dimensional binary64
diagnostics. They do not prove an additional continuum eigenvalue, a continuum
resolvent bound, a continuous-time semigroup estimate, nonautonomous transfer,
or a nonlinear Navier--Stokes statement. PDF and SVG are archival vector
masters; `figure.png` is the 600 dpi raster export.
