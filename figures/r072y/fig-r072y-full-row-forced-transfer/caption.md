scales.** (A) Exact row structure for the heat shear.  For positive horizontal
frequency, the Orr-Sommerfeld variable \(q=\mathcal L u_2\) drives the Squire

from Orr-Sommerfeld/Squire variables.  (B) Exact energy ratios from the
zero-coupling lift-up formula for three representative \((\xi,\Lambda)\)
choices.  Values above one give counterexamples to uniform strict contraction,
including positive-\(\xi\) mean-zero rows for sufficiently large
\(|\Lambda|\).  These curves are exact diagnostics of the closed formula, not
a stability theorem.  (C) Normalized rate guide for the scalar forced problem:
standard \(H^{-1}\) spacetime transfer has order \(\alpha\), semiclassical
\(\mathcal H^{-1}_{\alpha,\beta}\) spacetime transfer has order
\(\alpha^2\), and the standard \(H^{-1}\)-to-endpoint map has no uniform
\(\alpha\) gain.  Constants are suppressed and no exponent is fitted; the
proof and sharpness constructions are given in the analytic report.  The
complete linearized shear subsystem, nonlinear Navier-Stokes closure, and Clay
Millennium problem remain open.
