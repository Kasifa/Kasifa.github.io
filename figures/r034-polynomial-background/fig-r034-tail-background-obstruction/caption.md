The annotations give the witness tail start (s) and matrix order (r).  (b) Exhaustive principal-minor scan over tail starts (40\le s\le49) inside the available 50/49-coefficient window. Because every witness starts beyond (d), polynomial subtraction cannot alter its entries; negative determinant therefore excludes every choice of the background coefficients.
