**Figure R0.73D. Static viscous persistence of the certified periodic Rayleigh
cluster.** (A) The kinetic vorticity space is mapped isometrically to
\(L^2\) by \(U=2L_{1/4}^{-1/2}\).  The conjugated generator is
\(M+K-\varepsilon L_{1/4}\), with skew-adjoint multiplication \(M\), compact
\(K\), and the retained domain jump \(D(H_0)=L^2\),
\(D(H_\varepsilon)=H^2\). (B) Finite Fourier diagnostics show the leading
eigenvalue approaching the inherited R0.73C inviscid bracket as
\(\varepsilon\downarrow0\); cutoff agreement is not used as continuum
evidence. (C) At \(N=128\), the finite kinetic-space Riesz projector remains
bounded and its difference from the \(\varepsilon=0\) projector tends to
zero.  These curves illustrate, but do not prove, the operator theorem.
(D) The exact theorem closes a fixed contour, operator-norm Riesz projection
convergence, total algebraic multiplicity, and cluster eigenvalue convergence.
Algebraic simplicity, explicit rates, the whole complementary spectrum,
logarithmic fast-time transfer, nonlinear Navier--Stokes control, and the
Clay problem remain open.  General zero-viscosity unstable-spectrum
persistence was established by Shvydkoy and Friedlander (2008); no general
priority claim is made here.
