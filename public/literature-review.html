LITERATURE REVIEW

文献综述Literature review

本页只整理公开文献理论，不含本站研究节点、工作笔记或待执行方案。下列判据的附加假设不能被省略。This page reviews public literature only. It contains no project research nodes, working notes or planned experiments. The additional hypotheses of the criteria below remain essential.

01 · 问题边界与临界尺度01 · The problem and critical scales

问题不是是否存在某种积分意义的解，而是符合正式条件的三维光滑解能否全局延拓。二维结果、带外力的特定构造、特殊对称解和修改方程的结论，必须各自保留原设定。The issue is not whether some distributional solution exists, but whether three-dimensional smooth solutions satisfying the official conditions continue globally. Two-dimensional results, particular forced constructions, symmetric solutions and modified equations must retain their own settings.

uλ(x,t) = λu(λx,λ²t)，　‖uλ(·,t)‖₃ = ‖u(·,λ²t)‖₃

在全空间中，L³、齐次 H1/2、BMO−1 以及适当 Besov 范数具有临界缩放。对应的小数据理论各有适用的函数空间和解类；不是任意“大临界空间”都有不加区分的局部理论。能量有界仍允许向小尺度集中，涡量拉伸项则没有固定符号。In the whole space, L³, homogeneous H1/2, BMO−1 and suitable Besov norms have critical scaling. Their small-data theories use specific function spaces and solution classes; a blanket large-data local theory must not be asserted for every critical space. Bounded energy still permits small-scale concentration, while vortex stretching has no fixed sign.

02 · 五个理论视角02 · Five theoretical perspectives

强解延拓Continuation

Serrin 与 L³ 端点判据把延拓问题联系到速度的临界时空控制；关键仍是如何获得这一控制。Serrin and endpoint L³ criteria link continuation to critical space-time control of velocity. Obtaining that control remains the key issue.

部分正则性Partial regularity

CKN 理论说明适当弱解的奇异集非常小，但不说明它不存在。CKN theory shows that the singular set of a suitable weak solution is very small, not that it is absent.

剖面与刚性Profiles and rigidity

浓缩紧致性与后向唯一性研究潜在奇性的极限剖面。抽取极限需要前提，刚性结论只适用于规定的解类。Concentration compactness and backward uniqueness study limiting profiles of possible singularities. Taking such limits requires hypotheses, and rigidity applies only in specified solution classes.

涡量几何Vorticity geometry

涡量方向相干可削弱拉伸；相关条件正则性定理并未证明所有解自动具有这种几何结构。Coherence of vorticity direction can deplete stretching. The corresponding conditional regularity results do not establish that every solution automatically has this geometry.

定量正则性Quantitative regularity

临界范数的定量界约束潜在爆破的速度和集中程度；必要条件与排除奇性的矛盾论证不同。Quantitative critical-norm bounds constrain the rate and concentration of possible blowup. Necessary conditions differ from a contradiction excluding singularities.

条件结论不能换成无条件证明Conditional conclusions are not unconditional proofs

小临界数据的全局理论、特殊自相似类的排除、涡量方向判据以及定量浓缩估计，分别处理不同的附加条件。不能把它们拼接成覆盖任意光滑三维初值的结论。Small-critical-data theory, exclusions in particular self-similar classes, vorticity-direction criteria and quantitative concentration estimates each have their own assumptions. They cannot simply be combined into a result for arbitrary smooth three-dimensional data.

弱解非唯一性必须注明是否有外力、是否满足能量不等式以及初值的正则性；修改方程的爆破也必须与原方程分开。Weak nonuniqueness must be reported with its forcing, energy-inequality and initial-regularity assumptions. Blowup for a modified equation must likewise be distinguished from blowup for the original equation.

04 · 已排除、受约束与仍开放04 · Excluded, constrained and still open

已排除（附条件）：在 ESS 定理的解类与域中，临界 L∞ₜL³ₓ 有界条件下不能发生所讨论的有限时奇性。NRS 与 Tsai 的自相似排除则要求各自规定的可积性或局部能量条件，不能直接覆盖任意奇性剖面。Excluded under hypotheses: in the solution class and domain of the ESS theorem, bounded critical L∞ₜL³ₓ control rules out the finite-time singularities under consideration. The self-similar exclusions of NRS and Tsai have their own integrability or local-energy assumptions and do not cover arbitrary singularity profiles.

Nečas–Růžička–Šverák (1996) · On Leray’s self-similar solutions

Tsai (1998) · Self-similar solutions satisfying local energy estimates

受约束：Tao 的定量端点理论约束潜在奇点附近的临界范数增长。Barker–Prange 将正则性与空间浓缩联系起来；这些结果约束爆破必须具有什么性质，而不是证明一切浓缩不可能。Constrained: Tao’s quantitative endpoint theory constrains critical-norm growth near a possible singularity. Barker–Prange connect regularity with spatial concentration. These results constrain the properties that blowup would require, rather than prove that every concentration is impossible.

Tao · Quantitative bounds

Barker–Prange (2021) · Quantitative regularity via spatial concentration

仍开放：在正式一般三维设定中，没有由这些结果直接得到的全部潜在奇性排除。非自相似、随时间改变形状和跨尺度演化不因固定剖面被排除而自动消失。Still open: these results do not yield a general exclusion of all potential singularities in the official three-dimensional setting. Non-self-similar evolution, changing shapes and cross-scale dynamics are not ruled out merely by excluding fixed profiles.

方法边界：Tao 的平均方程具有有限时爆破，说明仅使用与该模型共享的能量抵消及粗估计不足以证明原方程全局光滑；该模型并不是原方程的反例。Methodological limit: Tao’s averaged equation has finite-time blowup. Energy cancellation and coarse estimates shared with that model are therefore insufficient on their own to prove global smoothness for the original equation. The model is not a counterexample for the original equation.

05 · 近期进展与证据边界05 · Recent developments and evidence boundaries

定量正则性把原先的定性端点判据变成可追踪的高阶界与爆破下界。部分正则性的进一步改进则继续限制潜在奇异集；即便集合更小，也不能把“更小”改写成“为空”。Quantitative regularity turns qualitative endpoint criteria into traceable higher-order bounds and blowup lower bounds. Further developments in partial regularity constrain the possible singular set; even a smaller set must not be described as an empty set.

Lei–Ren 的工作研究定量部分正则性及其应用。这里保留这条公开文献进展，不把它外推成一般三维全局正则性。Lei–Ren study quantitative partial regularity and its applications. This public-literature development is retained without extrapolating it to general three-dimensional global regularity.
