- Date: 2026-09-04
- Audience: mathematical analysts auditing the R0.75X--Z route
- Scope: finite Fourier clusters in one dyadic band, local observation on an
  interval, carrier-envelope modulation, and one-sided offset spectra
- Exclusions: arbitrary Navier--Stokes fields, nonlinear perturbations,
  suitable weak solutions, and any claim of novelty or priority

## Direct answer

The R0.75Z parameter partition, modulation identity, density/carrier split,
current identity, and pointwise counterexample are elementary consequences of
the displayed finite Fourier formulas.  They do not import a theorem from the
literature.

The bounded search found mature local-observation results for exponential
polynomials and functions whose Fourier support lies in one or finitely many
intervals.  Those results support the general idea that a fixed number of
spectral clusters can be observed on a sufficiently distributed set.  The
sources inspected do not, by their stated results, supply the particular
time-weighted signed collar-flux estimate needed here, nor do they remove the
carrier-sized local current created by the exact demodulation.  They therefore
remain context and possible tools for the next step, not proof of R0.75Z or of
the open cluster payment.

| Are local estimates for finite exponential sums classical? | Nazarov proves local estimates for exponential polynomials; Friedland--Yomdin restate the measurable-set Turan--Nazarov inequality and extend its observation-set geometry. | Do not present finite-term local observation as a novelty claim. | These estimates do not display the frozen radial quotient, transport clock, or signed carrier current. |

## Claim-to-source ledger

1. **Local estimates for exponential polynomials are established literature.**
   F. L. Nazarov, “Local estimates for exponential polynomials and their
   applications to inequalities of the uncertainty principle type,”
   *Algebra i Analiz* 5:4 (1993), 3--66; English version,
   *St. Petersburg Mathematical Journal* 5:4 (1994), 663--717.
   [Math-Net record and full text](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/aa397).

2. **A bounded union of Fourier intervals admits a Logvinenko--Sereda type
   estimate whose constant does not depend on where the intervals are
   placed.**  Oleg Kovrijkine, “Some results related to the
   Logvinenko--Sereda theorem,” 2000 preprint, later published in
   *Proceedings of the AMS*.
   [arXiv:math/0012186](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0012186).

3. **There are scale-free torus observation results for Fourier support in a
   finite number of parallelepipeds.**  Michela Egidi and Ivan Veselic,
   “Scale-free unique continuation estimates and Logvinenko--Sereda theorems
   on the torus,” preprint 2016.
   [arXiv:1609.07020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07020).

4. **The measurable-set Turan--Nazarov inequality has geometric observation
   refinements.**  Omer Friedland and Yosef Yomdin, “An observation on the
   Turan--Nazarov inequality,” 2011.
   [arXiv:1107.0039](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.0039).

5. **Ingham--Nazarov and small-interval trigonometric inequalities have a
   modern survey.**  Philippe Jaming and Chadi Saba, “From Ingham to
   Nazarov's inequality: a survey on some trigonometric inequalities,” 2023.
   [arXiv:2311.17714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17714).

## Boundary and stop rule

Searches combined the terms `clustered frequencies`, `exponential
polynomial`, `Logvinenko--Sereda`, `Ingham`, `one-sided Fourier spectrum`,
`localized current`, `carrier envelope`, and `heat equation modulation`.
The first pass was broad; the second pass opened the original or author-hosted
records for the closest local-observation results.

The search stopped because the relevant source families converged on static
observation and uncertainty inequalities, while the unresolved claim here is
a specific signed, time-weighted, radial collar-flux payment.  More general
search variants returned the same source families or unrelated uses of
carrier-envelope language.  A future search should be triggered by a precise
candidate localized-current inequality, not by another broad terminology
scan.

No inspected source is represented as proving the R0.75Z identities.  No
novelty, priority, or completeness claim is made.  The full cluster payment,
arbitrary-field transfer, regularity, and singularity remain open.
