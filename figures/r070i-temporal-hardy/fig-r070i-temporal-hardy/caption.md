**Figure R0.70I — Temporal Hardy envelope and the initial-boundary scale
ledger.** **A,** The normalized finite geometric-chain envelope
$G_K(s)=\min\{r_K^{-1},s^{-1/2}\}$ follows the Hardy singularity down to
$s=r_K^2$, then saturates at the finest-scale cap. **B,** For
$f(s)=s^{-\alpha}$, the exact truncations of
$\int s^{-1/2}f(s)^2ds$ are bounded for $\alpha<1/4$, logarithmic at
$\alpha=1/4$, and power-growing for $\alpha>1/4$. **C,** With fixed
$a=1/8$ and unit profile constants, the initial-boundary NSE scale ledger
has $E=D=a^2r$, normalized target scale factor
$\mathcal T_n=a^4r^{-1}$, and $r^{-3}E^2=a^4r^{-1}$.  The last two curves
coincide because their reference-profile constants are normalized to one;
this does not identify the two functionals.  All curves are closed-form
analytic evaluations.  This is not a simulated NSE trajectory, not a
fixed-positive-top counterexample, and not simulation evidence or a
numerical PDE proof.
