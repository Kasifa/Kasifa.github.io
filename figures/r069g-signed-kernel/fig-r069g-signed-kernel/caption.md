**Figure R0.69G — Sign cancellation of the vorticity-direction kernel is not
robust to magnitude concentration.** **a,** For
\(\xi(x)=e_3\), \(\xi(x+z)=(\sin0.73,0,\cos0.73)\), the local geometric
kernel is \(D=-\sin(0.73)\widehat z_2\widehat z_3\).  It has four alternating
sign lobes and zero uniform angular mean; solid and dashed contours distinguish
positive and negative levels independently of color.  **b,** Weighting the same
direction pattern by \(g_\eta=1+\eta\operatorname{sgn}D\) produces the exact
normalized mean \(\eta\,2\sin(0.73)/(3\pi)\).  Open markers are the certified
spherical-quadrature nodes.  This demonstrates the mechanism behind
\(\sup_{g\ge0,\int g=1}|\int Kg|=\|K\|_\infty\): direction-only sign
oscillation cannot yield a uniform gain over nonnegative vorticity magnitudes.
The result does not exclude magnitude-direction coupled estimates and is not a
Navier–Stokes regularity theorem.
