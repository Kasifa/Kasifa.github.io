## Primary sources

### Weighted energy controls

Pedro Gabriel Fernández-Dalgo and Pierre Gilles Lemarié-Rieusset,
[*Weak solutions for Navier--Stokes equations with initial data in weighted
\(L^2\) spaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.11038), arXiv:1906.11038,
published in *Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis*, DOI
[10.1007/s00205-020-01510-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-020-01510-w).

The paper proves global weak existence for initial data in polynomially
weighted \(L^2\) spaces and develops the associated weighted energy
controls.  Its weights and whole-space setting differ from R0.74H.

Pedro Gabriel Fernández-Dalgo and Pierre Gilles Lemarié-Rieusset,
[*Weighted energy estimates for the incompressible Navier--Stokes equations
and applications to axisymmetric solutions without
swirl*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00868), arXiv:2010.00868, DOI
[10.1007/s00021-021-00603-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00021-021-00603-0).

This paper extends a weighted Leray procedure and obtains suitable weak
solutions, with an axisymmetric no-swirl application.  It does not use the
R0.74E moving frames or dyadic collar observable.

### Local-energy solution frameworks

Zachary Bradshaw and Tai-Peng Tsai,
[*Global existence, regularity, and uniqueness of infinite energy solutions
to the Navier--Stokes equations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.00256),
arXiv:1907.00256.

This source establishes local-energy results under truncated Morrey-type
conditions at small or large scales.  The theorem objective and observables
are different from R0.74H.

Zachary Bradshaw and Tai-Peng Tsai,
[*Local energy solutions to the Navier--Stokes equations in Wiener amalgam
spaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.09204), arXiv:2008.09204.

This source develops a priori bounds and existence results in
\(L^2\)-based Wiener-amalgam classes.  Its cube/amalgam aggregation provides
local-energy context but not the present periodic shell identity.
