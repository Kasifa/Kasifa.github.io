# R0.70X exact certificate

This directory locks the finite exact payload for the R0.70X cyclic-null
and rank-at-most-one signed obstruction.

The release studies the complete-frame defect

\[
 \mathcal D_\times
 =\omega\otimes\omega
 -\sum_\alpha T_\alpha\omega\otimes T_\alpha\omega
\]

and its signed Biot--Savart strain work

\[
 \mathfrak E_S
 =\int_{\mathbb T^3}S(\omega):\mathcal D_\times\,dx.
\]

It locks two results.

First, if \(n+p+q=0\) and the Fourier polarizations are divergence-free,
the three cyclic strain placements satisfy

\[
 |n|^2A_n+|p|^2A_p+|q|^2A_q=0.
\]

In a high--high--low triad this identity gives one factor of the shell ratio
\(t/R\). An explicit family shows that this power is sharp for the
orbitwise estimate.

Second, the certificate constructs a real finite Fourier field generated by
the actual complete radial frame for which

\[
 \operatorname{rank}Q\le1,
 \qquad
 G_Q=0,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak E_S<0.
\]

The fixed sample uses

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 k&=(1,-1,1),\\
 p&=(1,1,0),\\
 q&=(-1,0,1),\\
 r&=(0,1,1),
 \end{aligned}
\]

\[
 \psi=\cos(p\cdot x)+\cos(q\cdot x)+\cos(r\cdot x),
 \qquad
 w=k\times\nabla\psi,
\]

and

\[
 \omega=
 [\cos(k\cdot x)+\cos(6k\cdot x)+\sin(7k\cdot x)]w.
\]

The three squared radii are \(5,110,149\). Strict annular support makes
the low response orthogonal to both high responses. If

\[
 \kappa
 =1-\Gamma(\sqrt{110},\sqrt{149})\ge0,
\]

then

\[
 \mathcal D_\times
 =2(f_1f_6+f_1f_7+\kappa f_6f_7)w\otimes w
\]

and

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak E_S
 =-\frac{81(62+1639\kappa)}{32780}<0.}
\]

For an admissible nonnegative cutoff,
\(0\le\Gamma(\sqrt{110},\sqrt{149})\le1\), so the old response guard
holds and the sign remains strict. The example therefore disproves the
nonnegative-frame branch of the proposed estimate

\[
 |\mathfrak E_S|
 \lesssim
 \|\nabla\omega\|_2\|G_Q\|_{6/5}.
\]

The sample does not have a uniformly positive top covariance eigenvalue.
The certificate makes no claim about a theorem that uses such a gap in an
essential way.

## Direct machine checks

The producer performs six groups of exact checks.

### 1. Cyclic triad identity

It parameterizes a generic divergence-free triad symbolically and checks

\[
 |n|^2A_n+|p|^2A_p+|q|^2A_q=0.
\]

It also checks the beta-difference representation

\[
 \beta_nB_n+\beta_pB_p+\beta_qB_q
 =(\beta_n-\beta_q)B_n+(\beta_p-\beta_q)B_p.
\]

### 2. Sharp high--high--low family

For

\[
 n=(1,0,0),\quad
 p=(M,M,0),\quad
 q=(-M-1,-M,0),
\]

and the explicit transverse polarizations, it obtains

\[
 (A_n,A_p,A_q)
 =\frac1{\sqrt{2M^2+2M+1}}(-M,-M-1,M)
\]

and

\[
 \mathcal G_M
 =-\frac{1+M\kappa_M}{\sqrt{2M^2+2M+1}}.
\]

Since \(\kappa_M\ge0\), this is at least a constant times \(M^{-1}\) in
absolute value.

### 3. Planar eigenfield geometry

The producer differentiates the three-wave field and checks

\[
 \nabla\cdot w=0,
 \quad k\cdot w=0,
 \quad -\Delta w=2w,
 \quad \langle\psi^3\rangle=\frac32.
\]

It verifies two exact representations of the strain factor \(A(x)\) and

\[
 \langle A\rangle=\frac{81}{2}.
\]

### 4. Complete-frame covariance

The frame covariance is reduced exactly to its three response inner
products. Every \(2\times2\) minor of the resulting tensor vanishes, and
the scalar defect coefficient is

\[
 2(f_1f_6+f_1f_7+\kappa f_6f_7).
\]

The producer locks the factor-four squared slacks

\[
 110-16\cdot5=30,
 \qquad
 149-16\cdot5=69.
\]

### 5. Physical-space signed work

The producer computes the exact axial trigonometric mean and combines it
with \(\langle A\rangle\). It obtains

\[
 \mathfrak E_S
 =-\frac{81abc(62+1639\kappa)}{32780}
\]

for \(M=6,N=7\), and checks the strict sign for \(abc>0\).

### 6. Independent Fourier reconstruction

The producer expands the fixed field into all thirty-six vorticity modes,
checks divergence and shell radii, constructs the Biot--Savart strain, forms
all ordered defect pairs, and evaluates the full Parseval sum. The result is
identical to the physical-space formula with zero symbolic residual.

## Boundary

The certificate does not prove a global vector-valued trilinear summation,
a critical replacement for \(G_Q\), a uniformly positive top gap, control
of the principal covariance stretching term, an enstrophy closure, a
continuation theorem, a singularity, unconditional global regularity, or a
solution of the Millennium problem.
