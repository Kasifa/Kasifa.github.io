# Figure contract

## Analytical question

Does a fixed matched spatial partition turn the R0.71J zero-entry positive
creation into a quantity paid by the same bounded-overlap local heat endpoint?

## Takeaway

No for one fixed aligned scale-covariant partition.  The finite selected-cell
creation is \(\gtrsim K^{-2}\), while the same local heat/support payment is
\(O((\nu K^4)^{-1})\).  The viscous collar is also \(K^{-2}\) and remains an
open possible payment rather than a lower-order error.
