**Figure R0.69J | Harmonic Taylor subtraction improves the remainder but
does not remove the leading quadrupole.** (a) The exact signed source
\(\delta_{Re_1}+\delta_{-Re_1}-\delta_{Re_2}-\delta_{-Re_2}\) has zero mass
and zero dipole, while its center pressure Hessian is
\(Q_R=3(2\pi R^3)^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)\). Pairing it with the
trace-free strain jet \(\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)\) gives
\(3/(\pi R^3)\ne0\). (b) For \(r\le R/2\), subtracting \(Q_R\) gives a
remainder envelope proportional to \(r/R\); the normalized leading
coefficient stays at order one. The witness concerns a scalar pressure source,
not yet one generated by an incompressible velocity, and gives no regularity
or singularity conclusion.
