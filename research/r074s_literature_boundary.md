# R0.74S literature and priority boundary

R0.74S is an internal algebraic route audit built on the already cited local-energy, suitable-weak, shell-clock, and cutoff literature of R0.74H/P/R. Its four-channel recombination and three-channel genealogy decomposition are proved in frozen project notation; they are not presented as new general Navier--Stokes theorems.

The proved no-go is deliberately narrow: scalar completed-clock positivity, cutoff linearity, the displayed tower identities, and unweighted component/epoch/merger counts do not imply the desired matched l2 compression. The witness is not a velocity field, pressure, dissipation measure, work density, spatial cutoff operator, or Navier--Stokes solution.

No novelty or priority claim is made. The absence of a PDE realization means the result must not be advertised as a PDE/NSE counterexample.
