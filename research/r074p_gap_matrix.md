## Route consequence

The positive-order window route is eliminated on the exact family, and the
energy-oscillation route merely repackages the endpoint.  The only surviving
candidate from this section is the defect-completed matched shell clock.
The next mathematical task is therefore not to invent another scalar
average, but to seek an effective-shell or packing theorem strong enough to
convert the proved \(\ell^1\) collar ledger into the matched \(\ell^2\)
observable without paying \(\Gamma^{-1/2}\).
