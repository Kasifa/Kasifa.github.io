**Figure R0.72G-1 | Complete-root packing in the exact one-carrier
lattice.** **A,** Complete target-root slope mass from the real-lattice RK4
producer (filled circles) and the independent Fourier Strang solver (open
squares), together with the producer mass of the first \(R\) selected roots
(triangles).  The neutral \(4/\pi^2\) line is a finite diagnostic guide, not
an analytic leading-constant claim.  The R0.72G theorem proves only the
two-sided order \(G_{\rm all}(\delta_R;X)=\Theta(\log\delta_R)\).
**B,** Number of resolved sign-changing roots in the finite window
\(0<\tau<12\sqrt\delta\).  The count grows close to the neutral
\((24/\pi)\sqrt\delta\) guide even though the slope-weighted mass remains
logarithmic.  **C,** Producer mass in dyadic scaled-time packets for
\(R=64\); late packets contain many roots but negligible squared slope.
Both solvers use IEEE-754 binary64 arithmetic and finite spectral windows.
The figure is restricted to the fixed-\(q_0=4\), real one-carrier exact
triangular family.  It does not prove a multi-carrier or arbitrary
Navier--Stokes trace estimate, a continuation criterion, singularity
formation, or regularity.  Source: R0.72G certificate package and analytic
report.
