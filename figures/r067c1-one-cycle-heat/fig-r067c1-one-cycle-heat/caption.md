# Figure R0.67C-1

The complete sixth-order heat coefficient is strictly positive at the first
stationary four-bit cycle. (a) Direct enumeration partitions the 34,690 valid
solutions of \(A+B+C-D-E=Q\) by the first positive-carrier offset \(a\); the
signed zero-time contributions sum to 500. Including all ten \((3,2)\)-shuffle
time orders gives 346,900 signed paths. (b) Exact complete-homogeneous Taylor
coefficients through degree 32 converge to the outward interval
\(0.0516697551544<S_{6,q}^{(M=16)}<0.0516697551584\). (c) The absolute
omitted-series bound is below \(1.944\times10^{-12}\), more than ten orders of
magnitude smaller than the strict coefficient lower bound. This figure is an
exact finite-scale calibration; it does not certify the dominant asymptotic
heat projection.
