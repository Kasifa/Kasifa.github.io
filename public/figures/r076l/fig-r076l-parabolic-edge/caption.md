**Figure R0.76L-1. Finite diagnostic for the forward Chebyshev edge
saddle.**  The positive Gaussian integral in (L.16) is evaluated at scaled
time `s=4` for four edge scales `A` and four degree laws
`m approximately A^p` (nearest even integer).  The panels show the saddle
location, common logarithmic amplitude, and unit edge-coordinate tilt,
normalized by their predicted powers of
`mu=(m^2/A)^(1/3)`.  Horizontal lines are the analytic limits
`2^(5/3)`, `3*2^(-2/3)`, and `2^(-4/3)`.  Most displayed sequences move
toward the predicted constants.  The `p=0.75` finite-tilt sequence moves
slightly away from its limit over the displayed range and has not yet
entered its eventual approach; this is compatible with the theorem, whose
limit also requires `mu -> infinity`.  Panel (c) is the finite unit-coordinate
log tilt `[log I_(m,A)(4,1)-log I_(m,A)(4,0)]/mu`, not a numerical derivative;
all logarithms are natural and all plotted quantities are dimensionless.
The three panels use separately enlarged vertical ranges.  Values are
deterministic binary64 quadratures; they audit scaling, sign, and constants
but do not prove the Laplace limit, exact integer-shear transfer,
signed-flux estimate, or any Navier--Stokes regularity claim.
