# R0.72C-1 caption

**Physical phases and the carrier-prefactor boundary.** **A**, exact
equal-modulus formulas for the coherent family and the odd-generation
Rudin--Shapiro sign family. The former has normalized geometric prefactor
\(M^{-10/3}\); the latter has the sharp phase-uniform order \(M^{-8/3}\).
**B**, strict sufficient coupling boundaries when
\(\eta=M^\alpha\) and \(L=M^{-\beta}\): coherent exact launch,
arbitrary-phase exact launch, and a fixed-positive-time tail. The last curve
does not pay roots accumulated before burn-in. All plotted lines are analytic
formulas reconstructed by two independent deterministic certificate paths.
The certificates are not interval arithmetic. Prefactor sharpness is not
actual root-ledger saturation, and the figure makes no general
Navier--Stokes regularity or blow-up claim.
