**Figure R0.69Q | Vorticity-direction diffusion does not create an extra
unconditional absorption mechanism.** (a) The exact polar identity decomposes
the full enstrophy dissipation into orthogonal radial and angular parts; their
sum is fixed, so the angular term cannot be counted twice. (b) On the compactly
supported affine core, positive vortex stretching attains
`sqrt(2/3) s w^2`, whereas radial, angular, and full interior dissipation all
vanish on the same open ball. The normalized bars display this exact endpoint,
not simulation data. (c) Under `v_{a,L}(x)=a L v(x/L)`, the ratio of positive
stretching to viscous vorticity dissipation gains the exact factor
`a L^2/nu`; the three curves show this closed-form scaling. The figure excludes
only interior-only, scale-independent absorption. It does not exclude cutoff
flux, initial-trace, or nonlocal magnitude--direction estimates and does not
establish Navier--Stokes regularity or blow-up.
