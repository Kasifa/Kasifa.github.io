# Figure R0.59-1 — Growing multi-output critical saturation

**English.** A single real divergence-free high-frequency packet with tensor
Rudin--Shapiro signs sustains a growing coherent output set in the complete
first Navier--Stokes Picard iterate.  (a) For \(H=4LM\), all exact target
coefficients \(d_m(t_H)\), \(1\leq m\leq M\), are positive and their scaled
profiles \(Hd_m\) remain inside the certified all-index envelope.  (b) The
explicit heat-Besov witness for the sum of all target modes is proportional to
\(H^{-1}\), uniformly over the two dyadic parameters; the horizontal lines
show the rigorous heat-Besov and periodic \(BMO^{-1}\) output lower bounds.
(c) Tensor Rudin--Shapiro phases keep the spatial maximum at
\(O(\sqrt{LM})\), while all-positive phases grow as \(LM\); every matched
nonlinear product nevertheless equals \(c_{r,n}^2=1\).  Curves are
deterministic floating-point evaluations of exact formulas for presentation.
The theorem is proved symbolically and audited with exact integer arithmetic.
It is a first-iterate saturation result, not norm inflation, nonlinear
remainder control, or a Navier--Stokes regularity proof.

**中文。** 一个采用张量 Rudin--Shapiro 符号的实值无散高频包，可以在完整的
Navier--Stokes 第一 Picard 迭代中同时维持一个随尺度增长的相干低频集合。
(a) 当 \(H=4LM\) 时，全部 \(M\) 个精确目标系数均为正，且无量纲轮廓落在
全指标认证包络内；(b) 所有目标模之和的显式热 Besov 见证量统一按
\(H^{-1}\) 缩放，水平线为严格的热 Besov 与周期 \(BMO^{-1}\) 输出下界；
(c) 张量 Rudin--Shapiro 相位把输入空间峰值压到 \(O(\sqrt{LM})\)，而全正
相位为 \(LM\)，但所有匹配非线性乘积仍为 \(c_{r,n}^2=1\)。曲线仅用于
展示精确公式；定理由符号推导和整数审计支持。这不是范数膨胀、高阶余项控制
或正则性证明。
