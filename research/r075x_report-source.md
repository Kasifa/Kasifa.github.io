## 1. Primary sources

### Nazarov's original result

F. L. Nazarov, *Local estimates for exponential polynomials and their
applications to inequalities of the uncertainty principle type*, Algebra i
Analiz 5:4 (1993), 3--66; English translation, St. Petersburg Mathematical
Journal 5:4 (1994), 663--717.

Bibliographic record and linked full-text PDF:
<https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/aa397>.

The landing record verifies the author, title, journal, year, volume, issue,
pages, and the linked original full text.  The precise inequality used below
is cross-checked in the accessible primary restatement rather than attributed
to text displayed on the MathNet landing page.

### Accessible primary restatement

Omer Friedland and Yosef Yomdin, *An observation on the Turan--Nazarov
inequality*, Studia Mathematica 218 (2013), 27--41; submitted 2011 and
revised 2013.

Author manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.0039>.

The abstract explicitly records that the frequencies, meaning the imaginary
parts of the exponents, do not enter the original Turan--Nazarov inequality.
The paper's Theorem 1.1 displays the measurable-set result for complex
coefficients and exponents.  Its factor uses the interval length, subset
measure, number of terms, and the largest absolute real part, with no
imaginary-frequency or exponent-gap factor.  R0.75X uses this original
positive-measure form, not the paper's later metric-span extension.
