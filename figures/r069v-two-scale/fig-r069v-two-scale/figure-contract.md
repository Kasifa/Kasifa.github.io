# Figure contract — R0.69V two-scale annular obstruction

- Analytical question: can a genuinely shape-changing two-scale compact
  affine family improve the full-space annular ratio, and can any amplitude
  make all annuli one sign at scale separation four?
- Supported takeaway: exact analysis proves uniform return to the one-profile
  ratio under large scale separation.  At separation four, the amplitude scan
  approaches but does not reach one; an independent estimator identifies a
  robust negative coarse annulus.
- Family and variants: one continuous amplitude curve; two common-sample cubic
  means with pointwise scramble bands; signed unordered-zone-pair bars.
- Data sufficiency: 16 independent scrambles for each estimator, 167,772,160
  stratified point pairs in the main scan, 83,886,080 in the fixed-candidate
  cross-check, and 41,943,040 in the targeted cubic root audit.
- Renderer and footprint: reproducible Matplotlib, 178 mm by 86 mm; PDF, SVG,
  and 600 dpi PNG.
- Palette: blue and rust with gold candidate marker; marker shape, signed bar
  direction, line identity, zero references, and interval whiskers duplicate
  color encoding.
- Boundary: uniform asymptotic decoupling is exact.  The finite-separation
  ratio, root gap, and scramble bands are randomized diagnostics, not rigorous
  interval enclosures.  Nothing here proves global regularity, finite-time
  singularity, or the Millennium Problem.
