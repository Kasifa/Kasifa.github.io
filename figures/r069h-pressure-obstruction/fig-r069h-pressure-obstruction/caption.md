**Figure R0.69H | Identical local velocity gradients do not determine the
pressure-Hessian sign.** (a) The exact principal pressure components for the
two smooth periodic divergence-free families are
\(H_{11}^{-}(0)=-1-(54/85)t^2\) and
\(H_{11}^{+}(0)=-1+(54/85)t^2\). Both start from the same local strain
\(S(0)=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)\) and vorticity \(\omega(0)=0\), but the
plus family changes sign at \(t^2=85/54\). Open markers show certificate
nodes. (b) At \(t=2\), the common base contribution is \(-1\), the exact
cross term is zero, and the remote perturbations contribute
\(\mp216/85\), giving \(-301/85\) and \(131/85\). The figure establishes a
pointwise-sign obstruction only; it neither proves Navier--Stokes regularity
nor constructs a singularity.
