two frequency powers.** (A) For the unsplit parent with
\(\alpha=\beta=1\) and \(\sigma=+1\), stretching and the transport--filter
commutator are separately signed and only their sum is the projected-Lamb

\(q_{\rm lo}/(a^4K^6)=e^{-2\tau}\). Its bottom value is one and its exact

bulk to the bottom costs
\(A_{\rm bottom}/\mathcal V_{\rm bulk}=2K^2\), while division by \(K^2\)
leaves the constant two. (D) Every finite-box mass
\(\mathcal V_\theta=(1-e^{-2\theta})/16\) is independent of \(K\) and tends
to the infinite-height value \(1/16\). Values are closed formulas, not DNS,
time stepping, or fitted data. The figure does not prove bottom-trace
integrability or divergence for general solutions, exclude NSE-specific
depletion, establish singularity or global regularity, or solve the
Millennium problem.
