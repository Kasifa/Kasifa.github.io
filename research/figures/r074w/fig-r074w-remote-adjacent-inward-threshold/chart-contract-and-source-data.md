# Chart contract and source-data ledger

## Question

What exact scale comparison decides whether a common-shear packet remains on
the fixed remote adjacent-inward strip, and what endpoint conclusion survives
after inversion, cross-packet, periodic, amplitude, and shell-weight effects?

## Claim boundary

The figure may show only the following source-supported statements.

1. The explicit strip \(\mathcal S_m\) lies in
   \(A_{k_m-1}(R)\) and has shell cutoff equal to one.
2. Under the central conditional bridge,
   \(-L^{-2}\log\mathfrak S_t\to q(\ell)=p^2/(4\ell)\) in probability
   at logarithmic scale; this is not a pathwise identity.
3. Uniform survival is proved for rates strictly below \(q_{65}\), and
   uniform sweeping is proved for rates strictly above \(q_{64}\).  The band
   between them is not classified by those uniform slab tests; at fixed
   limiting \(\ell\), strict comparison with \(q(\ell)\) resolves the side,
   while equality remains open.
4. Original-scale packet 1 is swept and packet 2 survives on their respective
   fixed strips.
5. Periodic windings are retained in the exact sum and then bounded; inversion
   and the other packet are controlled only after the physical amplitudes are
   inserted.
6. Packet 2 yields the weighted endpoint divergence in the adjacent inward
   shell, disproving the matching all-shell \(O(T_*)\) upper bound for the
   frozen placement.

The figure must not claim a resolved critical transition, a whole-shell
occupation estimate, a fixed-deletion obstruction, a result for arbitrary
solutions, a DNS computation, or a Clay Millennium result.

## Data semantics

`source-data.csv` is a deterministic long-form ledger generated by `plot.py`.
Its columns are:

- `panel`: panel identifier;
- `series`: semantic series name;
- `x`, `y`: plotted coordinates where applicable;
- `x_unit`, `y_unit`: unit declarations;
- `exact_value`: exact rational or symbolic source value;
- `role`: geometry, threshold, schematic node, or endpoint-scale role;
- `source_locator`: equation/section locator in the bound research note;
- `note`: non-graphical qualification.

Panel B values are exact evaluations of \(q(\ell)\) using rational decimal
inputs from 64 through 65.  Panel D values are exact-formula floating
evaluations of
\([\chi(65)L^2-\tfrac12\log L]/(1000\log 10)\).  Panel A and C rows are
schematic coordinates whose scientific semantics are supplied by their exact
labels and locators; they are not measured observations.
