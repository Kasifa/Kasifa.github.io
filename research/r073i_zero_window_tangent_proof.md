# R0.73I source proof: zero-window tangent action on the complete top block

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Parent inputs:** R0.73E uniform frozen semigroup and R0.73F moving
dichotomy  
**Evidence class:** exact consequence of the stated R0.73E--F operator
theorems; source stage  
**Public status:** not released and not an independent recertification of the
parent inputs

## 1. Result and exact order of limits

Let

\[
 H=L^2(\mathbb T_{2\pi}),
 \qquad
 L=-\partial_x^2+\frac14,
 \qquad
 B_\varepsilon(d)=\widetilde A(d)-\varepsilon L,
 \tag{1.1}
\]

with common domain \(D(B_\varepsilon(d))=H^2_{\rm per}\) for
\(\varepsilon>0\).  Let \(U_\varepsilon(t,s)\) be the exact fast-time
evolution generated by

\[
 \partial_t u=B_\varepsilon(\varepsilon t)u.
 \tag{1.2}
\]

Write

\[
 a=\max\{\operatorname{Re}z:z\in\sigma(\widetilde A(0))\}.
 \tag{1.3}
\]

For all sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\), let \(P_\varepsilon\) be the
R0.73E Riesz projection onto the **complete** viscous continuation of the
inviscid top cluster.  Its positive finite rank is independent of
\(\varepsilon\).  For \(D>0\), define the minimum and maximum gains on this
fixed initial top block by

\[
 m_\varepsilon(D)
 =\inf_{\substack{v\in P_\varepsilon H\\\|v\|_2=1}}
   \|U_\varepsilon(D/\varepsilon,0)v\|_2,
 \tag{1.4}
\]

and

\[
 M_\varepsilon(D)
 =\sup_{\substack{v\in P_\varepsilon H\\\|v\|_2=1}}
   \|U_\varepsilon(D/\varepsilon,0)v\|_2.
 \tag{1.5}
\]

The R0.73F estimate below shows that \(m_\varepsilon(D)>0\) for every
sufficiently small \(\varepsilon\) once \(D\) is in the corresponding local
window.  Thus all logarithms used in the \(D\downarrow0\) statement are
finite on the relevant tails.

For each fixed \(D>0\), put

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \underline g_m(D)&=\liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
   \frac{\varepsilon}{D}\log m_\varepsilon(D),
 &\quad
 \overline g_m(D)&=\limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
   \frac{\varepsilon}{D}\log m_\varepsilon(D),\\
 \underline g_M(D)&=\liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
   \frac{\varepsilon}{D}\log M_\varepsilon(D),
 &
 \overline g_M(D)&=\limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
   \frac{\varepsilon}{D}\log M_\varepsilon(D).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{1.6}
\]

The theorem proved below is

\[
 \boxed{
 \lim_{D\downarrow0}\underline g_m(D)
 =\lim_{D\downarrow0}\overline g_m(D)
 =\lim_{D\downarrow0}\underline g_M(D)
 =\lim_{D\downarrow0}\overline g_M(D)
 =a.}
 \tag{1.7}
\]

Thus both the smallest and largest top-block gains have tangent exponential
rate \(a\) at the zero window.  The order is essential: first
\(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) at a fixed \(D>0\), and only then
\(D\downarrow0\).  Equation (1.7) neither asserts that the inner limit exists
at any fixed positive \(D\), nor gives a joint two-parameter limit along an
arbitrary path \(D=D(\varepsilon)\).

## 2. Inputs used from R0.73E

Two R0.73E conclusions are used.

First, for every upper spectral margin \(\rho>0\), there are constants

\[
 C_\rho\ge1,
 \qquad
 \varepsilon_\rho>0,
 \tag{2.1}
\]

such that

\[
 \|e^{tB_\varepsilon(0)}\|_{H\to H}
 \le C_\rho e^{(a+\rho)t},
 \qquad
 t\ge0,\quad 0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_\rho.
 \tag{2.2}
\]

Second, the exact heat profile satisfies the bounded kinetic-space drift
estimate

\[
 \|\widetilde A(d)-\widetilde A(0)\|_{H\to H}
 \le C_A d,
 \qquad
 C_A=\frac{49}{4}.
 \tag{2.3}
\]

Consequently, in fast time,

\[
 E_\varepsilon(t)
 :=\widetilde A(\varepsilon t)-\widetilde A(0)
 \quad\hbox{satisfies}\quad
 \|E_\varepsilon(t)\|\le C_A\varepsilon t.
 \tag{2.4}
\]

The unbounded term \(-\varepsilon L\) remains in the fixed generator
\(B_\varepsilon(0)\).  It is not used as a bounded perturbation in this
argument.

## 3. Uniform Volterra upper bound

Fix \(\rho>0\), \(0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_\rho\), and \(u_0\in H\).
The bounded nonautonomous perturbation theorem gives the mild identity

\[
 U_\varepsilon(t,0)u_0
 =e^{tB_\varepsilon(0)}u_0
 +\int_0^t e^{(t-s)B_\varepsilon(0)}
   E_\varepsilon(s)U_\varepsilon(s,0)u_0\,ds.
 \tag{3.1}
\]

Equations (2.2)--(2.4) imply

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 e^{-(a+\rho)t}\|U_\varepsilon(t,0)u_0\|_2
 \le{}& C_\rho\|u_0\|_2\\
 &+C_\rho C_A\varepsilon
 \int_0^t s\,e^{-(a+\rho)s}
 \|U_\varepsilon(s,0)u_0\|_2\,ds.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{3.2}
\]

Scalar Gronwall therefore gives

\[
 \|U_\varepsilon(t,0)\|_{H\to H}
 \le C_\rho
 \exp\left((a+\rho)t
 +\frac12C_\rho C_A\varepsilon t^2\right).
 \tag{3.3}
\]

At \(t=D/\varepsilon\),

\[
 M_\varepsilon(D)
 \le C_\rho
 \exp\left\{
 \frac{(a+\rho)D+\frac12C_\rho C_A D^2}{\varepsilon}
 \right\}.
 \tag{3.4}
\]

For every fixed \(D>0\), the prefactor disappears after multiplication by
\(\varepsilon/D\) and passage to \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\).  Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 \overline g_M(D)
 \le a+\rho+\frac12C_\rho C_A D.}
 \tag{3.5}
\]

Because \(m_\varepsilon(D)\le M_\varepsilon(D)\), the same right-hand side
also bounds \(\overline g_m(D)\).

## 4. Dependence of the R0.73F lower window on the target margin

Fix a lower accuracy \(\zeta>0\).  Let \(\beta<a\) be the spectral abscissa
after removing the complete inviscid top cluster, as in R0.73F.  Choose,
in this order,

\[
 \max\{\beta,0.17035,a-\zeta\}<\alpha_\zeta<a,
 \tag{4.1}
\]

The interval in (4.1) is nonempty because the parent input gives
\(\beta<a\) and \(0.17035<a\).

then

\[
 \max\{\beta,0\}<b_\zeta<\alpha_\zeta<c_\zeta<a.
 \tag{4.2}
\]

R0.73E supplies the corresponding frozen dichotomy constants
\(C_{b_\zeta}\) and \(C_{c_\zeta}\).  Set

\[
 \nu_\zeta
 =\min\{\alpha_\zeta-b_\zeta,
          c_\zeta-\alpha_\zeta\}>0,
 \qquad
 K_\zeta=\max\{1,C_{b_\zeta},C_{c_\zeta}\}.
 \tag{4.3}
\]

The roughness lemma fixes

\[
 \eta_\zeta=\frac{\nu_\zeta}{2},
 \qquad
 r_\zeta=\alpha_\zeta+\eta_\zeta.
 \tag{4.4}
\]

The inequalities above imply

\[
 a-\zeta<\alpha_\zeta<r_\zeta<a.
 \tag{4.5}
\]

Indeed, the last strict inequality follows from
\(\eta_\zeta\le(c_\zeta-\alpha_\zeta)/2\) and \(c_\zeta<a\).

Now choose an R0.73F slow window \(d_\zeta>0\) satisfying, strictly,

\[
 C_A d_\zeta<\frac{\nu_\zeta}{16K_\zeta^2},
 \qquad
 r_\zeta d_\zeta<\frac5{16}.
 \tag{4.6}
\]

For example, any positive number below

\[
 \min\left\{
 \frac{\nu_\zeta}{16K_\zeta^2C_A},
 \frac5{16r_\zeta}
 \right\}
 \tag{4.7}
\]

is admissible, subject to any further harmless shrinking already made in
the parent proof.  This records the full dependence

\[
 \zeta
 \longmapsto
 (\alpha_\zeta,b_\zeta,c_\zeta)
 \longmapsto
 (\nu_\zeta,K_\zeta,r_\zeta)
 \longmapsto d_\zeta.
 \tag{4.8}
\]

There is no claim that \(d_\zeta\) has a positive lower bound as
\(\zeta\downarrow0\).  Shrinking of this window is precisely why the final
statement is a zero-window theorem.

## 5. R0.73F lower bound for every vector in the top block

For the choices in Section 4, R0.73F supplies constants

\[
 K_{1,\zeta}<\infty,
 \qquad
 \varepsilon_{F,\zeta}>0,
 \tag{5.1}
\]

such that, for every

\[
 0<D\le d_\zeta,
 \qquad
 0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_{F,\zeta},
 \qquad
 v\in P_\varepsilon H,\quad \|v\|_2=1,
 \tag{5.2}
\]

the moving unstable fiber starts exactly at \(P_\varepsilon H\), and its
backward estimate gives

\[
 \|U_\varepsilon(D/\varepsilon,0)v\|_2
 \ge K_{1,\zeta}^{-1}
 e^{r_\zeta D/\varepsilon}.
 \tag{5.3}
\]

The universal quantifier over \(v\) in (5.2) is essential: (5.3) is a
minimum-gain statement on the complete top block, not merely an
operator-norm lower bound obtained from one favorable vector.  Taking the
infimum gives

\[
 m_\varepsilon(D)
 \ge K_{1,\zeta}^{-1}e^{r_\zeta D/\varepsilon}.
 \tag{5.4}
\]

For fixed \(D>0\), the prefactor again disappears after multiplication by
\(\varepsilon/D\).  Thus, whenever \(0<D\le d_\zeta\),

\[
 \boxed{
 \underline g_m(D)\ge r_\zeta>a-\zeta.}
 \tag{5.5}
\]

Since \(M_\varepsilon(D)\ge m_\varepsilon(D)\), the same lower bound holds
for \(\underline g_M(D)\).

## 6. Squeezing all four iterated rates

For every fixed \(D>0\), the elementary ordering

\[
 m_\varepsilon(D)\le M_\varepsilon(D)
 \tag{6.1}
\]

and the definitions of liminf and limsup give

\[
 \underline g_m(D)\le\overline g_m(D),
 \qquad
 \underline g_M(D)\le\overline g_M(D),
 \tag{6.2}
\]

with the lower estimate (5.5) valid for both \(m\) and \(M\), and the upper
estimate (3.5) valid for both.

Fix \(\zeta>0\).  Equation (5.5) holds for every
\(0<D\le d_\zeta\); hence

\[
 \liminf_{D\downarrow0}\underline g_m(D)
 \ge a-\zeta,
 \qquad
 \liminf_{D\downarrow0}\underline g_M(D)
 \ge a-\zeta.
 \tag{6.3}
\]

Letting \(\zeta\downarrow0\) proves a lower bound \(a\) for both.

Independently, fix \(\rho>0\).  Equation (3.5) gives

\[
 \limsup_{D\downarrow0}\overline g_m(D)
 \le a+\rho,
 \qquad
 \limsup_{D\downarrow0}\overline g_M(D)
 \le a+\rho.
 \tag{6.4}
\]

Letting \(\rho\downarrow0\) proves the common upper bound \(a\).  Combining
(6.2)--(6.4) gives the explicit squeezes

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 a
 &\le\liminf_{D\downarrow0}\underline g_m(D)
 \le\limsup_{D\downarrow0}\overline g_m(D)
 \le\limsup_{D\downarrow0}\overline g_M(D)
 \le a,\\
 a
 &\le\liminf_{D\downarrow0}\underline g_M(D)
 \le\limsup_{D\downarrow0}\overline g_M(D)
 \le a.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{6.5}
\]

Since \(\underline g_m\le\overline g_m\) and
\(\underline g_M\le\overline g_M\), equation (6.5) proves all four
equalities in (1.7).

The quantifier order can be summarized as follows:

1. Given the desired lower accuracy \(\zeta>0\), choose the F spectral
   splitting and only then obtain the dependent window \(d_\zeta>0\).
2. Fix any \(D\in(0,d_\zeta]\).
3. Send \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\); this removes the dichotomy prefactor.
4. Send \(D\downarrow0\).
5. Finally send the arbitrary margins \(\zeta,\rho\downarrow0\).

Interchanging steps 2--3 is not licensed: if \(D=D(\varepsilon)\), the
terms \((\varepsilon/D)\log K_{1,\zeta}\) and
\((\varepsilon/D)\log C_\rho\) need not vanish.

## 7. Exact boundary of the theorem

This note proves a matching **tangent** rate for the complete top block.  It
does not prove any of the following:

- existence of
  \(\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log m_\varepsilon(D)\) or
  \(\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log M_\varepsilon(D)\) at a
  fixed \(D>0\);
- equality of the minimum and maximum fixed-window actions;
- a rank-one, simple, or unique rightmost eigenvalue branch;
- a canonical selected launch or a canonical positive endpoint;
- a bounded, polynomial, or otherwise identified prefactor after removal of
  the exponential action;
- prescribed-seed nonlinear departure, fixed-background instability,
  transverse three-dimensional closure, or any conclusion about the Clay
  problem.

The proof uses only the frozen semigroup bound and bounded profile drift from
R0.73E and the every-vector top-block lower law from R0.73F.  It does not use
finite Fourier data, instantaneous-eigenvalue integration, or an adiabatic
theorem.
