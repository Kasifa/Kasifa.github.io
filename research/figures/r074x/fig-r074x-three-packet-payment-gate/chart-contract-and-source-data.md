# Chart contract and source-data ledger

## Analytical question

Does adding a third common-shear packet defeat one fixed deletion, and if it
does at the \(T_*\) scale, why does the same construction still fail to close
the actual payment-normalized gate?

## Supported statements

1. The three-packet common-shear field is an exact smooth periodic unforced
   Navier--Stokes solution in the frozen family.
2. Packets 2 and 3 survive their remote adjacent-inward strips after all
   amplitude-weighted cross, inversion, and periodic-copy estimates.
3. Their two distinct endpoint coordinates diverge relative to the common
   \(T_*\), possibly at different times.
4. The deletion set in \(\inf_S\sup_t\) is fixed before time is selected, so
   the different-time pigeonhole is valid; simultaneous peaks are unnecessary.
5. The forced exterior cubic-payment rate exceeds the largest audited strip
   rate by the exact positive fraction shown in Panel C.
6. The two strip contributions are \(o((P_R^M)^{2/3})\). This statement is
   limited to those strip integrals and supplies no whole-shell upper bound.

The figure must not state that the actual payment-normalized fixed-deletion
gate has been disproved, that equality of witness times is necessary, that the
strip upper estimate controls a whole shell, or that bounded literature search
proves novelty.

## Source-data semantics

`source-data.csv` is deterministically generated by `plot.py` with columns:

- `panel`, `series`: panel and semantic series identifiers;
- `x`, `y`: plotted schematic or quantitative coordinates;
- `x_unit`, `y_unit`: declared units;
- `exact_value`: source rational or symbolic statement;
- `role`: packet, clock coordinate, quantifier branch, rate, or claim layer;
- `source_locator`: exact X-equation/section locator;
- `note`: qualification required to interpret the row.

Panel A and B coordinates are diagram layout values, not observations. Panel
C bars are exact rational rates displayed in \(10^{-3}L_1^2\)-exponent units.
Panel D rows encode the proved/open/no-go/next logical hierarchy and are not a
numerical ranking.
