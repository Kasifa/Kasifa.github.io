**Figure R0.70N — Exact failure of a universal scalar multi-scale vorticity
frame.** **A**, after trace normalization, exact periodic shear has eigenvalues
\((0,0,1)\), a one-axis helical wave has \((0,1/2,1/2)\), and a balanced
two-axis helical control has \((1/4,1/4,1/2)\); the first two therefore have
zero lower-frame constant, while the control has \(c_*=1/4\). **B**, for two
helical axes with energy fraction \(r\) and angle \(\theta\), the exact
constant is
\([1-\sqrt{1-4r(1-r)\sin^2\theta}]/4\): both nonparallel axes and balanced
observed energy are required. **C**, the genuinely three-dimensional
whole-space Schwartz family has
\(Q_L=\pi^{3/2}\operatorname{diag}(1/(4L),1/(4L),2L)\), so every finite
\(L\ge1\) is full rank but \(c_*=1/(8L^2+2)\to0\). **D**, adding repeated
positive scalar scales or time samples leaves the common-null shear and
one-axis constants at zero; the two-axis control is positive because it adds
directions, not because it adds observations. Values are closed exact formulas
converted to floating point only for plotting. No DNS, regularity theorem,
blow-up result, or Millennium claim is made.
