## High-risk 2D3C inference

The arbitrary-amplitude statement is not attributed to a theorem that the
source does not contain.  It follows directly from the verified split.  If
\(v=(v_1,v_2,0)\), \(\partial_3v=\partial_3\theta=0\), and

\[
 \partial_t\theta+v\cdot\nabla\theta=\nu\Delta\theta,
\]

then multiplying this linear homogeneous equation by any constant \(A\)
shows that \(u_A=v+A\theta e_3\) satisfies the vertical NSE component.  The
horizontal equation and pressure are unchanged because \(u_{A,3}\partial_3\)
vanishes.  Smooth periodic 2D data and a smooth passive scalar therefore give
a smooth periodic 3D NSE solution for every finite \(A\).

This derivation validates only the admissible family.  Biferale--Buzzicotti--
Linkmann do not define the frozen R0.74O \(P_R\), \(X_R\), or
\(\mathfrak C_R\), and do not prove any of their amplitude asymptotics.
