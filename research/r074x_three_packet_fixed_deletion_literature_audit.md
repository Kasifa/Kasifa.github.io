## 3. Exact Navier--Stokes shear precedent

N. K. Singh and S. Sridhar,
[*Plane shearing waves of arbitrary form: exact solutions of the
Navier--Stokes equations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.5507),
arXiv:1101.5507 (2011; revised 2017), later *European Physical Journal Plus*
132 (2017), 403,
[DOI 10.1140/epjp/i2017-11659-5](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjp/i2017-11659-5).

The source constructs exact incompressible Navier--Stokes solutions in a
background linear shear and permits superposition of Kelvin modes with
parallel time-dependent wave vectors.  This is a genuine exact-flow
precedent.  Its geometry and observable differ from R0.74X: it does not state
the three derivative-heat packet construction, the two completed-clock
coordinates, the fixed-deletion quantifier, or the cubic-payment comparison.

## 4. Pathwise and large-deviation scalar transport

V. Gardner, K. L. Liss, and J. C. Mattingly,
[*A pathwise approach to the enhanced dissipation of passive scalars advected
by shear flows*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05657),
arXiv:2410.05657 (2024).

The source studies stochastic particle trajectories for drift--diffusion in
shear flows, uses Girsanov control, and obtains local-in-space decay
information along streamlines.  It is relevant to the probabilistic language
behind remote packet survival.  It does not embed the scalar into the exact
three-component Navier--Stokes solution used by R0.74X and does not compare
two shell clocks with a local-energy cubic payment.

P. H. Haynes and J. Vanneste,
[*Dispersion in the large-deviation regime. Part I: shear flows and periodic
flows*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.6665),
arXiv:1401.6665 (2014),
[DOI 10.1017/jfm.2014.64](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2014.64).

This source develops rate functions for far passive-scalar concentration
tails and treats shear flows explicitly.  R0.74X instead uses a
scale-dependent conditional bridge comparison inherited from R0.74W and
feeds two resulting strip estimates into a fixed-deletion clock.  The
screened record does not contain that downstream object or its payment
blocker.

## 5. Local energy and weak-solution boundary

H. J. Choe and M. Yang,
[*Local kinetic energy and singularities of the incompressible
Navier--Stokes Equations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.04561),
arXiv:1705.04561 (2017).

The paper studies partial regularity under uniform control of a scaled local
kinetic-energy functional and derives singular-set dimension bounds.  It
provides neighboring local-energy context, but not the R0.74X packet
construction, deletion order, or exponent-level cubic-payment obstruction.

W. S. Ożański,
[*Weak solutions to the Navier-Stokes inequality with arbitrary energy
profiles*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02109),
arXiv:1809.02109 (2018; revised 2019), *Communications in Mathematical
Physics* 374 (2020), 33--62,
[DOI 10.1007/s00220-019-03588-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-019-03588-0).

The source constructs spatially localized fields satisfying the strong and
local energy inequalities with prescribed nonincreasing energy profiles.
Its fields need not solve the Navier--Stokes equations.  R0.74X instead uses
an exact smooth unforced solution and proves only a construction-level
endpoint/payment comparison.  Neither result supplies the other's theorem.
