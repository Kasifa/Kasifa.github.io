# R0.71W claim--evidence and gap matrix

**Audit date:** 2026-08-26

**Release boundary:** an exact globally smooth amplitude-doped triangular
2.5D sequence that defeats the complete data-uniform first-row Leray ledger
on a fixed macroscopic interval. The initial energy/enstrophy are not uniform.
