**Figure R0.70K — Normalized vorticity anisotropy is bounded but not
dissipative.** **A,** For the frozen source
\(\Sigma_0=\operatorname{diag}(-1/2,-1/2,1)\), an isotropic covariance
evolves exactly as
\(p=e^{3t}/(e^{3t}+2)\),
\(q=(e^{3t}-1)/(e^{3t}+2)\), and
\(|B|_F^2=2q^2/3\), approaching the sharp rank-one value \(2/3\).
**B,** The corresponding source-correlation production
\((1+2q)(1-q)\) is nonnegative on the realizable axisymmetric interval and
vanishes only at source eigenspace states. **C,** For the exact periodic
Navier--Stokes shear
\(u=(D e^{-4\nu t}\sin2z/2,-C e^{-\nu t}\sin z,0)\), the nonlinearity
vanishes and
\(\nu^{-1}d|B|_F^2/dt=12p(1-p)(2p-1)\), giving both signs and the exact
values \(\mp144/125\) at \(p=1/5,4/5\). All curves are evaluations of
closed formulas, not DNS. The figure does not establish regularity,
singularity formation, or a Millennium-problem result.
