dimensionless time \(\tau=\nu t\), the selected packet works are
\(w_1=2e^{-8\tau}\) and \(w_2=-2e^{-14\tau}\). **A**, their parent sum

Thus a zero signed parent packet is not preserved even by the linear heat

\(\tau=0\), \(E_{\rm root}=0\) while
\(E_{\rm fine}=\delta=1/2\), so all positive fine-ledger mass is initially
hidden by parent cancellation. At \(\tau_*=\log2/6\),
\(E_{\rm root}=2^{-16/3}\), \(E_{\rm fine}=2^{-7/3}\), and
\(\delta=7\,2^{-16/3}\). Values come from closed exact formulas and are
converted to binary64 only at the plotting boundary. This figure is an exact
