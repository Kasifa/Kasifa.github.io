# Figure contract

- **Question.** How do hidden-mean excitation, the explicit analytic transient
  envelope, and finite frozen projection diagnostics constrain the next
  low-gap theorem?
- **Takeaway.** The normalized bracket `h_d/(i c_mu)` has a nonzero limit along
  `c_mu -> c0 != 0`; fixed `Lambda` gives `c_mu -> 0` and remains undecided. The
  instantaneous lifted line is non-invariant at every positive gap. The
  proved `X_mu` estimate necessarily carries a transient prefactor; fixed
  one- or two-mode deletion does not uniformly stabilize the frozen screen.
- **Surface.** Static 178 mm by 145 mm journal figure; vector PDF/SVG and
  600-dpi PNG after rendering.
- **Families.** Exact relationship curves with limiting reference, analytic
  trend/envelope with a 120-row certified overlay, and signed grouped dot
  comparison on an explicitly transformed axis.
- **Data.** 244 hidden-mean rows, 61 singular-limit rows, 243 `J` rows, 243
  analytic-envelope rows, and 30 validated finite target rows expanded to 60
  plotted metric rows plus 120 propagator-certificate rows (971 total).
- **Palette.** Blue and gold roots plus neutrals. Line style, marker shape and
  fill, zero references, panel separation, and direct text are redundant.
- **Fallback.** Missing or unsealed propagator certificate permits source/draft
  layout only and blocks formal sealing. Any failed source hash, formula, row count, vector,
  font, or visible-boundary check blocks publication.
- **Scale QA.** Panel C signed-log domains are fixed at `[-0.25,5]` and
  `[-0.25,8]`, covering the full audited data with at least `0.2` transformed
  units of padding and retaining the zero reference. Formula annotations stay
  outside data rectangles; Panel B carries explicit `J start`, envelope
  `mu/|c|/s`, and 120-row certified-grid legends.
- **Certificate crosscheck.** Preserve raw certified gains and require
  `0 < gain <= bound + 2e-8`; the fixed tolerance covers numerical roundoff and
  is not a theorem slack or a license to clip values.
