# Figure R0.71K — Fixed matched cells preserve the two-power heat-payment gap

**A.** An explicit \(C_c^\infty\) one-dimensional atom \(h\) and its two
neighbors satisfy \(\sum_n h(y-2\pi n)=1\).  Their tensor products give the
fixed three-dimensional matched partition.  At the selected parent
\(\kappa=4K\), it has \(K^3\) translated cells, radius \(O(K^{-1})\), and
overlap at most eight.

**B.** The closed pure-heat parent profile

\[
 A_0(\theta)=\frac{16(e^{-34\theta}-e^{-52\theta})^2}
 {D_0(\theta)Y_0(\theta)}
\]

starts at zero.  At \(\theta_*=(\log2)/18\),
\(A_0=A_*=1.1965465392\times10^{-5}\).  The open marker is the independent
one-cell quadrature for the explicit template:
\(A_{\rm loc}=1.6511660825\times10^{-5}\).  It is a diagnostic, not an input
to the theorem.  Analytically,
\(A_{\rm loc}(\theta_*)\ge A_*/C_{\rm part}\) in the pure-heat limit, and the
large finite-\(K\) theorem retains half that lower bound.

**C.** Constants are divided out to display only the certified powers:

\[
 \frac{64C_{\rm part}}{A_*}\,\mathcal Z_K^{\rm lower}=K^{-2},
 \qquad
 \frac{2\nu}{N(1-2^{-1/9})}\,\mathcal H_K^{\rm upper}=K^{-4}.
\]

The exponents are exact and are not regression fits.  Their ratio therefore
grows as \(K^2\).  The lower bound is asserted for all sufficiently large
dyadic \(K\); no numerical threshold \(K_0\) is claimed.

**D.** The bars list powers of \(K\).  Per cell,
\(B_Q\sim K^0\), \(d_Q\sim K\), \(q_Q\sim K^{-1}\), and
\(a_Q\sim K^{-3}\).  After summing \(K^3\) cells, weighting, and integrating,
positive creation is \(K^{-2}\), local heat payment is \(K^{-4}\), and the
viscous-collar budget can also be \(K^{-2}\).  The collar is therefore
leading and is not eliminated by this no-go.

All curves come from closed formulas and a deterministic partition sampler.
There is no DNS, ODE/PDE time stepping, fitted model, or random data.  The
result covers one fixed aligned matched partition and the parent-only broad
frame.  It proves no unconditional continuation criterion, global
regularity, finite-time singularity, originality, or Millennium-problem
conclusion.
