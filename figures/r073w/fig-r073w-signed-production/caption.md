**R0.73W signed production on the heat plane.** (A) For periodic or
boundary-decaying transport, the exact heat-plane balance
\((\partial_t-\nu\partial_s)E_s=-\langle\Pi_s\rangle\) implies, along
\(s'(t)=-\nu\),
\(\int_{t_0}^{t_1}\langle\Pi_{s(t)}(t)\rangle\,dt
=E_{s(t_0)}(t_0)-E_{s(t_1)}(t_1)\).  The drawn characteristics use
\(\nu=1\) only for coordinates.  This is a signed spatial-mean identity, not
an estimate of \(\int|\Pi_s|\). (B) The Leray--Hopf energy class gives the
analytic envelopes
\(\|\Pi_s\|_{L^1_{t,x}}\lesssim s^{-1/4}\) and
\(\int_0^S\|\Pi_s\|_{L^1_{t,x}}\,ds\lesssim S^{3/4}\), after suppressing the
common energy factors and absolute constants.  Both plotted shapes are
normalized to one at unit scale.  They are upper-bound shapes, not observations,
DNS output, fits, or sharpness evidence. (C) For the exact rank-three
Fourier-support field \(u_A=\pm AR\), two independent finite producers give
\(\langle\Pi_s\rangle/A^3=\pm\tfrac14q^2(1-q^2)\), \(q=e^{-s}\).
The extrema occur at \(s=\tfrac12\log2\) with magnitude \(1/16\); changing
\(R\) to \(-R\) reverses production while leaving the quadratic defect
unchanged. (D) Exact cancellation gives
\[
 { |\langle\Pi_s\rangle| \over \nu\langle D_{ii,s}\rangle}
 ={A\over\nu}\,{q^2\over2(13+12q^2+10q^4+4q^6)}.
\]
The plotted dimensionless coefficient has the exact endpoint
\(\lim_{s\downarrow0}c_{\rm abs}(s)=1/78\); its small interior maximum is
retained rather than replaced by a monotone schematic.  Panels C--D are exact
finite counterexamples to universal sign and amplitude-independent same-time
quadratic absorption statements, not PDE trajectories, generic turbulence,
singularity evidence, a regularity theorem, global regularity, or a Clay
conclusion.
