投影 Lamb 场的热堆积与三维 Navier–Stokes 流的尖锐迹障碍

摘要

对于三维不可压缩 Navier–Stokes 方程，涡量拉伸项和输运项可以合并为无散 Lamb 向量 \(L=\mathbb P(u\times\omega)\) 的旋度。本文研究基于这一表示构造的有符号 Littlewood–Paley 壳注入量。 相应的竖直热扩展具有无条件的热体积估计：以涡量平方积分归一化后，对于有限区间上的每个周期 Leray–Hopf 解， 全热高度积分关于物理时间属于 \(L^1\)。 这一估计在有界重叠的空间局部化下仍然成立，并给出一个有条件的局部延拓判据。

热体积估计不能恢复底部迹。对于一个固定的光滑径向紧框架，沿二进频率 \(K=2^j\) 构造的显式全局光滑 2D3C Navier–Stokes 解族，给出底部迹与热体积量之间的精确因子 \(2K^2\)。 同一个解族表明，匹配尺度的单位分解不会掩盖这一损失；另一个协变内部柱体构造则排除了所有仅依赖几何的 \(o(r^{-2})\) 因子。第二项构造将有限维隐函数定理应用于精确 Navier–Stokes 流， 在指定的正时间使整个目标壳为零，同时保持非线性驱动非零。 经过振幅–频率缩放，尺度不变的入壳原子为 \(\lambda^{-4}\) 阶，而归一化的裸 Leray–Lamb 时间预算为 \(\lambda^{-6}\) 阶。 因此，无法仅用这一裸预算以统一常数支付这类内部入壳事件。 一个出向余面积恒等式给出了每个有限阶入壳事件的精确尺度匹配表示，但本文并未控制其零水平占据密度。 这些结果均未证明全局正则性，也未构造奇性。 计算机辅助的有限傅里叶、谱重建及 Galerkin 计算仅用于核查和佐证；本文不从浮点输出推断任何解析定理。

1 引言

设 \(u\) 为无散速度场，\(\omega=\operatorname{curl}u\) 为其涡量。 在三维情形下，直接建立全局涡量平方积分估计的障碍是涡拉伸功 \(\int S:\omega\otimes\omega\)，其中 \(S=(\nabla u+\nabla u^{T})/2\)。 经典正则性理论通过附加几何一致性、临界空间中的小性条件，或可积的强范数来控制这一功项； 代表性工作包括 [9]、[2] 和 [7]。 本文的目标更为有限：考察涡量平方积分注入的一种有符号频率局部化形式， 并精确区分其中哪些部分可由 Leray 能量不等式推出，哪些部分不能。

作为出发点的恒等式是经典的。相差一个 Bernoulli 梯度项的意义下， Navier–Stokes 非线性项就是无散 Lamb 向量 \[L=\mathbb P(u\times\omega)=\partial_tu-\nu\Delta u.\] 无散 Lamb 分解已见于流体力学及旋度算子的数学表述 [10], [5], [8]。 本文的贡献并非这一分解本身，而是下文特定的有符号壳商、其能量层面的热堆积估计， 以及由光滑解实现的、阻碍恢复底部迹的尖锐障碍。

1.1 主要结果

固定一个实偶标量紧傅里叶框架 \((T_j)_j\)。对于热高度 \(s\ge0\)，令 \[W_{j,s}=e^{s\Delta}T_j\omega, \qquad F_{j,s}=e^{s\Delta}T_jL, \qquad D_{j,s}=\left\lVert \nabla W_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2,\] 并定义完整壳注入量 \[b_{j,s}=\left\langle \operatorname{curl}W_{j,s},F_{j,s}\right\rangle.\] 正部壳平方函数为 \[\Theta_s^2 =\sum_{D_{j,s}>0}\frac{\left(b_{j,s}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,s}}.\] 第一个定理在第 3–4 节证明，其内容为 \[\begin{equation} \int_0^\infty\Theta_s^2\,\mathrm ds \le \frac12\left\lVert L\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2 \le \frac12\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^4. \end{equation}\](1) 若令 \(Y(t)=\left\lVert \omega(t)\right\rVert_2^2\) 以及 \[\mathcal V(t)=Y(t)^{-1}\int_0^\infty\Theta_s(t)^2\,\mathrm ds,\] 并在 \(Y=0\) 时取值为零，则每个周期 Leray–Hopf 解都满足 \[\begin{equation} \int_0^T\mathcal V(t)\,\mathrm dt \le \frac{C_S^3}{2\sqrt{2\nu}}\left\lVert u_0\right\rVert_2^2\sqrt T. \end{equation}\](2) 这是能量层面的无条件结论。 有界重叠的局部化给出带有重叠常数的相应估计，同时给出一个有条件的局部延拓判据。

底部值 \[A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(t)=\Theta_0(t)^2/Y(t)\] 则有所不同。第 6 节构造了一个固定的二进协变径向框架及一个全局光滑 2D3C 解族， 使得在每个二进频率 \(K=2^j\) 上都有 \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(0)=2K^2\mathcal V(0). \end{equation}\](3) 对于每个非零、非负的光滑截断函数，这一恒等式仍成立。 在抛物型热高度 \(h=\theta K^{-2}\) 处，高度平均值是底部值的一个固定正比例。 匹配尺度的单位分解保留同一尺度律。 另一个独立的全空间缩放论证将该障碍置于严格内部的容许倾斜柱体上， 并排除仅依赖几何、用于从热体积量恢复底部迹的 \(o(r^{-2})\) 因子。 临界的 \(Cr^{-2}\) 因子被这一构造饱和，并未被排除。

第 7 节回应静态迹例子在时间方面可能受到的质疑。从 \[U=(0,\cos x_1,\cos x_2),\] 出发，对精确局部 Navier–Stokes 流在目标壳上施加扰动， 使整个壳在指定的正时间 \(\tau\) 消失，而其非线性驱动保持非零。 所得零点对应一个真正的单重正向内部入壳事件。 选取基础振幅 \(a_\lambda=\lambda^{-2}\)，再施加整数倍的 Navier–Stokes 缩放，得到 \[\begin{equation} \frac{\text{internal entry atom}} {\displaystyle\int \left\lVert L_\lambda\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2/Y_\lambda\,\mathrm dt} =\frac{2\nu}{\sinh(2\nu\tau)}\lambda^2+o(\lambda^2). \end{equation}\](4) 初始动能与临界 \(\dot H^{1/2}\) 范数趋于零，而初始涡量平方积分保持有界。 因此，这种失效并非由很大的能量预算或位于初始时刻的观测面造成。

最后一个肯定性结论是：所有孤立的有限阶入壳事件均具有精确的出向余面积表示。 其密度具有正确的尺度，但集中在特殊的零水平上。 在本文的论证中，Leray 不等式并未提供所需的一致占据估计。

1.2 与相关工作的关系

热扩展视角在临界 Navier–Stokes 理论中已有成熟应用，尤以 \(BMO^{-1}\) 框架下的 [7] 为代表。 本文的平方函数依赖于解，平方前保留符号，并以涡量平方积分归一化；它不是 Koch–Tataru 范数。 [13] 给出了严格的倾斜柱体几何理论，[12] 则将其用于导数估计。 这些覆盖定理本身并不能恢复独立热高度变量上的边界值。 近期的热方程迹估计从更强的内部导数范数恢复边界正则性 [1]， 所用的并非式 (1) 中的零阶热体积量。

在本稿准备过程中找到的、最接近的滤波涡量比较工作是 [14]。 该工作控制正的近场拉伸部分，并在附加远场项、交换子项及局部化项的可求和性条件下，得到有条件的无权闭合。 其观测量与闭合假设均不同于本文。 同样，若没有额外的紧性或正则性输入，精确交换子恒等式也不能推出缺陷消失； 这一差别在 [3] 中已为人熟知。

本文不提出优先权主张，也不声称穷尽了文献。其确切范围汇总于第 10 节。

2 设定与记号

本文的周期部分在 \(\mathbb T^3=(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)^3\) 上进行，空间测度采用归一化测度。 速度具有零均值，并满足 \[\begin{equation} \partial_tu+u\cdot\nabla u+\nabla p=\nu\Delta u, \qquad \nabla\cdot u=0, \qquad \omega=\operatorname{curl}u. \end{equation}\](5) 所有傅里叶乘子在零模态上均设为零。Leray 投影记为 \(\mathbb P\)。

一个实偶标量紧框架是由标量傅里叶乘子组成的可数族 \((T_j)_j\)，其符号 \(m_j(k)\) 满足 \[\begin{equation} m_j(-k)=m_j(k)\in\mathbb R, \qquad \sum_j\left\lvert m_j(k)\right\rvert^2=1 \quad (k\ne0). \end{equation}\](6) 对于有限框架，所有求和均按通常的有限和理解。 当求和项非负时，无限和先作有限截断，再通过单调收敛或 Fatou 引理解释。

在能量正则性层面，将 \(e^{s\Delta}L\) 视作 \(L^2\) 场的表达式均在 \(s>0\) 时理解。 其热高度积分先对壳数作有限截断，并避开零热高度，再通过 Tonelli 定理和 Fatou 引理定义。 在 \(s=0\) 时，仅当 \(L\in L^2\) 才使用这些壳系数； 特别地，在几乎每个具有 \(H^2\) 正则性的时刻，周期 \(H^1\) 强解均满足这一条件。 在其余零测时间集合上改变系数的取值，不影响下文任何延拓假设。

式 (5) 的旋转形式给出 \[\begin{equation} u\times\omega=L+\nabla B, \qquad B=p+\frac12\left\lvert u\right\rvert^2, \qquad L=\mathbb P(u\times\omega) =\partial_tu-\nu\Delta u =-\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(u\otimes u). \end{equation}\](7) 取旋度可得 \[\begin{equation} \partial_t\omega-\nu\Delta\omega =\operatorname{curl}L=S\omega-u\cdot\nabla\omega. \end{equation}\](8)

对于 \(s\ge0\)，定义 \[\begin{equation} A_{j,s}=e^{s\Delta}T_j, \quad W_{j,s}=A_{j,s}\omega, \quad F_{j,s}=A_{j,s}L, \quad D_{j,s}=\left\lVert \nabla W_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2. \end{equation}\](9) 由于 \(W_{j,s}\) 无散，有 \(D_{j,s}=\left\lVert \operatorname{curl}W_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2\)。 热扩展后的涡量方程为 \[\begin{equation} (\partial_t-\nu\partial_s)W_{j,s}=\operatorname{curl}F_{j,s}. \end{equation}\](10)

3 有符号壳延拓系数

在热高度 \(s\)，定义完整壳功 \[\begin{equation} b_{j,s}=\left\langle \operatorname{curl}W_{j,s},F_{j,s}\right\rangle. \end{equation}\](11) 其中包含拉伸项及输运–滤波交换子。事实上， \[\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} b_{j,s} &=\left\langle W_{j,s},A_{j,s}(S\omega-u\cdot\nabla\omega)\right\rangle\\ &=\left\langle W_{j,s},A_{j,s}(S\omega)+[u\cdot\nabla,A_{j,s}]\omega\right\rangle. \end{aligned} \end{equation}\](12) 平流项与 \(W_{j,s}\) 在整个空间域上的配对为零。 压力属于式 (7) 中的梯度补项，因此不是第三个独立的涡量功项。

在 \(s=0\) 时，记 \[\begin{equation} \Theta_0^2=\sum_{D_{j,0}>0}\frac{\left(b_{j,0}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,0}}, \qquad A_{\mathrm{sb},+}=\frac{\Theta_0^2}{Y}, \qquad Y=\left\lVert \omega\right\rVert_2^2, \end{equation}\](13) 并在 \(Y=0\) 时取值为零。

命题 3.1 (有条件的壳延拓). 设 \(u\) 是周期 \(H^1\) 强解，其极大存在时间区间为 \([0,T_*)\)。若 \[\begin{equation} \int_0^{T_*}A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(t)\,\mathrm dt<\infty, \end{equation}\](14) 则 \(u\) 可延拓到 \(T_*\) 之后。

证明。 令 \(D=\left\lVert \nabla\omega\right\rVert_2^2\) 及 \(\mathfrak P=\int S:\omega\otimes\omega\)。 紧框架性质给出 \(\sum_jD_{j,0}=D\) 和 \(\sum_jb_{j,0}=\mathfrak P\)。 正部的次可加性及加权 Cauchy 不等式推出 \[\mathfrak P_+ \le \sqrt D\,\Theta_0 \le \frac\nu2D+\frac1{2\nu}A_{\mathrm{sb},+}Y.\] 因此，由涡量平方积分恒等式得到 \[Y'+\nu D\le\nu^{-1}A_{\mathrm{sb},+}Y.\] Gronwall 不等式与式 (14) 使 \(Y\) 在 \([0,T_*)\) 上有界， 并使 \(D\) 属于 \(L^1(0,T_*)\)。 于是标准的强解延拓定理给出所需延拓；例如可参见 [4], [6], [11]。 ◻

注 3.2.. 命题 3.1 是一个尺度临界的有条件归约，而不是无条件的正则性判据。 本文其余部分检验能否从受能量估计控制的热扩展推出式 (14)。

4 投影 Lamb 场的无条件热堆积

对于 \(s\ge0\)，令 \[\begin{equation} \Theta_s^2 =\sum_{D_{j,s}>0}\frac{\left(b_{j,s}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,s}}. \end{equation}\](15)

定理 4.1 (全域热体积估计). 在每个满足 \(u\in H^1(\mathbb T^3)\) 的时刻，都有 \[\begin{equation} \Theta_s^2\le\left\lVert e^{s\Delta}L\right\rVert_2^2\quad(s>0), \qquad \int_0^\infty\Theta_s^2\,\mathrm ds \le\frac12\left\lVert L\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2 \le\frac12\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^4. \end{equation}\](16) 若 \(L\in L^2\)，则逐点不等式在 \(s=0\) 时也成立。 对于周期 Leray–Hopf 解及每个有限的 \(T\)，归一化热体积量 \[\begin{equation} \mathcal V(t)=\frac1{Y(t)}\int_0^\infty\Theta_s(t)^2\,\mathrm ds \end{equation}\](17) 满足式 (2)。

证明。 逐壳应用 Cauchy 不等式，并利用式 (11)，得到 \[\frac{\left(b_{j,s}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,s}} \le\left\lVert F_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2.\] 求和并使用式 (6)，即可证明式 (16) 中的第一个不等式。 由于 \(L\) 具有零均值， \[\int_0^\infty\left\lVert e^{s\Delta}L\right\rVert_2^2\,\mathrm ds =\frac12\left\lVert (-\Delta)^{-1/2}L\right\rVert_2^2.\] 乘子 \((-\Delta)^{-1/2}\mathbb P\nabla\cdot\) 从采用 Frobenius 范数的矩阵场映射到向量场时， 其 \(L^2\) 算子范数至多为 1。因此，由式 (7) 得到 \[\left\lVert L\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}} \le\left\lVert u\otimes u\right\rVert_2=\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^2.\] 这就证明了式 (16)。

三维插值不等式和 Sobolev 不等式给出 \[\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^4 \le\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2\left\lVert u\right\rVert_6^3 \le C_S^3\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2Y^{3/2}.\] 因此 \[\mathcal V(t)\le\frac{C_S^3}{2}\left\lVert u(t)\right\rVert_2Y(t)^{1/2}.\] Leray 不等式推出 \[\left\lVert u(t)\right\rVert_2\le\left\lVert u_0\right\rVert_2, \qquad \nu\int_0^TY(t)\,\mathrm dt\le\frac12\left\lVert u_0\right\rVert_2^2.\] 对时间应用 Cauchy 不等式，即得式 (2)。

对于弱解，先在几乎每个具有 \(H^1\) 正则性的时刻， 对有限壳数及热高度截断应用上述论证，再由 Tonelli 定理和 Fatou 引理得到所述解释。 弱形式中并未插入依赖于解的检验函数。 ◻

现在可以显式写出尚未解决的迹问题的因子分解。分母为正时，定义 \[\begin{equation} \Lambda_L^2(t)= \frac{\Theta_0(t)^2}{\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\Theta_s(t)^2\,\mathrm ds}; \end{equation}\](18) 否则令 \(\Lambda_L=0\)。于是 \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{sb},+}=\Lambda_L^2\mathcal V. \end{equation}\](19) 定理 4.1 控制了 \(\mathcal V\)，但并未控制 \(\Lambda_L^2\)。

5 有界重叠的局部化

设 \((\chi_{j,Q})_Q\) 是匹配半径 \(r_j\simeq K_j^{-1}\) 上的非负光滑单位分解， 其中 \(T_j\) 的频率支集位于 \(cK_j\le\left\lvert k\right\rvert\le CK_j\)。 假设导数具有一致的控制界，且重叠次数至多为 \(N\)。定义 \[\begin{equation} B_{j,Q}(t,s)=\left\langle F_{j,s},\operatorname{curl}(\chi_{j,Q}W_{j,s})\right\rangle, \qquad d_{j,Q}(t,s)=\left\lVert \operatorname{curl}(\chi_{j,Q}W_{j,s})\right\rVert_2^2, \end{equation}\](20) 并令 \[\begin{equation} q_{j,Q}(t,s)= \begin{cases} \left(B_{j,Q}(t,s)\right)_{+}^2/d_{j,Q}(t,s),&d_{j,Q}>0,\\ 0,&d_{j,Q}=0. \end{cases} \end{equation}\](21) 式 (20) 中的旋度精确保留了截断函数过渡环带的贡献： \[B_{j,Q} =\int\chi_{j,Q}F_{j,s}\cdot\operatorname{curl}W_{j,s} +\int F_{j,s}\cdot(\nabla\chi_{j,Q}\times W_{j,s}).\]

命题 5.1 (局部堆积与判据). 在上述假设下， \[\begin{equation} \sum_{j,Q}q_{j,Q}(t,s) \le N\left\lVert e^{s\Delta}L(t)\right\rVert_2^2. \end{equation}\](22) 对每个 \(\alpha>0\)，都有 \[\begin{equation} \int_0^\infty s^{\alpha-1}\sum_{j,Q}q_{j,Q}(t,s)\,\mathrm ds \le\frac{N\Gamma(\alpha)}{2^\alpha} \left\lVert (-\Delta)^{-\alpha/2}L(t)\right\rVert_2^2. \end{equation}\](23) 当 \(\alpha=1\) 时，左端归一化后属于 \(L^1(0,T)\)， 其时间积分由式 (2) 的右端乘以 \(N\) 控制。

此外，若 \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{loc},+}(t) =\frac1{Y(t)}\sum_{j,Q}q_{j,Q}(t,0) \end{equation}\](24) 在极大强解时间区间上可积，则该解可以延拓。

证明。 在式 (20) 中应用 Cauchy 不等式，得到 \[q_{j,Q}\le \left\lVert 1_{\operatorname{supp}\chi_{j,Q}}F_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2.\] 有界重叠与紧框架性质给出式 (22)。 通过谱积分得到式 (23)；随后，\(\alpha=1\) 的情形可按定理 4.1 的方法推出。

对于延拓判据，匹配尺度的环带 Bernstein 估计及截断过渡环带估计给出 \[\sum_Qd_{j,Q}\le C_{\chi}\,D_{j,0}.\] 由于 \(\sum_QB_{j,Q}=b_{j,0}\)，加权 Cauchy 不等式给出 \[\frac{\left(b_{j,0}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,0}} \le C_{\chi}\sum_Qq_{j,Q}(t,0).\] 因此 \(A_{\mathrm{sb},+}\le C_{\chi}A_{\mathrm{loc},+}\)，从而可以应用命题 3.1。 ◻

注 5.2.. 本文并不声称这一局部判据弱于已有的标准判据。 直接应用 Cauchy 不等式可得 \(A_{\mathrm{loc},+}\le N\left\lVert L\right\rVert_2^2/Y \le N\left\lVert u\right\rVert_\infty^2\)， 因此 Serrin 端点条件 \(u\in L_t^2L_x^\infty\) 蕴含这一判据。

6 精确的临界迹损失

上述估计对热高度变量作积分。本节表明，即使对于全局光滑解，恢复尚未控制的边界迹也需要付出两个频率幂次的代价。

对 \(\sigma\in\{-1,1\}\)、\(a>0\) 以及二进整数频率 \(K=2^j\)、\(j\ge0\)，定义 \[\begin{equation} u_{\sigma,a,K}(x)=aK \left(0,-2\cos(Kx_1), 2\sigma\sin(Kx_1+Kx_2)-2\cos(Kx_2)\right). \end{equation}\](25) 该向量场为实值、零均值、无散，并且与 \(x_3\) 无关。 其水平速度是衰减的二维剪切流，第三分量满足由该剪切流驱动的线性平流扩散方程。 因此，它生成一个全局光滑的三维周期解。

选取一个固定的、光滑且在二进缩放下协变的径向紧框架，先将半径 \(K\) 和 \(\sqrt2K\) 放入同一个父块，再将它们精确拆分到低频子块和高频子块。 这样的框架可由 \(\log_2\left\lvert \xi\right\rvert\) 上具有平顶区的光滑平方分割，再经一次固定的正弦余弦细分得到。 该框架在选择 \(K\) 之前就已固定。

当 \(\sigma=-1\) 时，唯一给出正注入的子块是低半径块。直接作 Fourier 计算可得 \[\begin{equation} b_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)=2a^3K^6e^{-2K^2s}, \quad D_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)=4a^2K^6e^{-2K^2s}, \quad q_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)=a^4K^6e^{-2K^2s}. \end{equation}\](26)

定理 6.1 (尖锐的整体与局部化迹代价). 对解族 (25) 及上述固定框架，有 \[\begin{equation} q_{\mathrm{lo}}(0)=2K^2\int_0^\infty q_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)\,\mathrm ds, \end{equation}\](27) 并且，取 \(Y=8a^2K^4\)，有 \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(0)=\frac{a^2K^2}{8}, \qquad \mathcal V(0)=\frac{a^2}{16}, \qquad A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(0)=2K^2\mathcal V(0). \end{equation}\](28)

设 \(\phi\ge0\) 为任意非零光滑截断函数，并在选取初始数据之前固定；令 \(q_\phi\) 由 (20) 中的完整局部化功定义。则对每个 \(h>0\)，有 \[\begin{equation} q_\phi(0)=\frac{2K^2}{1-e^{-2K^2h}} \int_0^h q_\phi(s)\,\mathrm ds. \end{equation}\](29) 特别地，当 \(h=\theta K^{-2}\) 时，热高度平均值是底部值的一个固定正常数倍。

证明。 式 (27) 可由对 (26) 的最后一个公式积分得到；再除以 \(Y\)，即得 (28)。

对于局部化结论，低频块为 \[W_s=2aK^2e^{-K^2s}(\sin(Kx_2),0,\sin(Kx_1)), \quad F_s=-2a^2K^3e^{-K^2s}(0,0,\cos(Kx_2)).\] 包括截断过渡带在内的完整局部化功满足 \[B_\phi(s)=\left\langle F_s,\operatorname{curl}(\phi W_s)\right\rangle =4a^3K^6e^{-2K^2s} \int\phi(x)\sin^2(Kx_2)\,\mathrm dx>0.\] \(F_s\) 与 \(W_s\) 都位于 \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert=K\) 上，故 \(q_\phi(s)=e^{-2K^2s}q_\phi(0)\)。在 \([0,h]\) 上积分便得到 (29)。 ◻

在匹配的热高度处， \[\begin{equation} \frac{h^{-1}\int_0^h q_\phi(s)\,\mathrm ds}{q_\phi(0)} =\frac{1-e^{-2\theta}}{2\theta}. \end{equation}\](30) 该因子为正，且与 \(K\) 无关。

匹配分割并不能消除这一尺度。如果 \((\phi_Q)_Q\) 是半径 \(r=\rho/K\) 上的标准非负分割，其重叠数为 \(N\)，且满足一致的导数平方和界，则 \[\begin{equation} \frac{a^4K^6e^{-2K^2s}} {2(C_0+C_1/\rho^2)} \le\sum_Qq_Q(s) \le2Na^4K^6e^{-2K^2s}. \end{equation}\](31) 选择 \(a=K^{-1}\) 可保持 \(\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2^2=6\)。归一化后的底部总量仍介于两个正常数之间，而其热体积分为 \(O(K^{-2})\)。

6.1 内部柱体

上述精确解族是在初始时刻的迹处计算的。同一临界幂次也出现在严格内部柱体上，但相应结论的范围更窄。

仅在本小节中，采用协变的单块紧框架 \(T_0=I\)。在中心时刻 \(t_Q\) 考察倾斜柱体 \(Q\)，令 \(\chi_Q\) 为其协变输运的非负空间截断函数，并设 \[W_s=e^{s\Delta}\omega(t_Q),\qquad F_s=e^{s\Delta}L(t_Q).\] 定义 \[\begin{equation} B_Q(s)=\left\langle F_s,\operatorname{curl}(\chi_QW_s)\right\rangle,\qquad d_Q(s)=\left\lVert \operatorname{curl}(\chi_QW_s)\right\rVert_2^2, \end{equation}\](32) \[q_Q(s)= \begin{cases} \left(B_Q(s)\right)_{+}^2/d_Q(s),&d_Q(s)>0,\\ 0,&d_Q(s)=0. \end{cases}\] 取 \(Y_Q=\left\lVert \omega(t_Q)\right\rVert_2^2\)，定义 \[\begin{equation} A_Q=\frac{q_Q(0)}{Y_Q},\qquad \mathcal V_{Q,h}=\frac1{Y_Q}\int_0^h q_Q(s)\,\mathrm ds. \end{equation}\](33)

定理 6.2 (仅依赖几何的次临界迹估计不可能成立). 存在一族在尺度变换下协变的全空间局部时间光滑 Navier–Stokes 解，每个解均定义在包含一个严格内部的可容许倾斜柱体 \(Q_r\) 的时间区间上，并满足 \[\begin{equation} \frac{A_{Q_r}}{\mathcal V_{Q_r,\theta r^2}}=c_*r^{-2}, \qquad c_*>0. \end{equation}\](34) 因此，若估计 \[A_Q\le c(r)\mathcal V_{Q,\theta r^2}\] 要求对这一协变单块类中的所有光滑解及所有柱体成立，并且常数仅依赖一致的几何参数与可容许性参数，那么就不可能有 \(c(r)=o(r^{-2})\)。

证明。 令 \[u_{\mathrm{aff}}=(x_1,-x_2-x_3,0), \qquad \omega_{\mathrm{aff}}=(1,0,0).\] 令 \(A_0=-\frac13x\times u_{\mathrm{aff}}\)，并选取 \(\eta\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^3)\)，使其在某个球上恒等于一。则 \(u_0=\operatorname{curl}(\eta A_0)\) 光滑、紧支撑且无散。 由于 \(u_{\mathrm{aff}}\) 是线性、齐次且无散的， \(\operatorname{curl}A_0=u_{\mathrm{aff}}\)，故在较小的仿射核心区域上有 \(u_0=u_{\mathrm{aff}}\)。

在仿射核心区域内， \(\operatorname{curl}L=(\omega\cdot\nabla)u-(u\cdot\nabla)\omega=(1,0,0)\)。 选取支撑于该核心区域内的非负截断函数 \(\chi\)，使 \(\nabla\chi\times\omega_{\mathrm{aff}}\not\equiv0\)。在初始时刻的迹处，有 \[\int\chi\,\omega_{\mathrm{aff}}\cdot\operatorname{curl}L\,\mathrm dx>0, \qquad \left\lVert \operatorname{curl}(\chi\omega_{\mathrm{aff}})\right\rVert_2>0.\] 将初始数据乘以一个充分小的固定正振幅，仍可保持这两个不等式。光滑解的局部存在性与连续性保证，它们在一个较小的正中心时刻仍然成立。 充分小的倾斜柱体在 [13] 的意义下是可容许的，因为它们的可容许性量有上界 \(r^2\sup\left\lVert \nabla u\right\rVert_\infty\)。

固定一个这样的基准柱体，半径为 \(r_0\)。由关于 \(s\) 的连续性， \(q_Q(s)>0\) 在某个区间 \([0,h_0]\) 上成立；令 \(\theta=h_0/r_0^2\)。在精确缩放 \[u_\lambda(t,x)=\lambda u(\lambda^2t,\lambda x), \quad r_\lambda=\lambda^{-1}r_0, \quad h_\lambda=\lambda^{-2}h_0,\] 以及截断函数、光滑化核、轨迹和热高度的协变缩放下，有 \[B_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^3B_Q,\quad d_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^3d_Q,\quad q_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^3q_Q,\quad Y_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda Y_Q.\] 因此 \(A_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^2A_Q\)，而 \(\mathcal V_{Q_\lambda,h_\lambda}=\mathcal V_{Q,h_0}\)。两个基准量均为正，故这恰好给出 (34)，其中 \(c_*=r_0^2A_Q/\mathcal V_{Q,h_0}>0\)。 ◻

定理 6.2 并不排除临界的 \(Cr^{-2}\) 迹不等式，也不排除带有附加过渡带项或依赖解的项的定理，或者关于如下柱体的结论：当 \(r\to0\) 时，它们与初始面之间的物理时间距离始终不小于某个与尺度无关的正常数。

7 正时间内真实发生的内部入壳

上述迹障碍尚未回答一个问题：正时间动力学能否阻止相应的壳观测量穿过零点而入壳。本节在精确的光滑 Navier–Stokes 流中构造这样的入壳。

令 \[\begin{equation} U(x)=(0,\cos x_1,\cos x_2), \qquad \Omega=\operatorname{curl}U=(-\sin x_2,0,-\sin x_1). \end{equation}\](35) 投影后的 Lamb 场为 \[\begin{equation} F_*=\mathbb P(U\times\Omega)=(0,0,\cos x_1\sin x_2). \end{equation}\](36) 它支撑于四个模态 \((\pm1,\pm1,0)\)，因此位于 \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert^2=2\) 上，而 \(U\) 位于 \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert^2=1\) 上。对环面作归一化平均可得 \[\begin{equation} \left\lVert \Omega\right\rVert_2^2=1, \quad \left\lVert F_*\right\rVert_2^2=\frac14, \quad \left\lVert \operatorname{curl}F_*\right\rVert_2^2=\frac12, \quad -\Delta F_*=2F_*. \end{equation}\](37)

设 \(E_*\) 为目标模态上的有限维实无散速度空间，\(P_*\) 为相应投影。固定整数 \(m\ge3\)。标准周期局部理论在一个共同时间区间上给出光滑的固定时间解映射 \(S_t\)，它将无散、零均值 \(H^m\) 空间中零点的一个邻域映到 \(H^m\)；参见 [4], [11]。其导数为 \[\begin{equation} DS_\tau(0)h=e^{\nu\tau\Delta}h. \end{equation}\](38)

定理 7.1 (光滑解的正向内部入壳). 对每个充分小的固定 \(\tau>0\)，存在 \(a_0>0\) 以及光滑映射 \(z:(-a_0,a_0)\to E_*\)，使得 \[\begin{equation} z(a)=-a^2\tau F_*+O(a^3), \end{equation}\](39) 并且，对每个 \(0<\left\lvert a\right\rvert<a_0\)，初始数据为 \(u_0^a=aU+z(a)\) 的解在 \([0,2\tau]\) 上光滑，并在严格内部时刻 \(t=\tau\) 发生一次简单的目标壳正向入壳。其首阶入壳系数为 \[\begin{equation} A_+(a)=\frac{a^2e^{-2\nu\tau}}4+O(a^3), \qquad A_-(a)=0. \end{equation}\](40) 对于每一个非负覆盖分割，其中至少一个单元也在该时刻发生简单的正向入壳。

证明。 定义 \[\Phi(a,z)=P_*S_\tau(aU+z).\] 在原点处， \(D_z\Phi(0,0)=e^{-2\nu\tau}I_{E_*}\)，这是一个可逆的有限维矩阵。通常的隐函数定理给出光滑的 \(z(a)\)，满足 \(\Phi(a,z(a))=0\)。目标壳上的二次 Duhamel 项为 \[e^{\nu(\tau-r)\Delta}P_* \mathbb P\bigl((e^{\nu r\Delta}U)\times \operatorname{curl}(e^{\nu r\Delta}U)\bigr) =e^{-2\nu\tau}F_*.\] 对 \(r\) 积分即得 (39)。

在时刻 \(\tau\)，目标壳上的速度和涡量均为零，而投影后的 Lamb 强迫满足 \[P_*L(u^a(\tau)) =a^2e^{-2\nu\tau}F_*+O(a^3)\ne0.\] 令 \(W_*=T_*\omega\) 且 \(C_*=\operatorname{curl}W_*\)。由滤波涡量方程和 \(W_*(\tau)=0\)，得到 \[C_{*,t}(\tau)=-\Delta P_*L(u^a(\tau)).\] 目标强迫无散、支撑于环带且非零。因此 \[\left\langle P_*L(u^a(\tau)),C_{*,t}(\tau)\right\rangle =\left\lVert \nabla P_*L(u^a(\tau))\right\rVert_2^2>0.\] 该零点是简单的，并产生正向入壳。由式 (37) 和低频壳涡量平方积分的首阶项可得 (40)。

对于非负分割 \((\chi_Q)_Q\)，整壳零点给出 \[\left\langle P_*L,C_{Q,t}\right\rangle =\int\chi_Q\left\lvert \operatorname{curl}P_*L\right\rvert^2\,\mathrm dx.\] 关于 \(Q\) 的总和为正，因此至少一个单元发生简单的正向入壳。 ◻

引理 7.2 (整壳的正向入壳都是简单的). 对未作空间局部化的非零环带壳，每一次有限阶正向入壳都是简单的。

证明。 在整壳零点处，\(C_j=\operatorname{curl}W_j=0\)。由于 \(W_j\) 无散、支撑于环带且为零均值，这意味着 \(W_j=0\)。因此 \(C_{j,t}=-\Delta F_j\)。如果零点的阶数大于一，就有 \(F_j=0\)，这与入壳配对的正性矛盾。最后， \(\left\langle F_j,C_{j,t}\right\rangle=\left\lVert \nabla F_j\right\rVert_2^2>0\)。 ◻

8 不存在一致的裸支付

对发生于 \(t_\beta\) 的有限阶入壳，写成 \[C_\beta(t_\beta+s)=c_\beta s^{m_\beta}+O_{L^2}(\left\lvert s\right\rvert^{m_\beta+1}), \qquad c_\beta\ne0,\] 并定义 \[\begin{equation} A_{\beta,+} =\frac{\left\langle F_{j(\beta)}(t_\beta),c_\beta/\left\lVert c_\beta\right\rVert_2\right\rangle_+^2}{Y(t_\beta)}, \qquad a_\beta=\kappa_{j(\beta)}^{-2}A_{\beta,+}. \end{equation}\](41) 这里 \(\kappa_j\) 是壳的标称频率。在定理 7.1 的基准构造中，目标模态的 Fourier 半径为 \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert=\sqrt2\)，壳的约定为 \(\kappa_*=1\)；经过整数伸缩 \(\lambda\) 后，相应的标称频率为 \(\kappa_{*,\lambda}=\lambda\)。按此约定，原子量 \(a_\beta\) 是尺度不变的。

对于定理 7.1 中的解族， \[\begin{equation} R(a):=\int_0^{2\tau} \frac{\left\lVert L^a(t)\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2}{Y^a(t)}\,\mathrm dt =\frac{a^2(1-e^{-4\nu\tau})}{16\nu}+O(a^3). \end{equation}\](42) 选取 \(a_\lambda=\lambda^{-2}\)，并施行整数周期缩放 \[\begin{equation} u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u^{a_\lambda}(\lambda x,\lambda^2t). \end{equation}\](43)

定理 8.1 (内部入壳的缩放不可能性). 沿 (43)，所构造的内部入壳与裸预算满足 \[\begin{align} a_{\beta,\lambda} &=\frac{e^{-2\nu\tau}}{4\lambda^4}+O(\lambda^{-6}), \\ R_\lambda &=\frac{1-e^{-4\nu\tau}}{16\nu\lambda^6} +O(\lambda^{-8}), \end{align}\](44) · (45) 因此 \[\begin{equation} \frac{a_{\beta,\lambda}}{R_\lambda} =\frac{2\nu}{\sinh(2\nu\tau)}\lambda^2+o(\lambda^2). \end{equation}\](46) 不存在沿该解族一致的常数 \(C\)，使内部入壳原子量之和有上界 \(C R_\lambda\)。

此外， \[\begin{equation} \left\lVert u_\lambda(0)\right\rVert_2^2=O(\lambda^{-2}), \qquad \left\lVert u_\lambda(0)\right\rVert_{\dot H^{1/2}}^2=O(\lambda^{-1}), \qquad \left\lVert \omega_\lambda(0)\right\rVert_2^2=1+o(1). \end{equation}\](47)

证明。 基准入壳由 (40) 给出。协变伸缩将它移到时刻 \(\tau/\lambda^2\)，并保持尺度指数为零的原子量；振幅的选择贡献因子 \(a_\lambda^2=\lambda^{-4}\)，从而证明 (44)。基准预算 (42) 获得额外的时间缩放因子 \(\lambda^{-2}\)，这给出 (45)。两者之比即为 (46)。

在归一化环面上，该伸缩使 \(L^2\)、 \(\dot H^{1/2}\) 和 \(\dot H^1\) 范数的平方分别乘以 \(\lambda^2\)、 \(\lambda^3\) 和 \(\lambda^4\)。将这些因子与 \(a_\lambda^2=\lambda^{-4}\) 合并，即得 (47)。 ◻

定理 8.1 只排除以 (42) 中的裸右端为界、且常数沿该协变解族一致的估计。 它不排除固定频率下的定理、依赖高阶 Sobolev 范数的常数，或加入原子量、变差、强 Lamb 项或物质导数项的估计。

9 零水平处的出向占据

裸预算与入壳原子量的尺度不匹配。零水平占据密度具有正确的尺度，并能精确表示该原子量。

设 \(I\) 为经典解存在区间内的一个紧区间，且端点不是零点。 对有限指标族 \(\Lambda\)，假设每个 \(C_\alpha\)、\(\alpha\in\Lambda\) 的每一个零点都孤立且为有限阶。令 \[\begin{equation} r_\alpha(t)=\left\lVert C_\alpha(t)\right\rVert_2, \qquad \xi_\alpha(t)=\frac{C_\alpha(t)}{r_\alpha(t)}\quad(r_\alpha>0), \end{equation}\](48) 以及 \[\begin{equation} q_\alpha(t) =\frac{\left\langle F_{j(\alpha)}(t),\xi_\alpha(t)\right\rangle_+^2}{Y(t)}. \end{equation}\](49) 选取 \(\rho\in C_c^\infty((0,1))\)、\(\rho\ge0\)，满足 \(\int_0^1\rho=1\)，并设 \(\rho_\delta(s)=\delta^{-1}\rho(s/\delta)\)。

定理 9.1 (出向余面积恒等式). 在上述假设下， \[\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} &\sum_{\alpha\in\Lambda} \sum_{\substack{t_*\in I\\C_\alpha(t_*)=0}} \kappa_{j(\alpha)}^{-2}A_{\alpha,+}(t_*)\\ &\quad=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0} \sum_{\alpha\in\Lambda}\kappa_{j(\alpha)}^{-2} \int_Iq_\alpha(t)\rho_\delta(r_\alpha(t)) \left(r_{\alpha,t}(t)\right)_{+}\,\mathrm dt. \end{aligned} \end{equation}\](50) 该恒等式同时包括奇数阶穿越和偶数阶接触。

证明。 在一个 \(m\) 阶零点附近， \[C(t_*+s)=cs^m+O_{L^2}(\left\lvert s\right\rvert^{m+1}), \quad r(t_*+s)=\left\lVert c\right\rVert_2s^m(1+O(s)), \quad \xi(t_*+s)\longrightarrow c/\left\lVert c\right\rVert_2\] 这些展开与极限从右侧取值。因子 \(\left(r_t\right)_{+}\) 去除了左侧趋向零点的部分。 在右侧，作代换 \(y=r(t)\) 得 \[\int q(t)\rho_\delta(r(t))\left(r_t(t)\right)_{+}\,\mathrm dt \longrightarrow q(t_*+)=A_+(t_*).\] 对于偶数阶接触，论证不变。对有限个互不相交的零点邻域求和，即得 (50)。 ◻

若 \(C_t-\nu\Delta C=G\)，则在所有 \(r>0\) 的位置， \[\begin{equation} r_t=\left\langle \xi,G\right\rangle-\nu\frac{\left\lVert \nabla C\right\rVert_2^2}{r}. \end{equation}\](51) (50) 中的密度在时间积分之前具有尺度指数二，因而积分后的总尺度指数为零。 这只在表示式层面修复了裸预算的量纲不匹配。使用界 \(\left(r_t\right)_{+}\le\left\lVert G\right\rVert_2\) 后，仍留下 \(\rho_\delta(r)\sim\delta^{-1}\)，通常的 \(L_t^pG\) 界并不能推出一致的零水平占据估计。

作为比较，固定的光滑无散波包 \(\psi\) 具有可由 Leray 预算控制的振幅加权的变差界。若 \(b_\psi(t)=\left\langle u(t),\psi\right\rangle\)，则在 \(I=[s,t]\) 上， \[\begin{equation} V_I^+(b_\psi) \le\nu\left\lVert \nabla\psi\right\rVert_2(\left\lvert I\right\rvert D_I)^{1/2} +\left\lVert \nabla\psi\right\rVert_\infty E_I\left\lvert I\right\rvert, \end{equation}\](52) 其中 \(E_I=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_I\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2^2\)， \(D_I=\int_I\left\lVert \nabla u\right\rVert_2^2\)。该估计控制的是上述固定波包系数达到固定振幅的进入事件，而不能控制任意浅的过零事件。

10 讨论与结论的精确边界

这些结果明确区分了以下几个层次。

  1. 投影 Lamb 场的壳注入具有完整的带符号恒等式，以及一个尺度临界的条件延拓系数。

  2. Leray 能量无条件控制归一化的垂直热体积分；整体情形及有界重叠的局部化情形均成立。

  3. 底部迹并非由该体积分直接推出。精确的全局光滑解达到完整的 \(K^2\) 迹代价，而协变内部柱体达到 \(r^{-2}\) 的尺度。

  4. 光滑 Navier–Stokes 动力学中确实会发生正时间内部入壳。裸的归一化 \(\dot H^{-1}\)-Lamb 时间积分不能一致支付其尺度不变的原子量。

  5. 出向占据给出精确且尺度匹配的表示，但其零水平质量总和的估计仍然开放。

本文没有证明每个 Leray–Hopf 解都满足 \(A_{\mathrm{sb},+}\in L^1_t\)，没有证明占据或复现上界，也没有将有限阶入壳的估计延伸到越过未知奇异时刻的情形。 本文既未证明全局正则性，也未构造有限时间破裂。余下的问题并不是寻找另一个代数分解，而是精确的 Navier–Stokes 耦合能否提供尺度指数为零的占据量、jet 量或带振幅阈值的控制项，使其总和可由先验预算控制。

Abstract

For the three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes equations, the vorticity stretching and transport terms can be compressed into the curl of the solenoidal Lamb vector \(L=\mathbb P(u\times\omega)\). This paper studies a signed Littlewood–Paley shell injection built from that representation. The associated vertical heat extension has an unconditional bulk estimate: after normalization by enstrophy, its full heat-height integral belongs to \(L^1\) in physical time for every periodic Leray–Hopf solution on a finite interval. The estimate persists under bounded-overlap spatial localization and yields a conditional local continuation criterion.

The bulk estimate does not recover the bottom trace. A fixed smooth radial tight frame and an explicit global smooth 2D3C Navier–Stokes family along dyadic frequencies \(K=2^j\) give an exact bottom-to-bulk factor \(2K^2\). The same family shows that matched partitions do not hide the loss, while a covariant interior-cylinder construction rules out every geometry-only factor \(o(r^{-2})\). A second construction uses the finite-dimensional implicit-function theorem for the exact Navier–Stokes flow to prescribe a positive-time full-shell zero with nonzero nonlinear forcing. After an amplitude-frequency scaling, its scale-invariant entry atom is of order \(\lambda^{-4}\), whereas the bare normalized Leray–Lamb time budget is of order \(\lambda^{-6}\). Thus no uniform bare payment of such internal entries is possible. An outgoing coarea identity gives an exact scale-matched representation of every finite-order entry, but its zero-level occupation density is not controlled here. None of the results proves global regularity or constructs a singularity. Computer-assisted finite Fourier, spectral-reconstruction, and Galerkin calculations are used as audits and corroboration; no analytic theorem is inferred from floating-point output.

1 Introduction

Let \(u\) be a divergence-free velocity field and \(\omega=\operatorname{curl}u\) its vorticity. The obstruction to a direct global enstrophy estimate in three dimensions is the vortex-stretching work \(\int S:\omega\otimes\omega\), where \(S=(\nabla u+\nabla u^{T})/2\). Classical regularity theory controls this work by adding geometric coherence, critical-space smallness, or an integrable strong norm; representative examples include [9], [2], and [7]. The aim here is narrower. I examine one signed frequency-localized form of the enstrophy injection and determine exactly which part follows from the Leray energy inequality and which part does not.

The starting identity is classical. Modulo a Bernoulli gradient, the Navier–Stokes nonlinearity is the solenoidal Lamb vector \[L=\mathbb P(u\times\omega)=\partial_tu-\nu\Delta u.\] Solenoidal Lamb decompositions have appeared in fluid mechanics and in mathematical formulations of the curl operator [10], [5], [8]. The contribution of this paper is not that decomposition by itself. It is the specific signed shell quotient below, its energy-level heat packing, and sharp smooth-solution obstructions to recovering its bottom trace.

1.1 Main results

Fix a real-even scalar tight Fourier frame \((T_j)_j\). For heat height \(s\ge0\), set \[W_{j,s}=e^{s\Delta}T_j\omega, \qquad F_{j,s}=e^{s\Delta}T_jL, \qquad D_{j,s}=\left\lVert \nabla W_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2,\] and define the complete shell injection \[b_{j,s}=\left\langle \operatorname{curl}W_{j,s},F_{j,s}\right\rangle.\] The positive shell square function is \[\Theta_s^2 =\sum_{D_{j,s}>0}\frac{\left(b_{j,s}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,s}}.\] The first theorem, proved in Sections 3–4, is \[\begin{equation} \int_0^\infty\Theta_s^2\,\mathrm ds \le \frac12\left\lVert L\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2 \le \frac12\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^4. \end{equation}\](1) If \(Y(t)=\left\lVert \omega(t)\right\rVert_2^2\) and \[\mathcal V(t)=Y(t)^{-1}\int_0^\infty\Theta_s(t)^2\,\mathrm ds,\] with the value zero when \(Y=0\), then every periodic Leray–Hopf solution satisfies \[\begin{equation} \int_0^T\mathcal V(t)\,\mathrm dt \le \frac{C_S^3}{2\sqrt{2\nu}}\left\lVert u_0\right\rVert_2^2\sqrt T. \end{equation}\](2) This is an unconditional statement at the energy level. Bounded-overlap localization gives the corresponding estimate with an overlap constant and also gives a conditional local continuation criterion.

The bottom value \[A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(t)=\Theta_0(t)^2/Y(t)\] is different. Section 6 constructs one fixed dyadically covariant radial frame and a global smooth 2D3C family for which, at every dyadic frequency \(K=2^j\), \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(0)=2K^2\mathcal V(0). \end{equation}\](3) The identity survives every nonzero nonnegative smooth cutoff. At a parabolic heat height \(h=\theta K^{-2}\), the height average is a fixed positive fraction of the bottom value. Matched partitions retain the same scale law. A separate whole-space scaling argument places the obstruction on strictly interior admissible skewed cylinders and rules out a pure geometry-only bottom-from-bulk factor \(o(r^{-2})\). The critical \(Cr^{-2}\) factor is saturated, not rejected.

Section 7 addresses a temporal objection to a static trace example. Starting from \[U=(0,\cos x_1,\cos x_2),\] the exact local Navier–Stokes flow is perturbed in the target shell so that the entire shell vanishes at a prescribed positive time \(\tau\), while its nonlinear forcing remains nonzero. The resulting zero is a genuine simple internal positive entry. Choosing a base amplitude \(a_\lambda=\lambda^{-2}\) and then applying integer Navier–Stokes dilation gives \[\begin{equation} \frac{\text{internal entry atom}} {\displaystyle\int \left\lVert L_\lambda\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2/Y_\lambda\,\mathrm dt} =\frac{2\nu}{\sinh(2\nu\tau)}\lambda^2+o(\lambda^2). \end{equation}\](4) The initial kinetic energy and critical \(\dot H^{1/2}\) norm tend to zero, while the initial enstrophy remains bounded. Thus the failure is not caused by a large energy ledger or an initial-time observation face.

The final positive statement is an exact outgoing-coarea representation of all isolated finite-order entries. Its density has the correct scale, but it concentrates at the distinguished zero level. The Leray inequality does not provide the required uniform occupation estimate in the present argument.

1.2 Relation to nearby work

The heat-extension viewpoint is established in critical Navier–Stokes theory, most notably in the \(BMO^{-1}\) framework of [7]. The present square function is solution-dependent, signed before squaring, and normalized by enstrophy; it is not the Koch–Tataru norm. Rigorous skewed-cylinder geometry is available from [13] and has been used for derivative estimates by [12]. Those covering theorems do not by themselves recover a boundary value in the independent heat-height variable. Recent caloric trace estimates recover boundary regularity from stronger interior derivative norms [1], rather than from the zero-order bulk in (1).

The closest filtered-vorticity comparison located in the preparation of this draft is [14]. That work controls a positive near-field stretching part and obtains conditional unweighted closure under additional far-field, commutator, and localization summability. The observable and the closure hypotheses differ from those used here. Exact commutator identities also do not imply defect vanishing without an additional compactness or regularity input; this distinction is familiar from [3].

The paper makes no priority or literature-exhaustion claim. Its precise scope is summarized in Section 10.

2 Setting and notation

The periodic part of the paper is set on \(\mathbb T^3=(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)^3\) with normalized spatial measure. The velocity has zero mean and satisfies \[\begin{equation} \partial_tu+u\cdot\nabla u+\nabla p=\nu\Delta u, \qquad \nabla\cdot u=0, \qquad \omega=\operatorname{curl}u. \end{equation}\](5) All Fourier multipliers are set to zero on the zero mode. The Leray projection is denoted by \(\mathbb P\).

A real-even scalar tight frame is a countable family of scalar Fourier multipliers \((T_j)_j\), with symbols \(m_j(k)\), such that \[\begin{equation} m_j(-k)=m_j(k)\in\mathbb R, \qquad \sum_j\left\lvert m_j(k)\right\rvert^2=1 \quad (k\ne0). \end{equation}\](6) For finite frames all sums are literal. Infinite sums are interpreted by finite truncation followed by monotone or Fatou convergence whenever the summands are nonnegative.

At energy regularity, expressions containing \(e^{s\Delta}L\) as an \(L^2\) field are understood for \(s>0\). Their heat-height integrals are defined by finite shell and positive-height truncation followed by Tonelli and Fatou. At \(s=0\), the shell coefficients are used only when \(L\in L^2\); this holds, in particular, at almost every \(H^2\) time of a periodic \(H^1\) strong solution. Changing a coefficient on the remaining null set does not affect any continuation hypothesis below.

The rotational form of (5) gives \[\begin{equation} u\times\omega=L+\nabla B, \qquad B=p+\frac12\left\lvert u\right\rvert^2, \qquad L=\mathbb P(u\times\omega) =\partial_tu-\nu\Delta u =-\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(u\otimes u). \end{equation}\](7) Taking curl yields \[\begin{equation} \partial_t\omega-\nu\Delta\omega =\operatorname{curl}L=S\omega-u\cdot\nabla\omega. \end{equation}\](8)

For \(s\ge0\), define \[\begin{equation} A_{j,s}=e^{s\Delta}T_j, \quad W_{j,s}=A_{j,s}\omega, \quad F_{j,s}=A_{j,s}L, \quad D_{j,s}=\left\lVert \nabla W_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2. \end{equation}\](9) Since \(W_{j,s}\) is divergence free, \(D_{j,s}=\left\lVert \operatorname{curl}W_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2\). The heat-extended vorticity equation is \[\begin{equation} (\partial_t-\nu\partial_s)W_{j,s}=\operatorname{curl}F_{j,s}. \end{equation}\](10)

3 A signed shell continuation coefficient

At heat height \(s\), define the complete shell work \[\begin{equation} b_{j,s}=\left\langle \operatorname{curl}W_{j,s},F_{j,s}\right\rangle. \end{equation}\](11) This includes stretching and the transport-filter commutator. Indeed, \[\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} b_{j,s} &=\left\langle W_{j,s},A_{j,s}(S\omega-u\cdot\nabla\omega)\right\rangle\\ &=\left\langle W_{j,s},A_{j,s}(S\omega)+[u\cdot\nabla,A_{j,s}]\omega\right\rangle. \end{aligned} \end{equation}\](12) The advective pairing with \(W_{j,s}\) vanishes globally. Pressure is the gradient complement in (7) and is therefore not a third independent vorticity work term.

At \(s=0\), write \[\begin{equation} \Theta_0^2=\sum_{D_{j,0}>0}\frac{\left(b_{j,0}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,0}}, \qquad A_{\mathrm{sb},+}=\frac{\Theta_0^2}{Y}, \qquad Y=\left\lVert \omega\right\rVert_2^2, \end{equation}\](13) with value zero when \(Y=0\).

Proposition 3.1 (Conditional shell continuation). Let \(u\) be a maximal periodic \(H^1\) strong solution on \([0,T_*)\). If \[\begin{equation} \int_0^{T_*}A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(t)\,\mathrm dt<\infty, \end{equation}\](14) then \(u\) extends beyond \(T_*\).

Proof. Set \(D=\left\lVert \nabla\omega\right\rVert_2^2\) and \(\mathfrak P=\int S:\omega\otimes\omega\). Tightness gives \(\sum_jD_{j,0}=D\) and \(\sum_jb_{j,0}=\mathfrak P\). Positive-part subadditivity and weighted Cauchy imply \[\mathfrak P_+ \le \sqrt D\,\Theta_0 \le \frac\nu2D+\frac1{2\nu}A_{\mathrm{sb},+}Y.\] The enstrophy identity therefore yields \[Y'+\nu D\le\nu^{-1}A_{\mathrm{sb},+}Y.\] Gronwall and (14) bound \(Y\) on \([0,T_*)\) and place \(D\) in \(L^1(0,T_*)\). The standard strong-solution continuation theorem then extends the solution; see, for example, [4], [6], [11]. ◻

Remark 3.2.. Proposition 3.1 is a scale-critical conditional reduction, not an unconditional regularity criterion. The rest of the paper tests whether (14) follows from the energy-controlled heat extension.

4 Unconditional projected-Lamb heat packing

For \(s\ge0\), set \[\begin{equation} \Theta_s^2 =\sum_{D_{j,s}>0}\frac{\left(b_{j,s}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,s}}. \end{equation}\](15)

Theorem 4.1 (Global heat-bulk estimate). At every time at which \(u\in H^1(\mathbb T^3)\), \[\begin{equation} \Theta_s^2\le\left\lVert e^{s\Delta}L\right\rVert_2^2\quad(s>0), \qquad \int_0^\infty\Theta_s^2\,\mathrm ds \le\frac12\left\lVert L\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2 \le\frac12\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^4. \end{equation}\](16) If \(L\in L^2\), the pointwise inequality also holds at \(s=0\). For a periodic Leray–Hopf solution and every finite \(T\), the normalized bulk \[\begin{equation} \mathcal V(t)=\frac1{Y(t)}\int_0^\infty\Theta_s(t)^2\,\mathrm ds \end{equation}\](17) satisfies (2).

Proof. Shellwise Cauchy and (11) give \[\frac{\left(b_{j,s}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,s}} \le\left\lVert F_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2.\] Summing and using (6) proves the first inequality in (16). Since \(L\) has zero mean, \[\int_0^\infty\left\lVert e^{s\Delta}L\right\rVert_2^2\,\mathrm ds =\frac12\left\lVert (-\Delta)^{-1/2}L\right\rVert_2^2.\] The multiplier \((-\Delta)^{-1/2}\mathbb P\nabla\cdot\) has \(L^2\) operator norm at most one from matrix fields with the Frobenius norm to vector fields. Equation (7) therefore gives \[\left\lVert L\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}} \le\left\lVert u\otimes u\right\rVert_2=\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^2.\] This proves (16).

The three-dimensional interpolation and Sobolev inequalities give \[\left\lVert u\right\rVert_4^4 \le\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2\left\lVert u\right\rVert_6^3 \le C_S^3\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2Y^{3/2}.\] Hence \[\mathcal V(t)\le\frac{C_S^3}{2}\left\lVert u(t)\right\rVert_2Y(t)^{1/2}.\] The Leray inequality implies \[\left\lVert u(t)\right\rVert_2\le\left\lVert u_0\right\rVert_2, \qquad \nu\int_0^TY(t)\,\mathrm dt\le\frac12\left\lVert u_0\right\rVert_2^2.\] Cauchy in time proves (2).

For weak solutions, the argument is first applied to finite shell and heat-height truncations at almost every \(H^1\) time. Tonelli and Fatou then give the stated interpretation. No solution-dependent test function is inserted into the weak formulation. ◻

The factorization of the unresolved trace is now explicit. When the denominator is positive, define \[\begin{equation} \Lambda_L^2(t)= \frac{\Theta_0(t)^2}{\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\Theta_s(t)^2\,\mathrm ds}; \end{equation}\](18) otherwise set \(\Lambda_L=0\). Then \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{sb},+}=\Lambda_L^2\mathcal V. \end{equation}\](19) Theorem 4.1 controls \(\mathcal V\), but does not control \(\Lambda_L^2\).

5 Bounded-overlap localization

Let \((\chi_{j,Q})_Q\) be a nonnegative smooth partition of unity at the matched radius \(r_j\simeq K_j^{-1}\), where \(T_j\) is supported in \(cK_j\le\left\lvert k\right\rvert\le CK_j\). Assume uniform derivative bounds and overlap at most \(N\). Define \[\begin{equation} B_{j,Q}(t,s)=\left\langle F_{j,s},\operatorname{curl}(\chi_{j,Q}W_{j,s})\right\rangle, \qquad d_{j,Q}(t,s)=\left\lVert \operatorname{curl}(\chi_{j,Q}W_{j,s})\right\rVert_2^2, \end{equation}\](20) and set \[\begin{equation} q_{j,Q}(t,s)= \begin{cases} \left(B_{j,Q}(t,s)\right)_{+}^2/d_{j,Q}(t,s),&d_{j,Q}>0,\\ 0,&d_{j,Q}=0. \end{cases} \end{equation}\](21) The curl in (20) retains the cutoff collar exactly: \[B_{j,Q} =\int\chi_{j,Q}F_{j,s}\cdot\operatorname{curl}W_{j,s} +\int F_{j,s}\cdot(\nabla\chi_{j,Q}\times W_{j,s}).\]

Proposition 5.1 (Local packing and criterion). Under the preceding assumptions, \[\begin{equation} \sum_{j,Q}q_{j,Q}(t,s) \le N\left\lVert e^{s\Delta}L(t)\right\rVert_2^2. \end{equation}\](22) For every \(\alpha>0\), \[\begin{equation} \int_0^\infty s^{\alpha-1}\sum_{j,Q}q_{j,Q}(t,s)\,\mathrm ds \le\frac{N\Gamma(\alpha)}{2^\alpha} \left\lVert (-\Delta)^{-\alpha/2}L(t)\right\rVert_2^2. \end{equation}\](23) At \(\alpha=1\), the normalized left side belongs to \(L^1(0,T)\) with the right side of (2) multiplied by \(N\).

Moreover, if \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{loc},+}(t) =\frac1{Y(t)}\sum_{j,Q}q_{j,Q}(t,0) \end{equation}\](24) is integrable on the maximal strong interval, then the solution continues.

Proof. Cauchy in (20) gives \[q_{j,Q}\le \left\lVert 1_{\operatorname{supp}\chi_{j,Q}}F_{j,s}\right\rVert_2^2.\] Bounded overlap and frame tightness prove (22). Spectral integration gives (23); the case \(\alpha=1\) then follows as in Theorem 4.1.

For the criterion, the matched annular Bernstein and collar bounds imply \[\sum_Qd_{j,Q}\le C_{\chi}\,D_{j,0}.\] Since \(\sum_QB_{j,Q}=b_{j,0}\), weighted Cauchy gives \[\frac{\left(b_{j,0}\right)_{+}^2}{D_{j,0}} \le C_{\chi}\sum_Qq_{j,Q}(t,0).\] Thus \(A_{\mathrm{sb},+}\le C_{\chi}A_{\mathrm{loc},+}\), and Proposition 3.1 applies. ◻

Remark 5.2.. The local criterion is not claimed to be weaker than a standard published criterion. Direct Cauchy gives \(A_{\mathrm{loc},+}\le N\left\lVert L\right\rVert_2^2/Y \le N\left\lVert u\right\rVert_\infty^2\), so the Serrin endpoint \(u\in L_t^2L_x^\infty\) implies it.

6 Exact critical trace loss

The estimates above integrate in the heat-height variable. This section shows that the missing boundary trace costs two frequency powers even for global smooth solutions.

For \(\sigma\in\{-1,1\}\), \(a>0\), and dyadic integer \(K=2^j\), \(j\ge0\), define \[\begin{equation} u_{\sigma,a,K}(x)=aK \left(0,-2\cos(Kx_1), 2\sigma\sin(Kx_1+Kx_2)-2\cos(Kx_2)\right). \end{equation}\](25) This field is real, zero mean, divergence free, and independent of \(x_3\). Its horizontal velocity is a decaying two-dimensional shear and its third component solves a linear advection-diffusion equation driven by that shear. It therefore generates a global smooth three-dimensional periodic solution.

Choose one fixed smooth dyadically covariant radial tight frame that first places the radii \(K\) and \(\sqrt2K\) in one parent and then splits them exactly into low and high children. Such a frame is obtained from a smooth square partition in \(\log_2\left\lvert \xi\right\rvert\) with a flat top, followed by a fixed sine-cosine refinement. The frame is fixed before \(K\) is chosen.

For \(\sigma=-1\), the only positive child is the low-radius block. Direct Fourier calculation gives \[\begin{equation} b_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)=2a^3K^6e^{-2K^2s}, \quad D_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)=4a^2K^6e^{-2K^2s}, \quad q_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)=a^4K^6e^{-2K^2s}. \end{equation}\](26)

Theorem 6.1 (Sharp global and localized trace cost). For the family (25) and the fixed frame above, \[\begin{equation} q_{\mathrm{lo}}(0)=2K^2\int_0^\infty q_{\mathrm{lo}}(s)\,\mathrm ds, \end{equation}\](27) and, with \(Y=8a^2K^4\), \[\begin{equation} A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(0)=\frac{a^2K^2}{8}, \qquad \mathcal V(0)=\frac{a^2}{16}, \qquad A_{\mathrm{sb},+}(0)=2K^2\mathcal V(0). \end{equation}\](28)

Let \(\phi\ge0\) be any nonzero smooth cutoff fixed before the data, and let \(q_\phi\) be defined by the complete localized work in (20). Then for every \(h>0\), \[\begin{equation} q_\phi(0)=\frac{2K^2}{1-e^{-2K^2h}} \int_0^h q_\phi(s)\,\mathrm ds. \end{equation}\](29) In particular, at \(h=\theta K^{-2}\), the heat-height average is a fixed positive fraction of the bottom value.

Proof. Equation (27) follows by integrating the last formula in (26); division by \(Y\) gives (28).

For the local statement, the low block is \[W_s=2aK^2e^{-K^2s}(\sin(Kx_2),0,\sin(Kx_1)), \quad F_s=-2a^2K^3e^{-K^2s}(0,0,\cos(Kx_2)).\] The complete localized work, including the cutoff collar, satisfies \[B_\phi(s)=\left\langle F_s,\operatorname{curl}(\phi W_s)\right\rangle =4a^3K^6e^{-2K^2s} \int\phi(x)\sin^2(Kx_2)\,\mathrm dx>0.\] Both \(F_s\) and \(W_s\) lie on \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert=K\), so \(q_\phi(s)=e^{-2K^2s}q_\phi(0)\). Integration over \([0,h]\) proves (29). ◻

At the matched height, \[\begin{equation} \frac{h^{-1}\int_0^h q_\phi(s)\,\mathrm ds}{q_\phi(0)} =\frac{1-e^{-2\theta}}{2\theta}. \end{equation}\](30) The factor is positive and independent of \(K\).

Matched partitions do not remove the scale. If \((\phi_Q)_Q\) is a standard nonnegative partition at radius \(r=\rho/K\) with overlap \(N\) and uniform square-derivative bounds, then \[\begin{equation} \frac{a^4K^6e^{-2K^2s}} {2(C_0+C_1/\rho^2)} \le\sum_Qq_Q(s) \le2Na^4K^6e^{-2K^2s}. \end{equation}\](31) Choosing \(a=K^{-1}\) keeps \(\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2^2=6\). The normalized aggregate bottom remains between positive constants, while its heat bulk is \(O(K^{-2})\).

6.1 Interior cylinders

The exact family above is evaluated at an initial trace. The same critical power occurs on strictly interior cylinders, with a narrower conclusion.

For this subsection only, take the covariant one-block tight frame \(T_0=I\). At the center time \(t_Q\) of a skewed cylinder \(Q\), let \(\chi_Q\) be its covariantly transported nonnegative spatial cutoff and set \[W_s=e^{s\Delta}\omega(t_Q),\qquad F_s=e^{s\Delta}L(t_Q).\] Define \[\begin{equation} B_Q(s)=\left\langle F_s,\operatorname{curl}(\chi_QW_s)\right\rangle,\qquad d_Q(s)=\left\lVert \operatorname{curl}(\chi_QW_s)\right\rVert_2^2, \end{equation}\](32) \[q_Q(s)= \begin{cases} \left(B_Q(s)\right)_{+}^2/d_Q(s),&d_Q(s)>0,\\ 0,&d_Q(s)=0. \end{cases}\] With \(Y_Q=\left\lVert \omega(t_Q)\right\rVert_2^2\), put \[\begin{equation} A_Q=\frac{q_Q(0)}{Y_Q},\qquad \mathcal V_{Q,h}=\frac1{Y_Q}\int_0^h q_Q(s)\,\mathrm ds. \end{equation}\](33)

Theorem 6.2 (Geometry-only subcritical trace no-go). There is a scale-covariant family of local-in-time smooth whole-space Navier–Stokes solutions, each defined on an interval containing a strictly interior admissible skewed cylinder \(Q_r\), for which \[\begin{equation} \frac{A_{Q_r}}{\mathcal V_{Q_r,\theta r^2}}=c_*r^{-2}, \qquad c_*>0. \end{equation}\](34) Consequently, an estimate \[A_Q\le c(r)\mathcal V_{Q,\theta r^2}\] that is required for all smooth solutions and all cylinders in this covariant one-block class, with constants depending only on the uniform geometry and admissibility parameters, cannot have \(c(r)=o(r^{-2})\).

Proof. Let \[u_{\mathrm{aff}}=(x_1,-x_2-x_3,0), \qquad \omega_{\mathrm{aff}}=(1,0,0).\] Put \(A_0=-\frac13x\times u_{\mathrm{aff}}\), and choose \(\eta\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^3)\) equal to one on a ball. Then \(u_0=\operatorname{curl}(\eta A_0)\) is smooth, compactly supported, and divergence free. Because \(u_{\mathrm{aff}}\) is linear, homogeneous, and divergence free, \(\operatorname{curl}A_0=u_{\mathrm{aff}}\), so \(u_0=u_{\mathrm{aff}}\) on the smaller affine core.

In the affine core, \(\operatorname{curl}L=(\omega\cdot\nabla)u-(u\cdot\nabla)\omega=(1,0,0)\). Choose a nonnegative cutoff \(\chi\) supported in the core with \(\nabla\chi\times\omega_{\mathrm{aff}}\not\equiv0\). At the initial trace, \[\int\chi\,\omega_{\mathrm{aff}}\cdot\operatorname{curl}L\,\mathrm dx>0, \qquad \left\lVert \operatorname{curl}(\chi\omega_{\mathrm{aff}})\right\rVert_2>0.\] Multiplying the datum by a sufficiently small fixed positive amplitude preserves both inequalities. Smooth local existence and continuity preserve them at a small positive center time. Sufficiently small skewed cylinders are admissible in the sense of [13], because their admissibility quantity is bounded by \(r^2\sup\left\lVert \nabla u\right\rVert_\infty\).

Fix one such base cylinder with radius \(r_0\). Continuity in \(s\) gives \(q_Q(s)>0\) on some interval \([0,h_0]\); set \(\theta=h_0/r_0^2\). Under the exact scaling \[u_\lambda(t,x)=\lambda u(\lambda^2t,\lambda x), \quad r_\lambda=\lambda^{-1}r_0, \quad h_\lambda=\lambda^{-2}h_0,\] and covariant scaling of the cutoff, mollifier, trajectory, and heat height, \[B_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^3B_Q,\quad d_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^3d_Q,\quad q_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^3q_Q,\quad Y_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda Y_Q.\] Consequently \(A_{Q_\lambda}=\lambda^2A_Q\), while \(\mathcal V_{Q_\lambda,h_\lambda}=\mathcal V_{Q,h_0}\). Since both base quantities are positive, this is exactly (34), with \(c_*=r_0^2A_Q/\mathcal V_{Q,h_0}>0\). ◻

Theorem 6.2 does not reject a critical \(Cr^{-2}\) trace inequality, a theorem with additional collar or solution-dependent terms, or a statement on cylinders that stay a fixed positive physical time away from the initial face as \(r\to0\).

7 A genuine positive-time internal entry

The trace obstructions above leave open whether positive-time dynamics could prevent the relevant shell observable from entering through zero. This section constructs such an entry for the exact smooth Navier–Stokes flow.

Let \[\begin{equation} U(x)=(0,\cos x_1,\cos x_2), \qquad \Omega=\operatorname{curl}U=(-\sin x_2,0,-\sin x_1). \end{equation}\](35) The projected Lamb field is \[\begin{equation} F_*=\mathbb P(U\times\Omega)=(0,0,\cos x_1\sin x_2). \end{equation}\](36) It is supported on the four modes \((\pm1,\pm1,0)\), hence on \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert^2=2\), while \(U\) lies on \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert^2=1\). Normalized torus averages give \[\begin{equation} \left\lVert \Omega\right\rVert_2^2=1, \quad \left\lVert F_*\right\rVert_2^2=\frac14, \quad \left\lVert \operatorname{curl}F_*\right\rVert_2^2=\frac12, \quad -\Delta F_*=2F_*. \end{equation}\](37)

Let \(E_*\) be the finite-dimensional real divergence-free velocity space on the target modes and let \(P_*\) be the corresponding projection. Fix an integer \(m\ge3\). Standard periodic local theory gives a smooth fixed-time solution map \(S_t\) from a neighborhood of zero in the divergence-free, mean-zero \(H^m\) space to \(H^m\), on a common interval; see [4], [11]. Its derivative is \[\begin{equation} DS_\tau(0)h=e^{\nu\tau\Delta}h. \end{equation}\](38)

Theorem 7.1 (Smooth internal positive entry). For every sufficiently small fixed \(\tau>0\), there is \(a_0>0\) and a smooth map \(z:(-a_0,a_0)\to E_*\) such that \[\begin{equation} z(a)=-a^2\tau F_*+O(a^3), \end{equation}\](39) and, for every \(0<\left\lvert a\right\rvert<a_0\), the solution with initial datum \(u_0^a=aU+z(a)\) is smooth on \([0,2\tau]\) and has a simple positive target-shell entry at the strictly internal time \(t=\tau\). Its leading entry coefficient is \[\begin{equation} A_+(a)=\frac{a^2e^{-2\nu\tau}}4+O(a^3), \qquad A_-(a)=0. \end{equation}\](40) At least one cell in every nonnegative covering partition also has a simple positive entry at that time.

Proof. Define \[\Phi(a,z)=P_*S_\tau(aU+z).\] At the origin, \(D_z\Phi(0,0)=e^{-2\nu\tau}I_{E_*}\), an invertible finite matrix. The ordinary implicit-function theorem gives a smooth \(z(a)\) with \(\Phi(a,z(a))=0\). The quadratic Duhamel term on the target shell is \[e^{\nu(\tau-r)\Delta}P_* \mathbb P\bigl((e^{\nu r\Delta}U)\times \operatorname{curl}(e^{\nu r\Delta}U)\bigr) =e^{-2\nu\tau}F_*.\] Integration in \(r\) gives (39).

At time \(\tau\), the target-shell velocity and vorticity vanish, whereas the projected Lamb forcing satisfies \[P_*L(u^a(\tau)) =a^2e^{-2\nu\tau}F_*+O(a^3)\ne0.\] Let \(W_*=T_*\omega\) and \(C_*=\operatorname{curl}W_*\). From the filtered vorticity equation and \(W_*(\tau)=0\), \[C_{*,t}(\tau)=-\Delta P_*L(u^a(\tau)).\] The target forcing is divergence free, annular, and nonzero. Therefore \[\left\langle P_*L(u^a(\tau)),C_{*,t}(\tau)\right\rangle =\left\lVert \nabla P_*L(u^a(\tau))\right\rVert_2^2>0.\] The zero is simple and enters from the positive side. Equations (37) and the leading low-shell enstrophy give (40).

For a nonnegative partition \((\chi_Q)_Q\), the full-shell root gives \[\left\langle P_*L,C_{Q,t}\right\rangle =\int\chi_Q\left\lvert \operatorname{curl}P_*L\right\rvert^2\,\mathrm dx.\] The sum over \(Q\) is positive, so at least one cell has a positive simple entry. ◻

Lemma 7.2 (Global positive entries are simple). Every finite-order positive entry of a global nonzero annular shell is simple.

Proof. At a global-shell zero, \(C_j=\operatorname{curl}W_j=0\). Since \(W_j\) is divergence free, annular, and zero mean, this implies \(W_j=0\). Hence \(C_{j,t}=-\Delta F_j\). If the zero had order greater than one, then \(F_j=0\), contradicting positivity of the entry pairing. Finally, \(\left\langle F_j,C_{j,t}\right\rangle=\left\lVert \nabla F_j\right\rVert_2^2>0\). ◻

8 No uniform bare payment

For a finite-order entry at \(t_\beta\), write \[C_\beta(t_\beta+s)=c_\beta s^{m_\beta}+O_{L^2}(\left\lvert s\right\rvert^{m_\beta+1}), \qquad c_\beta\ne0,\] and define \[\begin{equation} A_{\beta,+} =\frac{\left\langle F_{j(\beta)}(t_\beta),c_\beta/\left\lVert c_\beta\right\rVert_2\right\rangle_+^2}{Y(t_\beta)}, \qquad a_\beta=\kappa_{j(\beta)}^{-2}A_{\beta,+}. \end{equation}\](41) Here \(\kappa_j\) is the nominal shell frequency. In the base construction of Theorem 7.1, the target modes have Fourier radius \(\left\lvert k\right\rvert=\sqrt2\), and the shell convention is \(\kappa_*=1\); after the integer dilation \(\lambda\), the corresponding nominal frequency is \(\kappa_{*,\lambda}=\lambda\). With this convention the atom \(a_\beta\) is scale invariant.

For the family in Theorem 7.1, \[\begin{equation} R(a):=\int_0^{2\tau} \frac{\left\lVert L^a(t)\right\rVert_{\dot H^{-1}}^2}{Y^a(t)}\,\mathrm dt =\frac{a^2(1-e^{-4\nu\tau})}{16\nu}+O(a^3). \end{equation}\](42) Choose \(a_\lambda=\lambda^{-2}\) and apply the integer periodic scaling \[\begin{equation} u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u^{a_\lambda}(\lambda x,\lambda^2t). \end{equation}\](43)

Theorem 8.1 (Internal scaling no-go). Along (43), the constructed internal entry and the bare budget satisfy \[\begin{align} a_{\beta,\lambda} &=\frac{e^{-2\nu\tau}}{4\lambda^4}+O(\lambda^{-6}), \\ R_\lambda &=\frac{1-e^{-4\nu\tau}}{16\nu\lambda^6} +O(\lambda^{-8}), \end{align}\](44) · (45) and therefore \[\begin{equation} \frac{a_{\beta,\lambda}}{R_\lambda} =\frac{2\nu}{\sinh(2\nu\tau)}\lambda^2+o(\lambda^2). \end{equation}\](46) There is no constant \(C\), uniform along this family, for which the sum of internal entry atoms is bounded by \(C R_\lambda\).

Moreover, \[\begin{equation} \left\lVert u_\lambda(0)\right\rVert_2^2=O(\lambda^{-2}), \qquad \left\lVert u_\lambda(0)\right\rVert_{\dot H^{1/2}}^2=O(\lambda^{-1}), \qquad \left\lVert \omega_\lambda(0)\right\rVert_2^2=1+o(1). \end{equation}\](47)

Proof. The base entry is given by (40). The covariant dilation moves it to time \(\tau/\lambda^2\) and preserves the scale-zero atom; the amplitude choice contributes \(a_\lambda^2=\lambda^{-4}\), proving (44). The base budget (42) acquires the additional time-scaling factor \(\lambda^{-2}\), which gives (45). Their quotient is (46).

On the normalized torus, the dilation multiplies squared \(L^2\), \(\dot H^{1/2}\), and \(\dot H^1\) norms by \(\lambda^2\), \(\lambda^3\), and \(\lambda^4\), respectively. Combining these factors with \(a_\lambda^2=\lambda^{-4}\) proves (47). ◻

Theorem 8.1 excludes only the bare right side in (42) with a constant uniform along the covariant family. It does not exclude a fixed-frequency theorem, a constant depending on high Sobolev norms, or an estimate with an added atomic, variation, strong-Lamb, or material-derivative charge.

9 Outgoing occupation at the zero level

The bare budget has the wrong scale for an atomic entry. A zero-level occupation density has the correct scale and represents the atom exactly.

Let \(I\) be a compact classical interval whose endpoints are not zeros. For a finite family \(\Lambda\), assume that every zero of every \(C_\alpha\), \(\alpha\in\Lambda\), is isolated and of finite order. Put \[\begin{equation} r_\alpha(t)=\left\lVert C_\alpha(t)\right\rVert_2, \qquad \xi_\alpha(t)=\frac{C_\alpha(t)}{r_\alpha(t)}\quad(r_\alpha>0), \end{equation}\](48) and \[\begin{equation} q_\alpha(t) =\frac{\left\langle F_{j(\alpha)}(t),\xi_\alpha(t)\right\rangle_+^2}{Y(t)}. \end{equation}\](49) Choose \(\rho\in C_c^\infty((0,1))\), \(\rho\ge0\), \(\int_0^1\rho=1\), and set \(\rho_\delta(s)=\delta^{-1}\rho(s/\delta)\).

Theorem 9.1 (Outgoing-coarea identity). Under the preceding assumptions, \[\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} &\sum_{\alpha\in\Lambda} \sum_{\substack{t_*\in I\\C_\alpha(t_*)=0}} \kappa_{j(\alpha)}^{-2}A_{\alpha,+}(t_*)\\ &\quad=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0} \sum_{\alpha\in\Lambda}\kappa_{j(\alpha)}^{-2} \int_Iq_\alpha(t)\rho_\delta(r_\alpha(t)) \left(r_{\alpha,t}(t)\right)_{+}\,\mathrm dt. \end{aligned} \end{equation}\](50) The identity includes both odd crossings and even touches.

Proof. Near a zero of order \(m\), \[C(t_*+s)=cs^m+O_{L^2}(\left\lvert s\right\rvert^{m+1}), \quad r(t_*+s)=\left\lVert c\right\rVert_2s^m(1+O(s)), \quad \xi(t_*+s)\longrightarrow c/\left\lVert c\right\rVert_2\] from the right. The factor \(\left(r_t\right)_{+}\) removes the incoming left side. On the right, the substitution \(y=r(t)\) gives \[\int q(t)\rho_\delta(r(t))\left(r_t(t)\right)_{+}\,\mathrm dt \longrightarrow q(t_*+)=A_+(t_*).\] The argument is unchanged for an even-order touch. Summing finitely many disjoint zero neighborhoods proves (50). ◻

If \(C_t-\nu\Delta C=G\), then wherever \(r>0\), \[\begin{equation} r_t=\left\langle \xi,G\right\rangle-\nu\frac{\left\lVert \nabla C\right\rVert_2^2}{r}. \end{equation}\](51) The density in (50) has scale exponent two before time integration and therefore total scale zero. This repairs the dimensional mismatch of the bare budget only at the level of representation. The bound \(\left(r_t\right)_{+}\le\left\lVert G\right\rVert_2\) still leaves \(\rho_\delta(r)\sim\delta^{-1}\), and no uniform zero-level occupation estimate follows from an ordinary \(L_t^pG\) bound.

For comparison, a fixed smooth divergence-free packet \(\psi\) has an amplitude-weighted excursion bound that is paid by the Leray ledger. If \(b_\psi(t)=\left\langle u(t),\psi\right\rangle\), then on \(I=[s,t]\), \[\begin{equation} V_I^+(b_\psi) \le\nu\left\lVert \nabla\psi\right\rVert_2(\left\lvert I\right\rvert D_I)^{1/2} +\left\lVert \nabla\psi\right\rVert_\infty E_I\left\lvert I\right\rvert, \end{equation}\](52) where \(E_I=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_I\left\lVert u\right\rVert_2^2\) and \(D_I=\int_I\left\lVert \nabla u\right\rVert_2^2\). This controls entries that reach a fixed amplitude, not arbitrarily shallow zero entries.

10 Discussion and exact claim boundary

The results isolate a precise separation.

  1. The projected-Lamb shell injection has a complete signed identity and a scale-critical conditional continuation coefficient.

  2. Leray energy unconditionally controls the normalized vertical heat bulk, globally and under bounded-overlap localization.

  3. The bottom trace is not a free consequence of that bulk. Exact global smooth solutions saturate the full \(K^2\) trace cost, and covariant interior cylinders saturate \(r^{-2}\).

  4. Positive-time internal shell entries genuinely occur in smooth Navier–Stokes dynamics. The bare normalized \(\dot H^{-1}\)-Lamb time integral cannot uniformly pay their scale-invariant atoms.

  5. Outgoing occupation gives an exact scale-matched representation, but its summed zero-level mass is an open estimate.

The paper does not prove that \(A_{\mathrm{sb},+}\in L^1_t\) for every Leray–Hopf solution, does not prove an occupation or recurrence bound, and does not extend the finite-order entry ledger through an unknown singular time. It neither proves global regularity nor constructs finite-time breakdown. The remaining question is not another algebraic decomposition: it is whether the exact Navier–Stokes coupling supplies a scale-zero occupation, jet, or amplitude-thresholded charge that can be summed from an a priori budget.
