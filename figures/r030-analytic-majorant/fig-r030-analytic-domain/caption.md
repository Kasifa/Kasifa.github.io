An all-order analytic majorant for the canonical edge system.** (a) The exact rational values of the convolution kernel \(H_L=L^3\sum_{i+j=L}\min(i,j)/(i^3j^3)\) through total degree 119. (b) That bound closes the coefficient induction with the conservative constant \(K=96\). The series \(a,U,V\) converge absolutely on the polydisc of radius \(1/96\); on the smaller polydisc of radius \(1/192\), the quotient tails are at most \(1/8\), so the logarithms and \(\phi\) are analytic. The figure does not locate the nearest singularity or assert a Navier--Stokes regularity result.
