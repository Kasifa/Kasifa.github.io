**Figure R0.72M-1. Exact action danger window and a full-lattice phase-mixing screen.**
**A**, for \(T(x)=\min\{U,Vx\}/(K+x)\), the superlevel set is the exact open
interval \(AK/(V-A)<x<U/A-K\) when \(A<U/(K+U/V)\), and is empty otherwise;
the shaded example uses \(K/H=0.2\) and \(A/V=0.2\). **B**, in the fixed
one-carrier zero-diffusion reference, the lifted action satisfies
\(x/H\asymp\sigma^{-2/3}\log\sigma\) and
\(Vx/K\asymp\sigma^{-1/3}\log\sigma\), while \(K/U\asymp\sigma^{-1/3}\).
All three ratios vanish, placing this benchmark below the danger window.
**C**, producer and independent finite Bessel quadratures approach the proved
frozen true-cubic coefficient \(16/\pi^2\) after division by
\(a^2\log\sigma\). **D**, two different dissipative solvers give converged
finite values of the analogous cubic/log ratio. These dissipative points are
diagnostic only: they do not prove logarithmic growth, the uniform action-poor
branch, a continuation criterion, or general three-dimensional
Navier--Stokes regularity.
