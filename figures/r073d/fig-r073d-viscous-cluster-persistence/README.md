# R0.73D viscous-cluster persistence figure

Four-panel 178 mm journal figure for the static vanishing-viscosity theorem.

- Panel A shows the exact kinetic-space isometry, compact decomposition, and
  singular domain jump.
- Panel B shows finite eigenvalue convergence across four Fourier cutoffs.
- Panel C shows finite kinetic-space projector norms and projector differences.
- Panel D separates the closed fixed-cluster theorem from the open complement,
  fast-time, nonlinear, and Clay gates.

The finite curves are diagnostic only.  The continuum conclusion comes from
the analytic proof and independently audited certificate.  The archival
masters are PDF and SVG; the PNG is rendered at 600 dpi.
