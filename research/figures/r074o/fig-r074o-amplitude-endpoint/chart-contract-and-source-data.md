`research/r074o_amplitude_endpoint_counterexample.md`.  The fail-closed
validator parses each rational, reconstructs the identities for \(m\), the
energy reserve, \(\delta_*\), and \(q_*\), checks the four-panel semantic
contract, and binds the current proof, exact certificate, reader sources, and
bounded primary-literature boundary by SHA-256.  Independent analytic,
reader, certificate, and literature audits remain external inputs; an absent
required audit makes validation fail rather than silently weakening the gate.

not sampled numerical values or a simulation trace.
