**Figure R0.71F | Localization preserves the projected-Lamb heat bulk but not a subcritical bottom trace.**
(A) For the localized smooth 2D3C low block, the exact profile
\(q_\phi(s)/q_\phi(0)=e^{-2\tau}\), \(\tau=K^2s\), is overlaid with
independent FFT reconstructions from the full trigonometric velocity at
\(K=1,2,4,8\).  The points are finite spectral checks, not DNS or time
stepping.  (B) On a finite heat box \(h=\theta/K^2\), the exact trace
multiplier divided by \(K^2\) is
\(2/(1-e^{-2\theta})\): it has the short-box asymptote
\(\theta^{-1}\) and tends to two for large \(\theta\).  (C) For the
matched-partition bounds at fixed kinetic energy \(a=K^{-1}\), both the
analytic lower and upper bottom envelopes are constant in \(K\), while their
corresponding infinite heat-bulk envelopes decay as \(1/(2K^2)\).  Each
envelope is divided by its own unknown analytic bottom prefactor, so the panel
shows only the certified scale law and is not a measured full-frame band.
(D) A covariantly scaled family of different smooth solutions and admissible
interior cylinders has bottom-to-bulk ratio proportional to \(r^{-2}\).  The
critical power is saturated; the plotted \(r^{-3/2}\) curve is only an
illustrative subcritical comparator.  The figure does not prove physical-time
persistence, reject a critical \(Cr^{-2}\) estimate, construct a singularity,
or establish global regularity.
