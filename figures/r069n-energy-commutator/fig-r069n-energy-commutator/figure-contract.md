# Figure contract — R0.69N energy-stress commutator

## Question

Does compensated pressure structure close against the energy regularity of a
suitable weak solution, and if not, where does the remaining mismatch occur?

## Supported claim

The exact stress-commutator identity avoids the direct Hardy--BMO duality wall
and yields a spatial energy-level estimate. Within the resulting
\(L^2\)-interpolation family, \(q=4\) is optimal and Young's inequality costs
\(\mu_v\sigma_v^3\). A time spike shows that quadratic temporal control alone
cannot bound this cubic cost.

## Boundary

The spike is a functional exponent witness only. It is not a Navier--Stokes
solution or a singularity construction. The commutator estimate does not by
itself prove regularity and does not solve the Millennium Problem.
