正变差给出壳层持留窗口，固定尺度通量速率具有时间 (L^{4/3}) 控制；把压力局部化后，过窄窗口只能导向“调和压力与中心漂移的联合正工作”或带 cutoff 扣除的局部耗散分支。跨壳有限重叠必须使用半径 (2R) 的权重，因此会产生新的扩大外壳耗散债务。

02 / U.1–U.9

正变差产生壳层窗口，但耗散主导支路仍未支付

对 defect-completed clock (K=E+D=Q+F)，先对完整空间积分后的 (F') 取正部。端点能量若没有被耗散或 (Q) 的正变差支付，就产生一个保持固定比例能量的壳层时间窗。

terminal clock → dissipation branch OR quadratic-Q branch OR positive-flux window. (U.1–U.9)

二次 (Q) 行由既有预算支付；第三支可转为持留因子 Θ 的下界。U.9 所需的是跨壳加权逆窗口宽度和，当前并未证明；第一条耗散主导支也没有被反向速度三次量估计支付。

03 / V.1–V.12

固定尺度的 (L_t^{4/3}) 通量速率给出四次宽度

在一个固定半径和固定时间窗内，周期 Sobolev 插值、压力 Calderón–Zygmund 估计与中心速度界给出正通量速率的时间 (L^{4/3}) 控制。由 Hölder 可得第三支窗口的定量下界：

δ_F ≳ ( E / (4N) )^4, β := R^(1/2) N / E, so the conditional all-shell price is an eighth-power β budget. (V.8–V.12)

这个结论只在固定尺度成立。粗略 β 包络没有统一的全壳八次和；因此 (L_t^{4/3}) 可积性阻止单壳任意尖锐，却不排除跨无穷多壳的时间集中。

04 / W.1–W.6

局部压力可支付，调和余项与中心漂移不能消失

在单一欧氏提升上的加厚环壳，把压力写成紧支撑源的 Riesz 部分与内加厚域上的调和余项 (h)。局部部分由速度三次量支付；剩余通量写成完整空间积分后的联合项：

F' = L + S, S = harmonic-pressure work + prescribed-centre drift work, and only [ ∫shell S ]_+ is taken. (W.3–W.6)

压力的时间 gauge 可在整壳积分中消去，但这不等于压力功消失。中心速度也不能改成另一个平滑速度来删除漂移项；先拆绝对值再取正部会丢掉本节必须保留的抵消。

05 / W.7–W.10

过窄窗口给出条件二分支，不是自动的正耗散下界

局部 Sobolev–Gagliardo–Nirenberg 估计把窗口内的三次量写成局部质量、耗散与 cutoff 代价。达到阈值时，只能推出下面的替代：

joint signed residual work is large, OR local viscous dissipation ≥ main threshold − C δ R^(-2) M_k^2. (W.9–W.10)

负的 cutoff 修正不可删除，右端可以非正。所以 W.10 是严格保留修正项的条件分支，不能写成每个过窄窗口都产生一个正的、可求和的局部耗散包。

06 / W.11–W.14

有限重叠成立，但正确权重位于扩大半径

加厚环壳的最大重叠数有限；然而几何包含关系把第 (k) 个半径 (R) 的区域放入半径 (2R) 的相邻权重。原同尺度权重之间的比值并不统一，因此“有限重叠”不能偷换为原 (R) 账本支付。

Σ_k γ_k · local_dissipation(enlarged shell at R) ≤ C · doubled-radius dissipation ledger, not the original radius-R exterior dissipation or P_R. (W.11–W.14)

不同壳层的窗口只通过非负性扩张到共同的 (I_{2R})，没有假设它们相同。扩大域耗散是否由目标预算控制，仍是独立未证项。

Local dissipation and pressure remainders on short windows

Positive variation yields shellwise persistence windows, and the fixed-scale flux rate is controlled in time (L^{4/3}). After localizing pressure, a window below threshold leads either to joint positive work from the harmonic pressure and centre drift or to a local-dissipation branch with a cutoff subtraction. Finite overlap across shells requires weights at radius (2R), creating a new enlarged-exterior dissipation debt.

02 / U.1–U.9

Positive variation creates a shellwise window, but the dissipation-dominant branch is unpaid

For the defect-completed clock (K=E+D=Q+F), the positive part is taken only after the full spatial integral defining (F'). Unless the terminal energy is paid by dissipation or positive variation of (Q), one obtains a shellwise interval on which a fixed fraction of that energy persists.

terminal clock → dissipation branch OR quadratic-Q branch OR positive-flux window. (U.1–U.9)

The quadratic (Q) row is covered by the inherited budget, and the third branch yields a lower bound for the persistence factor Θ. The weighted inverse-width sum required by U.9 is not proved, and the first branch still lacks a reverse velocity-cubic payment.

03 / V.1–V.12

Fixed-scale (L_t^{4/3}) control produces a fourth-power width

On one fixed radius and time interval, periodic Sobolev interpolation, the Calderón–Zygmund pressure bound, and the centre-speed estimate put the positive flux rate in time (L^{4/3}). Hölder then yields

δ_F ≳ ( E / (4N) )^4, β := R^(1/2) N / E, so the conditional all-shell price is an eighth-power β budget. (V.8–V.12)

This is a fixed-scale statement. The crude β envelope has no uniform weighted eighth-power sum. Thus (L_t^{4/3}) rules out an arbitrarily sharp spike on one shell without ruling out concentration distributed over infinitely many shells.

04 / W.1–W.6

The local pressure is payable; the harmonic remainder and centre drift do not disappear

On padded annuli in a single Euclidean lift, pressure is split into a Riesz transform of a compactly supported source and a remainder (h) harmonic on the inner padded region. The local term is paid by cubic velocity mass, while the rest remains as one signed spatial integral.

F' = L + S, S = harmonic-pressure work + prescribed-centre drift work, and only [ ∫shell S ]_+ is taken. (W.3–W.6)

A time-dependent pressure gauge cancels over the whole shell, but the pressure work does not vanish. The centre velocity cannot be replaced by a different smoothed velocity to delete drift, and taking absolute values before the positive part would discard the cancellation retained here.

05 / W.7–W.10

A too-short window yields a conditional alternative, not automatic positive dissipation

Local Sobolev–Gagliardo–Nirenberg bounds express cubic mass on the window through local mass, viscous dissipation, and a cutoff cost. At the threshold the conclusion is only

joint signed residual work is large, OR local viscous dissipation ≥ main threshold − C δ R^(-2) M_k^2. (W.9–W.10)

The negative cutoff correction cannot be dropped, and the right-hand side may be nonpositive. W.10 is therefore a conditional branch with the correction retained, not a positive summable dissipation packet produced by every narrow window.

06 / W.11–W.14

Overlap is finite, but the correct weights live at the enlarged radius

The padded annuli have bounded overlap. Their containment, however, assigns an annulus at radius (R) to neighboring weights at radius (2R). Ratios of the original same-radius weights are not uniformly bounded, so finite overlap cannot be relabeled as payment by the original ledger.

Σ_k γ_k · local_dissipation(enlarged shell at R) ≤ C · doubled-radius dissipation ledger, not the original radius-R exterior dissipation or P_R. (W.11–W.14)

The shell windows are enlarged to a common (I_{2R}) only by nonnegativity; they are not assumed equal. Control of this enlarged-exterior dissipation remains a separate open input.
