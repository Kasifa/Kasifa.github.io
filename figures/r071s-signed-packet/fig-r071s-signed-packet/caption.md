# Figure R0.71S

**R0.71S. Signed and separable bilinear time packets retain entry data only
through an inverse-height or temporal-density cost.** A, for the nonzero-mean
\(L^2\)-normalized box packet at \(h=\theta K^{-2}\), exact constant
reproduction has diagonal \(1/h=K^2/\theta\); moving \(K^{-2}\) outside makes
the displayed coefficient constant but changes the paid ledger from
\(\dot H^{-1}\) to the strong \(L^2\) Lamb budget. B, for \(N=64\)
same-direction boxes, the finite Gram largest eigenvalue lies between the
exact Rayleigh lower bound \(p-(p^2-1)/(3N)\) and exact row-sum upper bound
\(p\); the periodic counterpart is exactly \(p=Nh/T\). C, the normalized
backward/adjoint heat packet has inverse mean square
\((\nu K^2/2)\coth(\nu\theta/2)\), with the same critical-versus-strong
normalization dichotomy in the frozen-denominator linear model. For variable
\(Y\), the normalized NSE observable has an additional \(Y_t/(2Y)\) term,
which this panel does not estimate. D, at a forced-parabolic even touch, the
right positive layer and Jordan layer tend to one and two, while the signed
precursor and a zero-mean separable response remain zero. Panels A--C are
exact packet or linear models; panel D is not an NSE trajectory. In the
separately certified covariant initial-observation family
\(u_{0,K}=a_0K(0,\cos Kx_1,\cos Kx_2)\), the genuine R0.71O/R0.71P NSE
initial face has \(A_+=a_0^2K^2/4\) and

These are initial-face scaling identities and are not shown as positive-time
evolution. No temporal-packing or regularity theorem is asserted.
