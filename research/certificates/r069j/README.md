## Decision boundary

The witness is a signed scalar pressure source, not yet a realization of
`q = tr((grad u)^2)` generated by an incompressible velocity. It proves that
zero mass, zero dipole, harmonicity, and trace freedom alone do not eliminate
the leading quadrupole. It gives no Navier--Stokes regularity or singularity
conclusion and does not solve the Millennium Problem.
