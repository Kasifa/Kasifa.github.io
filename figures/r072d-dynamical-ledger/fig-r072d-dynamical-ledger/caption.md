# Figure R0.72D-1

**Shifted Rudin--Shapiro heat profile and root-ledger diagnostics.**
**A,** The sampled multiplier norm
\(\|V_M(x)\|/(2|K_z|a\sqrt M)\) for the shifted carrier block
\(r_j=M+j\), plotted against \(s=M^2x\) with \(x=\kappa t\).
The five dyadic curves use a fixed 32,768-point phase grid; their collapse is
a finite diagnostic of the \(M^{-2}\) heat lifetime. **B,** Dimensionless
diagnostics for the exact endpoint-corrected root at \(\tau_M=M^{-3}\): the
target-row slope ratio, the sampled \(M^2\ell_\times\) mixed-exposure proxy,
the root atom normalized by \(\gamma^{4/3}\), and the full-charge proxy
normalized by \(\gamma^2\). Root values use independent DOP853 evolution on
the symmetric radius-\(6M\) finite Fourier lattice. Thin reference levels are
analytic model values, not regression fits. Numerical root residuals and
slope ratios corroborate but do not prove the infinite-lattice theorem; the
analytic Duhamel argument in the R0.72D report carries that conclusion. No
PDE DNS or general Navier--Stokes endpoint claim is made.
