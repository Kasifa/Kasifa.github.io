**Figure R0.72L-1. An enstrophy-aware moderate strong-coupling window and its full-lattice boundary.**
**A**, the proved window \(1\lesssim\varepsilon\lesssim p^{2/3}R^{2/3}(1+\log R)\) is shown for incoherent multipliers \(p=1\) and the worst allowed coherent scaling \(p=R^{-1/2}\).  The upper edge gives a bounded normalized ledger; decay requires a little-o relation.  The hatched region is unresolved, not divergent. **B**, with \(R=4096\), \(p=R^{-1/2}\), \(K=1\), and suppressed absolute constants normalized to one, the first-root, mixed-row, and true-cubic terms in the reduced bound are plotted against \(q=\varepsilon/\varepsilon_{\max}\).  This is a scaling diagnostic; the theorem is the analytic inequality. **C**, finite RK4 solutions of the exact three-mode Galerkin ODE show \(G_{\rm Gal}\) and \(\mathcal C_{\times,{\rm Gal}}\) growing linearly in \(\sigma\), while \(\mathcal E_{Q,{\rm Gal}}\) remains order one.  These points corroborate the projected-ODE asymptotics and are not a full-lattice calculation. **D**, the second single-carrier application sends the retained three-mode sector into the omitted \(\pm2R\) shell with exact norm ratio \(\|(I-P_{\mathcal H_R})W_Ru_R\|/\|P_{\mathcal H_R}W_Ru_R\|=1/\sqrt2\).  Hence no nonzero finite Fourier support is invariant; the extreme-coupling region above the window remains open.  Nothing in this figure proves a continuation criterion, finite-time singularity, or general three-dimensional Navier--Stokes regularity.
