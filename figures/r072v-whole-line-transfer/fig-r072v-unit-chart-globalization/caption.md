**R0.72V coefficient-uniform unit charts and whole-line transfer.**
(A) For the normalized even probe
\(q_0=(315/128)(1-4y^2)^4\mathbf 1_{[-1/2,1/2]}\), the exact moments are
\(\mu_2=1/44\), \(\mu_4=3/2288\), and
\(\mu_4-\mu_2^2=5/6292\). Hence
\(\kappa(\theta)=(5/6292)\cos^2\theta+(1/44)\sin^2\theta\) has the exact
positive floor \(5/6292\).
(B) Translating the whole-line cell \(x=k+y\) gives
\(a=3k\), \(b=3k^2+6c\), so every integer cell lies on
\(b=a^2/3+6c\). The displayed curves for several \(c\) values are analytic
presentations of this identity, and the markers are exact integer-\(k\)
locations.
(C) Disjoint unit cells, the exact full-\(H^1\)-norm \(H^{-1}\) restriction
inequality, and the coefficient-uniform local theorem give the whole-line
graph estimate. For the separately constructed exact cubic energy evolution,
this implies the strict block contraction
\(\|U_{c,\sigma}(T,-T)\|\le C_T/\sqrt{T+C_T^2}<1\).
The curve \(r=s/(1+s)\), \(s=C_T^2/T\), shows this algebraic ratio only;
\(C_T\) has not been numerically evaluated. No PDE simulation is shown.
Whole-line block contraction is closed only for the exact cubic energy model.
Periodic transfer, nonlinear Navier--Stokes closure, and the Clay problem
remain open.
