# Figure contract

The figure may support only the following finite statements.

1. The stored $N=96$, $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$ compression has the displayed
   leading cluster, rightmost complementary values, resolvent peak samples,
   and semigroup samples.
2. The primary producer checks pass and an independent implementation
   reproduces the selected quantities to the tolerances in
   `independent_validation.json`.
3. The fixed $Q_0$ and moving $Q_\varepsilon$ curves diagnose long-time
   leakage on this finite time grid.

The package must not identify a finite eigenvalue with continuum spectrum,
turn vertical-line samples into a uniform half-plane bound, turn a time grid
into a continuous-time semigroup estimate, or claim a nonlinear or Clay
result. Any continuum theorem belongs to the analytic proof and certificate,
not to this figure.
