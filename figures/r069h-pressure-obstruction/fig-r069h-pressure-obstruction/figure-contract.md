# Figure contract — R0.69H pressure-Hessian pointwise obstruction

## Question

Can the sign of the principal pressure-Hessian component be determined from
the local strain and vorticity?

## Supported claim

Two smooth periodic divergence-free families have the same local data

\[
 S(0)=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0),\qquad \omega(0)=0,
\]

but exact pressure components

\[
 H_{11}^{-}(0)=-1-\frac{54}{85}t^2,\qquad
 H_{11}^{+}(0)=-1+\frac{54}{85}t^2.
\]

Their signs differ when \(t^2>85/54\).

## Boundary

The figure rules out only a pointwise pressure-sign rule based solely on the
local pair \((S,\omega)\). It does not rule out nonlocal or integrated pressure
mechanisms, and it neither proves Navier--Stokes regularity nor constructs a
singularity.

## Non-color encoding

The response families use solid and dashed curves with circle and square
markers. The decomposition uses distinct hatches, a zero axis, and exact
rational endpoint labels. These distinctions remain visible in grayscale.
