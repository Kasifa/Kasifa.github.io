**One-sentence takeaway.** The 30/30 exact certificate confirms a strict five-exponent hierarchy and, under stated terminal-lobe hypotheses, annular inclusion for the discrete sequence exactly when `j >= 13`; it does not prove the analytic estimates or packet survival.

**Surface and family.** Static two-panel journal figure, 180 x 82 mm. Panel A is a benchmark/interval view with an enlarged close-range inset. Panel B is a set of conditional bound-against-threshold comparisons plus a discrete threshold strip. SVG/PDF/PNG are generated by `plot.py` with ReportLab.

**Data sufficiency.** `source-data.csv` contains exact records parsed from, or recomputed with `fractions.Fraction` from, `research/r074f_two_packet_survival_certificate.json`. No fitted values, sampled paths, simulations, asymptotic constants, or unstated hypotheses are plotted.

**Palette policy.** Hard two-root cap: blue and gold plus neutral ink/greys. Circle, diamond, square, triangle, open/filled states, solid/dashed strokes, row separation, and direct labels carry meaning without hue. The last three close exponents are also shown in an enlarged view.

**Output footprint.** Live-text SVG, one-page embedded-font PDF, approximately 600 dpi master PNG, grayscale derivative, final-size derivative, and PDF render. The research blossom is locked to the top-right header.

**Claim boundary.** Supported: finite exact rational compatibility, strict exponent gaps, the finite buffer threshold, the first admissible discrete index, and conditional annular inclusion under the displayed lobe hypotheses. Unsupported by this figure and visibly marked: periodic-copy estimates, Brownian-bridge analysis, Feynman--Kac ordering, packet survival/sign, the remaining payment ledger, amplitude closure, endpoint divergence, regularity, and any Millennium conclusion. **NOT CLAY.**
