**R0.73G finite top-profile and nonlinear row-leakage diagnostic.**
(A) Real part of the leading eigenvalue of the frozen kinetic Fourier
compression for four cutoffs and seven values of
\(|\Lambda|=\varepsilon^{-1}\); the dotted line marks the certified
inviscid lower reference \(0.17035\), not a fitted limit.  (B) Physical
\(H^3/L^2\) cost of the reconstructed positive-row velocity.  (C) Leray-
projected quadratic leakage from the positive \(K_z=1\) row to \(K_z=2\)
and from the unscaled real conjugate pair to \(K_z=0\), at \(N=128\).
(D) Difference between two independent convolution kernels and the ordinary
\(N=96\) versus \(N=128\) relative change.  All values are binary64 finite
Fourier diagnostics.  Cutoff agreement is not a continuum tail bound; the
figure does not prove the nonlinear theorem, an instability threshold,
three-dimensional vortex stretching, or a Clay-problem conclusion.
