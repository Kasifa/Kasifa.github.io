# QA protocol

Verify three live SHA-256/byte bindings and core commit; recompute exact geometry, weights, exponents, and rate; regenerate source-data byte-for-byte; reject complete-clock/fixed-deletion/novelty/simulation/DNS/Clay promotions. Require live-text SVG, 4204×2740 PNG, one-page vector PDF, 2102×1370 final-size/grayscale/PDF QA, embedded fonts, locked blossom, two byte-identical rerenders, 25 files, 24-entry SHA256SUMS, local seal, and verify-only PASS. Inspect all QA rasters for legibility, clipping, branch logic, and grayscale distinction.
