# Figure R0.64-1 — A zero-time digit cycle exceeds the factor-two threshold

**English.** (a) The exact zero-time quartic recursion has `2 × 8 × 3 = 48`
target-sign, cubic, and carry states.  For the least-significant-bit-first
word `0100`, the four-step product has rank `6`.  (b) Its nonzero spectrum is
the two roots at `16` together with the four roots of
`x^4 - 25x^3 - 120x^2 + 3248x - 8192`; the dominant root is
`25.151589...`, strictly above the four-level threshold `16`.  (c) The
actual target family `M_r=16^r`, `q_r=2(16^r-1)/15` is reachable and
observable.  The exact scalar recurrence has asymptotic block growth
`25.151589...`.  This rules out a common pointwise factor-two norm on the
full zero-time state space.  It does not prove or disprove the
Gaussian-weighted integrated estimate.

**中文。** (a) 零热时间的精确四次递推包含 `2 × 8 × 3 = 48` 个目标符号、
三次乘积和进位状态。按最低位到最高位读取 `0100`，四步乘积的秩为 `6`。
(b) 非零谱由两个 `16` 和四次多项式
`x^4 - 25x^3 - 120x^2 + 3248x - 8192` 的四个根组成；最大根为
`25.151589...`，严格超过四层阈值 `16`。(c) 真实目标族
`M_r=16^r`、`q_r=2(16^r-1)/15` 可以到达并观测该增长模态，精确标量递推
的渐近四层增长率同样为 `25.151589...`。因此完整零时间状态空间不存在统一
的逐层因子二范数；这一结论不证明也不否定带高斯权的积分估计。
