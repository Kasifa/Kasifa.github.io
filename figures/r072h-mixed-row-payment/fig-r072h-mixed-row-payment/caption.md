**Figure R0.72H-1. Critical-log payment and its sharp carrier scale.**
**A**, the reciprocal critical-log envelope
\(\Phi(a)\) divided by
\((1+a)^{-1/3}/[1+\log(2+a)]\); the bounded ratio records the analytic
crossover, not a fitted law. **B**, producer and independent finite-lattice
values normalized by the three proved scales:
\(\mathcal E_Q/(a^2M^2)\),
\(Q_*/(a^2M^{2/3}\log M)\), and
\(m_*/(a^2M^{7/3}/\log M)\).
**C**, the action-only quotient grows with the reference
\(M^{4/3}/\log M\), while
\(\sqrt{Mm_*Q_*}/\mathcal E_Q\) remains bounded. The final exact-root
residual is below \(10^{-18}\). The plotted finite calculations corroborate
the analytic theorem; they do not establish a full physical
\(D^{1/3}\Lambda_{1,*}\) estimate or general Navier--Stokes regularity.
