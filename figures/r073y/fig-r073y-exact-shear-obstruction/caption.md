**Figure R0.73Y. An analytic exact-shear witness invisible to signed production observables.** For the normalized exact Navier-Stokes shear $u=\sin(x_2)e_1$ at a fixed time slice,

\[
D_{ii,s}(x_2)=\frac{1}{2}(1-e^{-2s})(1-e^{-2s}\cos 2x_2),
\]

while $\Pi_s=\mathscr S_s=0$ pointwise at every heat scale. (A) Exact covariance profiles at four heat scales. (B) Exact pointwise minimum, spatial mean, and pointwise maximum; $\min_{x_2}D_{ii,s}=\frac{1}{2}(1-e^{-2s})^2>0$ for every $s>0$. (C) After normalization at $|A|=1$, the positive exterior-size quantity scales as $|A|^3$, while both signed production observables remain zero. Hence no amplitude-independent finite coercive modulus formed only through zero-preserving operations on $\Pi_s$ and $\mathscr S_s$ can control this family. All curves are closed-form evaluations, not direct numerical simulation. This obstruction is not a Navier-Stokes regularity theorem.
