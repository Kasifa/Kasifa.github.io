**Figure R0.69E. Positive-time gluing of the critical linearized
resolvent.** (a) The exact two-block majorant has inverse row-sum factor
\(1/[(1-a)(1-b)]\) in the strict square \(0\le a,b<1\). Contours show its
base-10 logarithm; the factor diverges only at a diagonal-block gate.
(b) For equal late slabs, the causal coefficients are
\(\ell_0=\eta\) and
\(\ell_k=\eta(\sqrt{k}-\sqrt{k-1})\). The plotted curves give the exact
forward-substitution row sum of the finite inverse for four dimensionless
values of \(\eta\). Every finite system remains invertible when \(\eta<1\),
although conditioning can grow quickly with the number of slabs. The values
of \(a,b,\eta\) are dimensionless scenarios, not numerical assignments of
universal Koch--Tataru or Stokes constants.
