**Figure R0.71V-1. Fixed-target zero-level boundary layer.** The exact
two-root tangent of the 2.5D invariant Navier-Stokes family is evaluated for
\(q=8,16,32,64,128,256\), with \(\nu=0.02\), \(K_y=K_z=1\), fixed target-shell
scale \(\kappa_*=1\), multiplier \(m_*=1\) (target wave number \(\rho^2=2\)),
\(d=8\), scaled roots
\(0.1\) and \(0.2\), fixed audit length \(\ell=0.5\), background mode \(Q=4\),
and seed amplitude \(\varepsilon_q=q^{-2}\). **A**, the rescaled
profiles \(q^2\gamma_q(r/q^2)\) converge to the two-root limit
\(\Gamma(r)\). **B**, the atom at the **second prescribed root**, the
theorem-weighted singleton target-shell rows
\((2/\ell)\mathcal B_{1,q}^{(*)}\) and
\((7\ell/3)\mathcal B_{2,q}^{(*)}\), and the terminal excursion height square
have the orders \(q^{-4}\), \(q^{-6}\), \(q^{-2}\), and \(q^{-8}\). **C**, the
second-root atom to first-row ratio grows as \(q^2\), while its atom to
second-row ratio decays as \(q^{-2}\); the terminal height charge loses four
powers relative to that atom. Thus the first-row-only obstruction remains
after the first prescribed root is separately paid. The fixed singleton-shell
constants are restored: relative to reduced scalar quantities,
\(J=2j_{\rm red}\), \(\mathcal B_r^{(*)}=8b_{r,\rm red}\) for \(r=1,2\),
and \(H_E^2=8h_{E,\rm red}^2\), while \(D_E\) is unchanged. **D**, the internal and
terminal excursion non-collapse factors decay
as \(q^{-2}\) and \(q^{-4}\). The data are evaluations of the exact response
functions and IFT tangent with deterministic one-dimensional quadrature. The
nonlinear trajectory is not integrated. The fixed target and fixed
macroscopic window make this a multiscale auxiliary-to-target family, not a
covariant Navier-Stokes dilation. The figure does not prove the IFT, a
uniform nonlinear remainder, the sharpness of the coefficient \(7\ell/3\),
or global regularity.
