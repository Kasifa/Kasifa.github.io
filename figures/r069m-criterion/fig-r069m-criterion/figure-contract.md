# Figure contract — R0.69M criterion comparison

## Question

Which part of the R0.69L three-zone pressure budget is controlled at critical
Morrey or suitable-weak-solution regularity?

## Supported claim

The exact shell sum gives

\[
 B_\infty\leq\mathfrak M_2/120.
\]

There is no reverse estimate with a universal constant. For the smooth
time-slice family with amplitude \(a_N=N^{-1/2}\), velocity \(L^3\) and
kinetic Morrey quantities decay, while the near \(L^2\) source and absolute
annular \(u q\) quantity grow.

## Boundary

The high-frequency family tests a spatial functional inequality only. It is
not a singular Navier--Stokes solution, does not contradict parabolic
epsilon-regularity, and proves neither regularity nor singularity.
