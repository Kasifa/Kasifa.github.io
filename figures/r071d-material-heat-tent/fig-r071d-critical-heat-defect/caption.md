**Figure R0.71D. Complete material heat tents retain a critical viscous
refinement defect.** (A) The smooth weights
\(\phi_\pm=(1\pm\rho\cos 2z)/2\), with \(\rho=1/2\), form an exact material
partition for the shear solution because its velocity has no \(x_1\)
component. The normalized enstrophy density is shown in gray. (B) The two
child viscous injections have equal magnitude and opposite sign, so their
parent sum is zero, but squaring the positive child after refinement gives
\(\delta_k/(\nu^2k^2Y)=\rho^2/(2+\rho)=0.1\). (C) The unscaled normalized
defect grows exactly as \(k^2\), while division by \(k^2\) gives a constant;
there is no \(k^{-\varepsilon}\) gain. (D) On
\(\tau_k=\theta/(\nu k^2)\), the dimensionless box cost is independent of
\(k\) for every displayed \(\theta\), and the signed time-box Cauchy
inequality is exactly saturated. Values are closed formulas, not a PDE
simulation. The figure does not prove divergence of a general tent norm,
exclude an NSE-specific nonlinear depletion mechanism, or establish
unconditional regularity.
