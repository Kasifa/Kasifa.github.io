# Figure R0.71H - Projective heat curvature and the remaining weighted-BV gap

**A.** Let \(A_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)\), \(\nu=1\), and
\(C(t)=(e^{-t},e^{-4t})\). For \(E=C/\|C\|\) and
\(r=\langle A_0E,E\rangle\), the exact pure-heat identity is

\[
 \int_0^t\|E_s\|^2\,ds
 +\int_0^t\|P_{E^\perp}A_0E\|^2\,ds
 =r(0)-r(t).
\]

The angular and curvature terms coincide in this unforced case, so each
contributes one half of the Rayleigh payment. The open squares show their
sum on top of the closed-form payment curve.

**B.** At the true initial time of the exact global-smooth 2D3C family, fix
\(\nu=1\), \(\|u_0\|_2^2=6\), and \(a_K=K^{-1}\). Exact Fourier algebra gives
\(\Omega_K(0)=K/2\) and
\(S_K(0)=\frac14(3+2/K)^2\to9/4\). Thus no finite energy-only bound can
control the unweighted pointwise angular speed uniformly in \(K\), although
the source density remains finite. This panel concerns \(t=0\) only and says
nothing negative about time-integrated angular turning.

**C.** For
\(\chi_\delta(Z)=(1+\delta\cos Z)/2\), finite-Fourier orthogonality gives

\[
 R_\delta=\frac{2(3\delta^2+2)}{3\delta^2+4},\qquad
 J_\delta=\frac{12\delta^2}{(3\delta^2+4)^2}.
\]

Hence \(1\le R_\delta\le10/7\) and
\(0\le J_\delta\le12/49\) on the displayed interval. This is finite
saturation for one fixed cutoff template, not a general denominator or
partition theorem.

**D.** After the required outer \(K^{-2}\) BV weight, the direct Young
estimate leaves an unweighted factor \(\|F\|_2^2/Y\). The R0.71F heat bulk
has only the coefficient \(K^{-2}\|F\|_2^2/Y\). Their ratio is exactly
\(K^2\): the direct route is two frequency powers stronger. This is an exact
scaling comparison, not a fitted slope and not proof that a deeper
Navier-Stokes cancellation is impossible.

All plotted values come from closed-form formulas. There is no DNS, fitted
data, ODE integration, or time-evolved three-dimensional simulation. The
figure gives no regularity theorem, singularity claim, originality claim, or
Millennium-problem conclusion.
