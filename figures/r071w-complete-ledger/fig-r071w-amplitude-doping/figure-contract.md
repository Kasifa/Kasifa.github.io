# Figure contract: R0.71W amplitude-doped complete-ledger test

## Analytical question

For the exact unforced 2.5D recurrence family with

\[
A_q=q^{3/2},\qquad B_q=A_q q,
\]

does the selected second-root atom outgrow the complete normalized
\(\nu^2\)-plus-rotational ledger? Does an independently integrated finite
Fourier coset reproduce the predicted atom and rotational powers?

## Evidence boundary

Panel A uses the primary high-precision R0.71W certificate and its independent
reconstruction. Panels B and C use the separate deterministic retained-coset
calculation. The two evidence classes must not be visually or verbally merged.
The finite-coset calculation is nonlinear in the shear parameters, but it is
not DNS and is not a proof that the truncation converges to the continuum
solution.

## Data grain

- Panel A: thirteen certified asymptotic cases, one per \(q\).
- Panel B: five nonlinear retained-coset cases, \(q=256,\ldots,4096\), with
  every retained convolution mode included in the \(\dot H^{-1}\) sum.
- Panel C: the five normalized second-root slopes and three independent
  truncation comparisons against \(R=40\) at \(q=1024\).
- No stochastic sample, fitted physical parameter, or DNS time series occurs.

## Visual encoding

- Three panels in a static 178.05 mm double-column figure.
- Panel A spans the width; Panels B and C share the lower row.
- Near-white paper, dark ink, muted blue and ochre only.
- Solid/filled-circle versus dashed/open-square encodings duplicate color.
- Logarithmic axes are used only for positive scale comparisons. Panel C's
  slope axis is linear; its inset uses a log scale for positive relative
  truncation differences.
- Analytic power guides are labeled directly and are not used as proof.

## Required boundaries

- Panel A is labeled as a leading certificate proxy, not the nonlinear solve.
- Panel B is labeled as a retained-coset corroboration and plots the rotational
  charge including its analytic time-tail upper bound.
- Panel C identifies \(R=40\) as the reference truncation.
- Caption and footer state: computation corroboration only, not DNS, continuum
  IFT and truncation convergence not established, and initial data are not
  uniformly bounded.
