**Figure R0.71O-1. Soft-denominator profiles and one-sided face measures.**
(A) Normalized inner profiles
`Phi_m(s)=s^(2m)/(1+s^(2m))` for a first-order odd zero with positive
leading pairing and a second-order even zero. The odd profile has only the
right positive atom; the even profile has a left negative and a right
positive atom at the same limiting time. (B) With unit trace amplitude, the
odd zero has signed atom, hard-BV jump, and relaxed/Jordan cost all equal to
one. At the even zero the signed atom and hard-BV jump cancel to zero, while
the relaxed/Jordan cost is two. Thus the segmented soft relaxation is not
identical to standard distributional BV of the hard limit. (C) For the
abstract smooth Hilbert paths
`C_N=N^(-1) sin(Nt)e`, `F=e`, `Y=1`, and `epsilon_N=N^(-4)`, the soft face
variation is `2N^3/(N^2+1)`, asymptotic to `2N`, whereas
`integral d dt=pi/N^2` and `integral |C_t|^2 dt=pi`. This is an abstract
budget separation, not an NSE counterexample. (D) For
`u_0=(0,cos x_1,0)+(0,0,cos x_2)` and a real-even radial multiplier vanishing
at radius one and equal to one at radius `sqrt(2)`, the filtered Lamb field
has four target modes `(plus or minus 1, plus or minus 1,0)`. Exact Fourier
convolution gives `Y(0)=1`, `B_t(0)=1/2`,
`|C_t(0)|_2^2=1`, and the one-sided right entry trace `a(0+)=1/4`.
Panel D is an initial-jet result only: it gives no time integration, internal
face, unbounded NSE face count, continuation criterion, or regularity result.
