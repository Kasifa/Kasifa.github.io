# Figure caption

**Figure R0.74S-17. Recurrence separates signed excursion from absolute temporal variation in an exact Taylor vortex.**

**(A)** The regular closed streamline
\(\Gamma=\{\sin x_1\sin x_2=1/2\}\) in the positive cell, oriented by
\(W=(\sin x_1\cos x_2,-\cos x_1\sin x_2)\).  The marked points
\(x_*=(\pi/4,\pi/4)\) and \(x^\dagger=(\pi/2,\pi/6)\) satisfy
\(|W|^2=1/2\) and \(3/4\), respectively.

**(B)** One period of \(g(s)=|W(\chi(s))|^2\), with
\(\chi'=W(\chi)\) and \(\chi(0)=x_*\).  The period is obtained from a
deterministic quadrature of \(\int_\Gamma d\ell/|W|\), and the plotted time
parametrization is a fixed-step RK4 rendering of the exact analytic orbit.

**(C)** Over four returns, the signed primitive \(g(s)-g(0)\) remains
bounded while the cumulative absolute variation \(\int_0^s|q(r)|dr\),
\(q=g'\), grows by \(V_1=2\) per circuit.  This is the recurrence mechanism
behind the backtracking debt.

**(D)** Normalized amplitude-exponent guides summarize the scale separation.
The proved positive-excursion and \((P_R^M)^{2/3}\) laws lie in the quadratic
class; \(\mathfrak M^K\) is placed on that line as the surviving target scale
identified by (S.473)--(S.475), not as a separately measured exact law.
Absolute variation \(\mathfrak H^F_1\) and the complete payment \(P_R^M\)
lie in the cubic class.  Constants are suppressed; the panel compares
exponents rather than estimating universal constants.

All panels are analytic-exact or deterministic numerical renderings of the
frozen exact family.  They are not DNS, do not prove the open estimate
(S.472), and do not solve the Navier--Stokes Millennium problem.
