研究背景Background

三维不可压缩 Navier–Stokes 方程描述黏性流体的运动。本研究关注光滑解的持续存在，以及有限时间奇性是否可能形成；一般情形仍未解决。

The three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes equations describe viscous fluid motion. The research concerns continued smooth evolution and possible finite-time singularities. The general problem remains open.

最新进展Latest progress

研究继续梳理条件性正则判据及构造机制的适用范围。局部估计、有限结构检验与一般方程的完整结论严格区分。现阶段没有一般正则性或奇性证明。

Work continues on conditional regularity criteria and the scope of proposed construction mechanisms. Local estimates and finite structural checks are distinguished from conclusions for the full equation. No general regularity or singularity proof is claimed.
