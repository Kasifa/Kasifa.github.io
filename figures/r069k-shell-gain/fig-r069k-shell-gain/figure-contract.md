# Figure contract — R0.69K velocity-generated shell gain

## Question

Does the velocity-generated pressure source improve far-shell decay?

## Supported claim

Stress localization and two integrations by parts give
\[
 |Q^{(m)}|\le CR_m^{-5}\int\chi_m|u|^2.
\]
An anisotropic positive-semidefinite energy tensor has a nonzero \(R^{-5}\)
coefficient, so the improved power is sharp.

## Boundary

Near shells and cutoff-transition pressure terms remain open. The figure
neither proves Navier--Stokes regularity nor constructs a singularity.
