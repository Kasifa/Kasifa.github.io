# R0.74S literature and priority boundary

R0.74S is an internal algebraic route audit built on the already cited local-energy, suitable-weak, shell-clock, and cutoff literature of R0.74H/P/R. It does not claim a new Navier--Stokes theorem from the abstract tower witness.

The proved statement is deliberately narrow: scalar completed-clock positivity, cutoff linearity, and the displayed tower identities do not imply the desired matched l2 compression. The witness is not a velocity field, pressure, dissipation measure, work density, spatial cutoff operator, or Navier--Stokes solution.

No novelty or priority claim is made. The absence of a PDE realization means the result must not be advertised as a PDE/NSE counterexample. Cross-channel dynamical signs, finite block genealogy, the fixed-scale inequality, scale contraction, regularity, singularity formation, and Clay remain OPEN. NOT CLAY.
