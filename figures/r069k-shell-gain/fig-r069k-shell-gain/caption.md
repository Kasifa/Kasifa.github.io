**Figure R0.69K | Velocity generation gains two powers of far-shell
decay, but the improved coefficient remains nonzero.** (a) A general scalar
source can retain an \(R^{-3}\) quadrupole. Stress-localizing
\(q=\partial_i\partial_j(u_i u_j)\) permits two integrations by parts and
gives an \(R^{-5}\) kinetic-energy bound. (b) The positive-semidefinite energy
tensor \(\operatorname{diag}(1,2,0)\) produces
\(4\pi R^5Q_R=\operatorname{diag}(0,6,-6)\), whose strain pairing is
\(-3/(2\pi R^5)\). The power is sharp; no regularity conclusion follows.
