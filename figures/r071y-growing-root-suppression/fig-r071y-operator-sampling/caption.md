**Figure R0.71Y-1. Growing-root operator-sampling bounds.** **A**, for
(M=2N+1) unit-modulus carrier phases on distinct positive integer
frequencies, the minimum lattice cost
(K_s=\sum_{j=1}^{M}j^2) gives the exact factor (NM/K_s); it decreases
with fitted tail power (-0.999986) and lies below (3/(4N)). **B**, the
exact selected-root theorem envelopes are divided by their (N=1) values.
At the displayed fixed observation coupling
(\delta_{\mathrm{obs}}=1/8), the no-separation envelope has tail power
(-0.999986). The separated estimate has tail power (-1.999986) at fixed
(h=0.05), while (h=N^{-1}) has the same normalized (N^{-1}) law as
the no-separation envelope. These are analytic/certificate envelopes, not
simulated values. **C**, for canonical rates (r_l=l) and equal-grid roots
with (h=N^{-3}), the certified determinant estimate raises the
base-ten logarithm of the inverse lower bound from (1.806) at (N=4) to
(49.029) at (N=64). This conditioning lower bound is not an upper bound
on the true nonlinear IFT radius. The selected-root bounds assume real shear,
unit carrier phases, fixed target, and full data/root-time enstrophy floors;
they neither construct a growing exact-root family nor count all nonlinear
roots. The package is not DNS and proves no universal endpoint estimate or
Navier--Stokes regularity statement.
