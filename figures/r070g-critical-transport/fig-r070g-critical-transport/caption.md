**Figure R0.70G — Critical transport of adjacent fixed-annulus jets.**
(A) After dilation to the unit shell, a homogeneous jet of degree
\(n\) obeys
\(\widehat J_j^{(n)}=\widehat P_j^{(n)}-
2^{-(n+2)}\widehat P_{j-1}^{(n)}\). The constant, linear, and quadratic
transport coefficients are therefore \(1/4\), \(1/8\), and \(1/16\); the
dual moment must dilate by the reciprocal factor for the defect to vanish.
(B) For the exact comparator \(p_j=1-2^{-j}\), the raw cumulative mass is
\(N-1+2^{-N}\), while the ordinary signed differences telescope to
\(p_N\le1\); the inset shows \(\Delta p_j=2^{-j}\). (C) The source-side
weighted square-function factor in the displayed comparator stays below one,
whereas the required dual core factor grows like \(\sqrt N\). This identifies
the missing input rather than proving that physical Navier--Stokes core
moments have that behavior. Every curve evaluates a closed formula. The
figure is not a simulation, a positive-part packing theorem, or evidence for
a common-terminal-time cascade.
