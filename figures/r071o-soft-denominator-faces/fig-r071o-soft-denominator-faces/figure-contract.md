# Figure contract

## Analytical question

What survives when the soft denominator approaches an isolated zero, and can
ordinary denominator-mass and first-time-derivative budgets control the
resulting one-sided face cost?

## Takeaway

At a finite-order zero, the soft layer has explicit positive and negative
one-sided atoms. Their signed difference can cancel while their Jordan mass
remains. An abstract smooth path separates that relaxed face cost from
ordinary quadratic budgets. A separate exact NSE initial jet realizes one
positive right entry trace, but supplies no internal or unbounded NSE face
count.

## Visual policy

- Surface: static journal figure.
- Size: 178 mm by 118 mm, double column.
- Outputs: vector PDF and SVG, plus 600 dpi PNG.
- Palette: hard two-root cap, blue and orange, plus neutral ink and grays.
- Non-color distinctions: solid versus dashed/dotted lines, filled circles
  versus open squares/diamonds, hatched/open bars, atom direction, and direct
  labels.
- Panel C uses logarithmic axes because the exact positive quantities span
  more than five orders of magnitude; all sampled values are retained in
  `data.csv`.
- Titles are neutral and descriptive.
- The abstract-path and NSE-initial-jet boundaries are visible in the figure,
  caption, manifest, and source data.
