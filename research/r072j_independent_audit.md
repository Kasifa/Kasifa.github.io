# R0.72J independent audit -- coherent mixed-parity block

**Date:** 2026-08-27

**Decision:** producer, independent, and cross-route finite audits all report
passed. The two implementations corroborate the exact signed-triple count,
one complex root correction, the true cubic integral, and its physical
decay over \(R=4,8,16,32,64\). They do not prove the analytic asymptotics or
enumerate the complete complex root set.

## 1. Independently checked finite ledger

Both routes use

\[
 S_R=\{R,R+1,\ldots,3R-1\},
 \qquad N=2R,
 \qquad w_r=1,
 \qquad \delta=0.05R.
\tag{1.1}
\]

They separately enumerate

\[
 A_R=\frac{R(R+1)}2,
 \qquad
 T_R=6A_R=3R(R+1),
\tag{1.2}
\]

and verify for the uncorrected coherent launch that

\[
 |P_0V_R(0)^2G_R|=\frac{T_R}{\sqrt2}.
\tag{1.3}
\]

Each route evolves an independent \(e_0\) column and applies the exact
linear correction at \(\tau=R^{-3}\). Both also recompute the carrier moment,
physical conversion, critical action, exact exposure,
\(\delta\int|hP_0V^2F|\), and the normalized true cubic.

## 4. Exact exposure check

Both implementations use the exact identity

\[
 |\delta|\int_0^\infty\|V_R(x)\|\,dx
 =2|\delta|\sum_{r=R}^{3R-1}r^{-2}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

For \(\delta=0.05R\), the right side tends to
\(4(0.05)/3=1/15\). At \(R=64\), it is
\(0.06736502921960838\), with relative error \(0.0104754\) from that limit.

## 6. What the finite audit does not certify

The runs do not certify:

1. the infinite-\(R\) estimates;
2. interval enclosures for rounding, quadrature, or lattice truncation;
3. every non-bipartite carrier set;
4. every temporal zero of the complex target coordinate;
5. a valid real-line Rolle reduction for the coherent complex launch;
6. strong coupling, multiscale carriers, or general three-dimensional
   Navier--Stokes regularity.

The exact combinatorial identities and the analytic common-band theorem do
not depend on the finite runs. The computations are corroboration only.
