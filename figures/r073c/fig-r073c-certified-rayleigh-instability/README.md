# R0.73C certified Rayleigh-instability figure

Four-panel 178 mm journal figure for the frozen collision profile.

- Panel A displays the exact profile and periodic neutral mode.
- Panel B displays a high-accuracy diagnostic monodromy-trace curve and the
  two rigorously certified endpoint signs.
- Panel C displays finite Fourier cutoff convergence and complete embedded
  residuals; these rows are explicitly diagnostic only.
- Panel D displays the proof/open-gate boundary from C3 through C6.

The archival masters are PDF and SVG.  The PNG is rendered at 600 dpi.  The
figure does not claim uniqueness of the unstable root, viscous persistence,
nonautonomous transfer, nonlinear regularity, or a Clay solution.
