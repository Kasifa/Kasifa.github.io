# Figure R0.71G — Sign residence, relative exits, and the missing sum

**A.** The exact 2D3C sideband chain is plotted through the rescaled signed
low-shell work \(e^{4\theta}H_\mu(\theta)\), where
\(\theta=\nu K^2t\) and \(\mu=a/\nu\).  The first sign crossing moves from
\(0.8937\) at \(\mu=0.5\) to \(9.7763\) at \(\mu=0.05\).  The dotted
\(\mu=0\) coefficient profile equals one.  These finite curves illustrate
the exact chain; the arbitrary-duration theorem follows from an analytic
Duhamel estimate, not from extrapolation.

**B.** First sign exits for
\(\mu=1,0.5,0.2,0.1,0.05\) grow with \(\mu^{-1}\).  The dashed
\(0.5\mu^{-1}\) line is a first-order guide, not a proved asymptotic law.  The
horizontal dotted line marks one viscous time.

**C.** First exits of the positive quotient at
\(q/q(0)=0.5,0.1,0.01\) stay bounded in dimensionless time as
\(\mu\downarrow0\).  Dotted horizontal lines are the exact weak-coupling
limits \(\theta_q=\frac16\log(1/\rho)\).  Thus this true-solution family
rejects sign-only residence but does not reject a critical \(K^{-2}\) law for
fixed positive relative levels.

**D.** An exact functional counterexample uses disjoint intervals of length
\(K_n^{-2}\) and amplitudes \(K_n^2\).  The weighted partial sum tends to
\(1/3\), while the unweighted bottom integral equals the number of shells and
diverges.  Critical residence plus the R0.71F weighted heat bulk is therefore
insufficient without amplitude, crossing, or frequency-envelope summability.

Panels A--C are finite integrations of the exact reduced global-smooth 2D3C
chain at \(|m|\le24\); Panel D is closed-form.  This is not DNS, not a
three-dimensional PDE time stepper, not evidence of blow-up, and not an
unconditional regularity theorem.
