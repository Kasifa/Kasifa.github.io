**Figure R0.69V. Exact two-scale decoupling and the finite-separation coarse-annulus obstruction.**
(a) Full-space amplitude screen for the shape-changing family
\(u_{\varepsilon,a}=aU_1+(1-a)U_\varepsilon\) at \(\varepsilon=1/4\).
The numerator is the exact cubic local production; the denominator is the sum
of absolute annular means from 16 independent scrambled-Sobol replicates with
\(2^{18}\) samples in every annulus-zone stratum.  The selected value
\(a=0.1595\) reaches the exploratory ratio 0.9636, not one.  (b) Independent
direct zone-pair reconstruction of the \(j=-2\) and \(j=0\) annular cubics.
Shading gives pointwise 95% scramble intervals.  On the 4001-point grid no
amplitude makes both means nonnegative; even the maximized minimum is
\(-2.95\times10^{-4}\) at \(a=0.107\).  The bands are not simultaneous
confidence bands.  (c) Unordered zone-pair decomposition of \(j=0\) at the
selected amplitude, using 16 scrambles with \(2^{19}\) samples per pair.
The outer-transition self-pair supplies the dominant negative term, and the
complete \(j=0\) carrier is \(-6.2919\times10^{-4}\) with reported standard
error \(1.0343\times10^{-5}\).  The exact theorem states that as
\(\varepsilon\to0\), uniformly in amplitude, the full annular ratio returns to
the one-profile baseline.  All finite-separation intervals and root-gap
statements in the figure are randomized diagnostics, not rigorous enclosures
or a Navier--Stokes regularity theorem.
