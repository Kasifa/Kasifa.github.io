CB.3｜伴随测试的定位代价

真实物理时间的反向伴随测试可以合法进入 suitable Leray–Hopf 局部能量不等式，并把提升二阶矩控制到 C(1+DJ/R)；但它仍保留早期能量、有符号残余输运和压力，也没有自动给出原移动管的能量下界。

这是固定正尺度的局部结果与有限范围反例：不推进 R0 主序列，不是正则性证明，不是奇点构造，不主张新颖性。合同 G 仍 OPEN。NOT CLAY.

路线上的作用是明确下一道真正的支付：伴随密度的矩和质量尾界可以得到，但它们尚不能替代移动球内的原局部能量，也不能令剩余有符号项自动可吸收。

02 / B.1–B.5

固定尺度的真实时间反向测试

令 bR 为尺度 R 的平滑速度，令非负测试 ψ 沿 bR 的物理时间伴随方程反向演化。代入 suitable 弱解的局部能量不等式后，平滑主输运被抵消，但残余速度 u−bR、压力和早期能量仍在账本中。

terminal localized energy + viscous payment ≤ starting localized energy + residual transport + pressure. (B.1–B.5)

起点必须是强 L² 右 Lebesgue 时间；终点可以取任意弱代表并用正权弱下半连续性处理。终点的弱下半连续缺口不能被冒充为局部能量 defect measure 的时间原子。

03 / B.6–B.13

相对平滑路径的提升二阶矩只有线性耗散代价

把伴随密度提升到有限质量的欧氏代表，以原平滑路径为参考。随机表示、速度相对空间均值的振荡、Young 与 Sobolev–Poincaré 给出

Q / (M R²) ≤ C ( 1 + D_J / R ). (B.8)

这里 Q 是提升二阶矩，M 是质量，DJ 是对应时间窗上的耗散尺度量。线性优于直接 Lipschitz 形变产生的指数代价，但 B.8 并不证明 DJ/R 一致有界或趋小。

Chebyshev 只能把该矩转成质量尾界；它不提供原移动球内能量的统一正下界。

04 / B.14–B.16

周期 Nash 高度界与直接 Hölder 缺口

固定三维环面上的 Nash 不等式带有常数模态修正。不可压缩漂移保持 L¹ 收缩，并给伴随核标准的漂移无关高度控制。

||ψ(τ)||∞ ≤ C (1 + τ^{-3/2}) ||ψ(0)||₁. (B.14)

这仍只是核密度高度，不是空间中心控制。对残余输运与压力直接使用 Hölder 时，需要的核范数与当前能量级支付并不自动闭合；B.16 记录的是这条直接路线的缺口，不是否定其他补偿机制。

05 / C.1–C.6

真实光滑 NS 剪切族排除幅值与漂移无关的免费矩定位

一个精确周期剪切解使非线性平流恒为零，因而是无外力、全时光滑的真实三维 Navier–Stokes 解。固定 R 与普通光滑终点 T 后放大幅值 A，可使提升欧氏二阶矩的 A² 系数严格为正。

fixed R,T; all solutions smooth; lifted second moment has a strictly positive A² contribution. (C.1–C.6)

因此，不含任何初值幅值或漂移支付的统一 O(R²) 矩界不成立。这里初始 L² 与 H¹ 都不统一；该族不检验首次奇点，不证明 B.8 最优，也不否定合同 G。

CB.3 | The localization cost of an adjoint test

A backward adjoint on physical time is admissible in the suitable Leray–Hopf local-energy inequality and controls the lifted second moment by C(1+DJ/R). It still retains the early energy, signed residual transport, and pressure, and it does not automatically control the original moving energy tube.

This is a fixed-positive-scale local result with a limited counterexample. It does not advance the R0 series, prove regularity, construct a singularity, or claim novelty. Contract G remains OPEN. NOT CLAY.

The route-level conclusion is precise: a moment and a mass-tail bound are available for the adjoint density, but neither substitutes for the original local energy inside the moving ball or makes the remaining signed terms absorbable.

02 / B.1–B.5

A backward physical-time test at fixed scale

Let bR be the velocity smoothed at scale R, and evolve a nonnegative test backward by the physical-time adjoint transported by bR. In the suitable local-energy inequality the smoothed transport cancels, while the residual u−bR, pressure, and early energy remain.

terminal localized energy + viscous payment ≤ starting localized energy + residual transport + pressure. (B.1–B.5)

The starting time must be a strong right-Lebesgue time in L2. An arbitrary weak terminal representative is allowed through positive-weight weak lower semicontinuity. Its gap must not be re-labelled as a time atom of the local-energy defect measure.

03 / B.6–B.13

The lifted second moment pays linearly in dissipation

Lift the adjoint density to a finite-mass Euclidean representative and compare it with the smoothed path. A stochastic representation, velocity oscillation about its spatial mean, Young's inequality, and Sobolev–Poincare give

Q / (M R²) ≤ C ( 1 + D_J / R ). (B.8)

Q is the lifted second moment, M the mass, and DJ the scale dissipation on the time window. This improves the direct Lipschitz exponential cost, but does not make DJ/R uniformly bounded or small. Chebyshev yields only a mass-tail estimate, not a uniform lower bound for the original moving-ball energy.

04 / B.14–B.16

Periodic Nash height and the direct Hölder gap

The Nash inequality on a fixed three-torus includes a constant-mode correction. Incompressible drift preserves L1 contraction and yields a standard drift-independent height estimate for the adjoint kernel.

||ψ(τ)||∞ ≤ C (1 + τ^{-3/2}) ||ψ(0)||₁. (B.14)

This is a density-height estimate, not centre control. Direct Hölder estimates of residual transport and pressure demand kernel norms that the present energy payments do not automatically close. B.16 identifies that direct-method gap; it does not rule out another compensation mechanism.

05 / C.1–C.6

A true smooth NS shear rules out amplitude- and drift-free moment localization

An exact periodic shear has identically zero nonlinear transport and is therefore a genuine globally smooth unforced three-dimensional Navier–Stokes solution. Fix R and an ordinary smooth terminal time T, then increase amplitude A; the Euclidean lifted second moment has a strictly positive A-squared coefficient.

fixed R,T; all solutions smooth; lifted second moment has a strictly positive A² contribution. (C.1–C.6)

Thus a uniform O(R²) moment bound with no initial-amplitude or drift payment is false. Initial L2 and H1 are both nonuniform. The family does not test a first singular time, prove B.8 optimal, or refute Contract G.
