**R0.73U tensor heat hierarchy.** (A) For the local product tensor
\(T=u\otimes u\) and \(\Theta_s=e^{s\Delta}T\), pressure is reconstructed at
the same heat scale by \(p_s=R_iR_j\Theta_{s,ij}\).  The quadratic state is
even under \(u\mapsto-u\), whereas the cubic transport and
pressure--velocity pieces in its physical-time tangent are odd, so the signed
tangent is not an autonomous function of the even tensor hierarchy alone.
(B) For the smooth four-site witness at \(h_*=(1,2,0)\), the exact cubic
transport matrix \(A\) and pressure--velocity matrix \(B\) sum to
\(K=[[-2,1],[1,0]]\), with \(\|K\|_F=\sqrt6\).  The states generated by
\(u\) and \(-u\) coincide, while their heat-filtered tensor tangents differ by
\(2e^{-5s}K\).  (C) For the dilation \(u_L\), the coefficient separation is
\(D_L(s)=2\sqrt6L e^{-5sL^2}\).  With \(z=\sqrt{s}L\), its normalized profile
is the exact analytic function \(f(z)=ze^{-5z^2}\), whose unique positive
maximum is at \(z=1/\sqrt{10}\).  Thus at a fixed parabolic slice
\(s=\theta L^{-2}\),
\(D_L(s)=2\sqrt{6\theta}e^{-5\theta}s^{-1/2}\), an explicit
coefficient-level one-derivative cost at a fixed slice.  This is an exact
analytic/finite diagnostic, not a simulation, closure model, singularity
example, improved regularity criterion, or Clay result.
