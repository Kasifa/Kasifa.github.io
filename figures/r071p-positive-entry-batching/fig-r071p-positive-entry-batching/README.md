# R0.71P journal figure package

This package archives the finite R0.71P positive-entry batching result. The
measure is formed by taking the soft positive part component by component, or
equivalently by retaining the componentwise relaxed positive-entry atoms,
before the shell-cell sum. It is generally not the positive Jordan part of a
signed aggregate; that later operation can lose cancelling component entries.

Panel A separates the componentwise segmented/soft positive entry `A_+` from
the ordinary hard positive jump `(A_+-A_-)^+`; an even-order touch retains
entry mass one while ordinary hard `V+` has zero atomic mass. Panel B records the exact
finite-overlap model and the theorem-level chain from cell entries to local
support energy and then to the overlap budget. Panel C uses the exact smooth
Hilbert paths `C_N=N^(-1) sin(Nt)e` on the half-open window `[0,2*pi)`:
the left endpoint is included, the right endpoint is excluded, and there are
exactly `N` positive atoms. The ordinary unit-time and first-derivative
budgets are fixed, and denominator mass is `pi/N^2`.
Panel D reconstructs four target Fourier modes of a genuine smooth NSE
initial jet and shows the sharp equality `A_+=||F||_2^2/Y=1/4`.

The archive contains 178 mm vector PDF and SVG exports, a 600 dpi PNG,
self-contained exact and independent certificates, machine-readable plotted
data, producer and independent validators, color and true-grayscale QA
previews, a visual QA report, environment details, a manifest, and SHA-256
hashes.

Panel C is not an NSE trajectory. Panel D is one one-sided initial jet only.
The package does not assert an internal or repeated NSE face construction, a
uniform all-shell/all-cell estimate, an infinite-frame or Leray passage, a
continuation criterion, singularity, global regularity, originality, or a
Millennium-problem result.
