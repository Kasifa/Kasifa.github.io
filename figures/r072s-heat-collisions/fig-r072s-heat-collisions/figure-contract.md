## Question

\(1{:}2{:}3\) incidence, and how do two explicit normalized heat paths realize

type, and the coefficient family is restricted miniversal modulo additive

its unique \(A_2\) event.  A real-even path with nonzero third carrier has
\(4/2/2\) across an \(A_3\) event.  Both crossing-time counts are four when
multiplicity is used.
