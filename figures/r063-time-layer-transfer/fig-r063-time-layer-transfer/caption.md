# Figure R0.63-1 — The quartic transfer closes only after a lifted state system

**English.** (a) The ordinary two-state Rudin--Shapiro recursion is the base
generator.  A cubic coefficient requires all eight `P/Q` products; multiplying
by the target sign gives sixteen states, while coefficient shifts transport
the carries `-1,0,1,2`.  The resulting Gaussian-weighted operator is
non-autonomous across dyadic scales.  (b) At six targets chosen to maximize
the corresponding ordinary unweighted cubic correlation, the exact
long-double heat-kernel path sum keeps `S4/M` between `0.0064` and `0.0191`
through `M=131072`.  (c) The cancellation condition nevertheless reaches
`5.06e4`; these probes are finite stress tests, not a proof of `|S4,m| <= C
L^2 M`.  The exact content is the time-layer factorization and lifted
recursion; the operator norm remains open.

**中文。** (a) 普通 Rudin--Shapiro 符号由两态递推生成，但一个三次系数
必须同时跟踪八个 `P/Q` 乘积；再乘目标符号后自然得到十六态，并同时传递
`-1,0,1,2` 四个系数进位。高斯热权在不同二进制层的限制不同，因此最终
算子是非自治的。(b) 对六个“普通未加权三次相关最坏”的目标，真实热核
路径和在直到 `M=131072` 时仍满足 `S4/M` 位于 `0.0064` 到 `0.0191`。
(c) 消去条件数同时升至 `5.06e4`，所以这些结果只是有限压力测试，不能证明
`|S4,m| <= C L^2 M`。当前精确定理是分层因式分解和提升递推；算子范数仍
待解决。
