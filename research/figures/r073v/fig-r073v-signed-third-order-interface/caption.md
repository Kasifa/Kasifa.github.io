**R0.73V pressure-aware signed third-order interface.** (A) Let
\(N=\mathbb P\nabla\!\cdot(u\otimes u)\),
\(\mathcal C_s=P_s(u\odot N)\), and
\(\chi_s=\mathcal C_s-v_s\odot N_s\). For the smooth four-site
witness and \(h_*=(1,2,0)\), the two independent exact producers give
\(\widehat{\mathcal C_s}=-q^5K\),
\(\widehat{v_s\odot N_s}=-q^3K\), and
\(\widehat\chi_s=(q^3-q^5)K\), where
\(q=e^{-s}\) and
\(K=[[-2,1,0],[1,0,0],[0,0,0]]\). Thus the sign-pair difference
is \(2(q^3-q^5)K\). (B) At the same mode, the contracted third
cumulant flux equals
\(q^3(1-q^2)^2(q^2+2)[[2,-3],[-3,4]]\), while pressure diffusion
and pressure strain are respectively
\(q^3(1-q^2)[[4,2],[2,-8]]\) and
\(q^3(1-q^2)[[-4,0],[0,4]]\). Their combined pressure matrix is
\(q^3(1-q^2)[[0,2],[2,-4]]\). The exact small-s orders are
two versus one, so absorbing this displayed pressure coefficient into the
displayed cumulant-flux coefficient costs at least \(s^{-1}\) near the
bottom scale. Panel B prints the active 2 by 2 block only; the omitted third
row and third column are exactly zero in every displayed 3 by 3 coefficient.
This statement is coefficientwise. (C) For the six-site
witness at \(h=0\), the contracted cumulant flux and pressure diffusion
vanish, but
\(\widehat\Xi(0)=(1-q^4)\operatorname{diag}(-48,48,0)\). This is a
same-output-coefficient witness, not a whole-field information collision.
(D) For the four-site witness, the selected nonlinear physical-time tangent is
\(2iq^2(1-q^2)^2\) at \(\kappa_{112}\) and mode
\((0,2,0)\); an independent finite-\(\varepsilon\) extraction
at \(q=1/2\) gives \(9i/32\). Under
\(u_L(x)=u(Lx)\) and \(s=\theta L^{-2}\), the coefficient is
\(2iL e^{-2\theta}(1-e^{-2\theta})^2\). The plotted line is a
deterministic rendering of this closed formula, not a fit. All conclusions
are exact finite/coefficientwise statements; no closure theorem, singularity,
global regularity, or Clay conclusion follows.
