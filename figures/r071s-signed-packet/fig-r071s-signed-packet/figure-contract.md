# R0.71S figure contract

- **Question:** can a signed or separable bilinear parabolic packet avoid both
  inverse-height sampling and sequential Gram growth?
- **Panel A:** show the exact \(K^2/\theta\) diagonal required to reproduce
  constant data with an \(L^2\)-normalized box packet, together with the
  \(K^{-2}\)-weighted strong-ledger coefficient \(1/\theta\).
- **Panel B:** for \(N=64\) same-direction box packets, show the computed
  \(\lambda_{\max}\) between the exact Rayleigh lower bound
  \(p-(p^2-1)/(3N)\) and row-sum upper bound \(p\).
- **Panel C:** show the exact adjoint-heat constant
  \((\nu K^2/2)\coth(\nu\theta/2)\) and its \(K^{-2}\)-weighted constant in
  the frozen-denominator linear model. This panel does not estimate the
  \(Y_t/(2Y)\) term in the fully normalized NSE observable.
- **Panel D:** show the soft even-touch positive and Jordan masses approaching
  one and two, while the signed precursor and a zero-mean separable detector
  remain zero.
- **Boundary:** panels A--C are exact packet or linear models. Panel D is not
  an NSE trajectory. For the separately certified covariant family
  \(a_K=a_0K\), the genuine NSE information is limited to the initial-face
  identities \(A_+\sim K^2\), \(K^{-2}A_+=a_0^2/4\), and the \(K^{-2}\)
  bare Leray time-integral scale. It is not plotted as positive-time
  evolution.
