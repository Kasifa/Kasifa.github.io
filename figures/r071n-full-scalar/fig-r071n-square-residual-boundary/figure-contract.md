# Figure contract

## Analytical question

Does the complete fixed-cell scalar acquire a coercive positive term after
the square--residual decomposition and the local filtered-enstrophy identity
are combined?

## Takeaway

The complete scalar has an exact nonnegative-square plus signed-residual
form.  The same square cancels when the local filtered-enstrophy acceleration
is substituted, leaving a signed second jet.  Two smooth finite-Fourier NSE
initial-jet diagnostics have positive \(z_Q\) and opposite signs of
\(\mathcal J_Q\).  Every surviving numerator row remains at the critical
scaling order; R0.71O next audits the hard/soft denominator face.
