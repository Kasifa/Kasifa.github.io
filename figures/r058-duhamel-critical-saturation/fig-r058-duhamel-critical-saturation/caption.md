**Figure R0.58-1. Exact Duhamel denominator and critical-space saturation.**
(a) At the parabolic observation time \(t_L=(\log2)/(2L^2)\), the exact
low-frequency Duhamel coefficient satisfies \(d_L(t_L)\asymp L^{-1}\), and
the scaled coefficient converges to a positive Riemann integral. (b) The
certified bilinear quotient decays as \(L^{-2}\), \(L^{-1}\), and \(L^{-3}\)
in the fixed-output block, Fourier \(\mathcal X^{-1}\), and
\(\dot H^{1/2}\) tests, respectively, but has shell-uniform positive lower
bounds in the heat \(\dot B^{-1}_{\infty,\infty}\) and periodic
\(BMO^{-1}\) norms. (c) Rudin--Shapiro signs keep the high-shell spatial
maximum at \(O(\sqrt L)\), whereas aligned signs give \(L\); the matched
nonlinear products still satisfy \(a_n^2=1\) and remain coherent. The formal
claims are all-index analytic statements. Plotted exponentials and sampled
unit-circle maxima are deterministic presentation data, not proof. This is a
critical bilinear saturation result, not norm inflation, higher-iterate
control, or a three-dimensional Navier--Stokes regularity theorem.
