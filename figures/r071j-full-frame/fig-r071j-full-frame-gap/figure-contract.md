# Figure contract

## Analytical question

After taking shellwise positive parts, can cancellation across every parent
shell convert the available heat volume into the weighted positive-creation
budget, or does a two-frequency-power gap remain?

## Takeaway

The positive-part identity telescopes only the endpoint derivative. The
selected global-smooth 2D3C family supplies a positive parent-shell pulse,
and the parent-only full-frame lower coefficient scales as \(K^{-2}\), while
the heat upper bound scales as \(K^{-4}\). Thus a frequency-uniform payment
of this positive-creation term by this heat volume fails in the stated
parent-frame/global-cell scope.
