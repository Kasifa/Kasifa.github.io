CB.6｜固定球集中之后，还缺少什么

已核验的固定球文献输入可以转移到固定尺度的原路径，并经对角化得到解依赖的慢缩半径；但这不是预设幂律，也不是一条可直接代入原合同的变尺度路径。两个反模型进一步标定局部持留命题需要支付的初值、远场压力、成熟时间与动力学成本。

01 / 结论地图

集中、路径和持留是三道不同的门

集中在实际奇点附近，每个固定内点球的局部 L³ 范数发散；周期应用保留文献解释条件。
路径固定半径可跟随原指定路径；缩球只能先得到解依赖、阶梯式的慢对角半径。
持留从一个终点的 L³ 大值反推整个短窗口，需要初值、远场、成熟时间或其他动力学支付。

因此，新节点没有把固定球集中改写成 Clay-B 所需的变尺度移动管结论。它建立的是可安全使用的最强已证接口，并用两个不同层级的反模型排除过强捷径。

02 / L.1–L.4

固定球文献输入保留显式排字解释

Albritton–Barker, Theorem 1.1, arXiv v2 给出实际奇点处的固定球内点 L³ 集中。周期情形通过坐标球限制并取空边界部分 Γ=∅ 使用。其 Definition 2.1(1) 原页排成 overline Ω' ⊂ Ω ⊂ Γ；本页按同一定义第 (3) 项、Proposition 2.2 与标准局部域关系理解为 overline Ω' ⊂ Ω ∪ Γ。

For every fixed interior ball B_r(x*), limsup_(t↑T*) ||u(t)||_(L³(B_r(x*))) = ∞. (L.4)

这项使用是 LITERATURE CONDITIONAL：它不是作者发布的勘误，也不是本站重新证明了原定理。它只提供固定球结论，不提供规定幂律的缩球、指定移动中心或原路径柱结论。

03 / P.1–P.5 与 D.1–D.8

固定尺度进入原路径；缩尺度只能解依赖地对角化

在能量界 M 和固定半径 R 下，原指定中心在短时 h 内的漂移满足

|X_R(T*−h)−x*| ≤ C_φ M R^(−3/2) h. (P.5)

故固定球集中可转移到 B_R(X_R(T*−h))。再对任意固定 α<2/5 作慢对角选择，可构造阶梯式半径 ρ(h)→0，使 ρ(h)≥h^α、局部 L³ 集中仍发散，且归一化路径漂移趋零。

ρ(h) ≥ h^α, ||u(T*−h)||_(L³(B_(ρ(h))(x*))) → ∞, |X_(ρ(h))(T*−h)−x*| / ρ(h) → 0. (D.6–D.8)

ρ 依赖具体解与固定球发散速度，通常只是阶梯函数；它不是预设幂律，不是一条可微的变尺度路径，也不给出完整小量 E_R 或 G/G-P/G-C。

04 / I.1–I.3 与 M.1–N.6

端点发散不强迫免费持留；精确能量模型仍不是 NS

L_t^4L_x^3 的有限性与端点 L³ 发散相容，所以纯时间可积性不提供统一持留。冻结源进一步构造一个满足精确全局能量等式、有限耗散、固定球集中和常原路径的逻辑模型，同时每个合法核心能量都可保持在阈值之上。

这个模型故意不满足 Navier–Stokes。对假设存在的压力取 curl，再用一阶矩积分，得到非零向量必须为零的矛盾；因此没有任何压力可以把它变成无外力 NS 解。

exact energy equality + fixed-ball concentration + every legal-scale core energy above threshold + curl/moment obstruction to the NS equation. (M/N)

它只说明“能量等式 + 浓度”在逻辑上还不够，绝不能称为 NS 反例、正则性反例或 Clay 反例。

05 / AA.1–AA.15

真正平滑 NS 族否定无成本的全窗口持留

在黏性为一、无外力的周期 Navier–Stokes 中，远处平滑散度自由初值通过压力产生局部加速度：局部场先被抵消，随后反向增大。经过精确缩放，可取

s_B = b B^(−8/5), t_B = (b+a) B^(−8/5), ||u_B(s_B)||_(L³(B_r)) = O(B^(−1/5)), ||u_B(t_B)||_(L³(B_r)) ≍ a G_0 B^(2/5), (t_B−s_B)/t_B → 2/11. (AA.11–AA.15)

于是不存在只依赖通用常数、而完全不支付初值或外部压力成本的“所有光滑窗口”AA.1 型 L³ 持留定理。该族的初始能量随 B 增长，并且 t_B/r²→0；它不否定成熟时间 t≥Cr² 的版本，也不否定靠近某个固定解首次奇点的版本。

06 / AA.16–AA.18

固定解的远源压力冲量可单独支付

对固定能量 M、固定球半径 r，把 B_(3r) 外源产生的压力分出后，内球上的 Calderón–Zygmund 远场估计给出

||∇q_far||_(L³(B_(2r))) ≤ C r^(−3) M², (∫_(t−δ)^t ||∇q_far||_(L³(B_(2r))) ds) / ||u(t)||_(L³(B_r)) ≤ C c_0 M² r^(−1) L^(−5), δ=c_0 r² L^(−4). (AA.16–AA.18)

AA.18 只是裸远源压力冲量。它不是速度加权压力功，也不控制近源压力、非线性输运、Δu、黏性变化或原移动路径上的完整局部能量。

CB.6 | What remains after fixed-ball concentration

The verified fixed-ball literature input transfers to the original path at each fixed scale and, by diagonalization, to solution-dependent slowly shrinking radii. It does not provide a prescribed power law or a variable-scale path that can be inserted directly into the original contract. Two different countermodels then identify the initial-data, far-pressure, mature-time, and dynamical costs required by a local persistence statement.

The literature input retains its typographical interpretation and LITERATURE CONDITIONAL status. The exact-energy countermodel is not an NS solution. The genuine smooth periodic NS family rules out only a cost-free all-smooth-window statement; its initial energy grows and t_B/r²→0. AA.18 is a bare far-source pressure impulse, not velocity-weighted pressure work. Near pressure, viscosity, nonlinear transport, and the first-singularity version remain OPEN. NOT CLAY. No novelty claim.

01 / Result map

Concentration, path control, and persistence are three distinct gates

ConcentrationThe local L³ norm diverges in every fixed interior ball near an actual singularity; the periodic application retains a literature-interpretation condition.

The new node therefore does not rewrite fixed-ball concentration as the variable-scale moving-tube statement needed by Clay B. It isolates the strongest safe interface and uses two countermodels of different strength to eliminate overstrong shortcuts.

02 / L.1–L.4

The fixed-ball literature input retains an explicit typographical interpretation

Albritton–Barker, Theorem 1.1, arXiv v2 gives fixed-ball interior L³ concentration at an actual singularity. The periodic application uses a coordinate-ball restriction and Γ=∅. Definition 2.1(1) in the printed v2 reads overline Ω' ⊂ Ω ⊂ Γ; this page interprets it as the standard overline Ω' ⊂ Ω ∪ Γ, consistently with part (3) of the same definition, Proposition 2.2, and the usual local-domain relation.

For every fixed interior ball B_r(x*), limsup_(t↑T*) ||u(t)||_(L³(B_r(x*))) = ∞. (L.4)

This use is LITERATURE CONDITIONAL. It is not an author-issued erratum and not a local reproof of the theorem. It supplies only a fixed-ball conclusion, not a prescribed shrinking power law, a prescribed moving centre, or an original-path cylinder.

03 / P.1–P.5 and D.1–D.8

Fixed scales enter the original path; shrinking scales are only solution-dependent diagonals

Under energy bound M and at fixed radius R, the original prescribed centre satisfies the short-time drift estimate

|X_R(T*−h)−x*| ≤ C_φ M R^(−3/2) h. (P.5)

Fixed-ball concentration therefore transfers to B_R(X_R(T*−h)). For any fixed α<2/5, a slow diagonal choice then constructs a stepwise radius ρ(h)→0 such that ρ(h)≥h^α, concentration still diverges, and normalized path drift tends to zero.

ρ(h) ≥ h^α, ||u(T*−h)||_(L³(B_(ρ(h))(x*))) → ∞, |X_(ρ(h))(T*−h)−x*| / ρ(h) → 0. (D.6–D.8)

The radius depends on the particular solution and on its fixed-ball divergence rate. It is generally stepwise, not a prescribed power law, not a differentiable variable-scale path, and it yields neither a small full E_R nor G/G-P/G-C.

04 / I.1–I.3 and M.1–N.6

Endpoint divergence does not force free persistence; the exact-energy model is still not NS

Finite L_t^4L_x^3 is compatible with endpoint L³ divergence, so time integrability alone supplies no uniform persistence. The frozen source also builds a logical model with exact global energy equality, finite dissipation, fixed-ball concentration, and a constant original path, while every legal-scale core energy can stay above threshold.

The construction intentionally fails the Navier–Stokes equation. Taking curl under the assumption that a pressure exists, and then integrating a first moment, forces a nonzero vector to vanish. Thus no pressure can turn it into an unforced NS solution.

exact energy equality + fixed-ball concentration + every legal-scale core energy above threshold + curl/moment obstruction to the NS equation. (M/N)

It shows only that “energy equality plus concentration” is logically insufficient. It is not an NS, regularity, or Clay counterexample.

05 / AA.1–AA.15

A genuine smooth NS family rejects cost-free all-window persistence

For viscosity-one unforced periodic Navier–Stokes, smooth divergence-free data far from the observation ball create a local pressure acceleration. The local field is first cancelled and then grows with the opposite sign. After exact scaling, one obtains

s_B = b B^(−8/5), t_B = (b+a) B^(−8/5), ||u_B(s_B)||_(L³(B_r)) = O(B^(−1/5)), ||u_B(t_B)||_(L³(B_r)) ≍ a G_0 B^(2/5), (t_B−s_B)/t_B → 2/11. (AA.11–AA.15)

Hence no AA.1-type persistence theorem can hold on every smooth window with universal constants and no initial-data or exterior-pressure cost. The family has growing initial energy and t_B/r²→0. It does not refute a mature-time version with t≥Cr², or a version restricted to the first singularity of one fixed solution.

06 / AA.16–AA.18

The far-source pressure impulse for one fixed solution can be paid separately

For fixed energy M and fixed ball radius r, split off pressure generated outside B_(3r). The inner-ball Calderón–Zygmund far-field estimate gives

||∇q_far||_(L³(B_(2r))) ≤ C r^(−3) M², (∫_(t−δ)^t ||∇q_far||_(L³(B_(2r))) ds) / ||u(t)||_(L³(B_r)) ≤ C c_0 M² r^(−1) L^(−5), δ=c_0 r² L^(−4). (AA.16–AA.18)

AA.18 controls only the bare far-source pressure impulse. It is not velocity-weighted pressure work and does not control near-source pressure, nonlinear transport, Δu, viscous change, or full local energy along the original moving path.
