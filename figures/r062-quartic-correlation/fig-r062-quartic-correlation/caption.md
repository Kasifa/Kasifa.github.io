# Figure R0.62-1 — Heat-weighted quartic profiles and the unweighted correlation gap

**English.** (a) Complete heat-weighted quartic target profiles at `L=1` for
four dyadic output counts.  All 3,840 displayed values are positive in the
finite scan, and their maxima remain at scale `10^-3`; the logarithmic axis
also preserves the much smaller low-target values.  (b) The maximum over all
targets does not show growth from `M=256` through `M=2048`.  The horizontal
line is the earlier overall finite maximum at `(L,M,m)=(4,64,64)`.  (c) In
contrast, the maximum three-carry coefficient of the ordinary, unweighted
outer Rudin--Shapiro convolution, divided by `M`, grows along selected dyadic
levels through `M=1,048,576`.  This does not contradict the heat-weighted
data: it shows that replacing the simplex heat kernel by its supremum loses
the cancellation needed for a uniform theorem.  R0.62 proves the all-index
ceiling `|R_{L,M,m}| <= 7.8343 (m/M)^2 sqrt(M)`; the plots are finite evidence
and do not prove positivity or a bound independent of `M`.

**中文。** (a) 给出 `L=1` 时四个二进制输出数的完整热核加权四次目标
剖面。有限扫描中的 3,840 个值全部为正，最大值保持在 `10^-3` 量级；
对数纵轴同时保留了低目标处更小的数值。(b) 从 `M=256` 到 `M=2048`，
全部目标中的最大值没有呈现增长；水平线是较早在 `(L,M,m)=(4,64,64)`
取得的全体有限观测最大值。(c) 与此不同，普通未加权外层
Rudin--Shapiro 三进位卷积的最大系数除以 `M` 后，在直到
`M=1,048,576` 的部分二进制尺度上仍会增长。这与热核加权结果不矛盾，
而是说明不能先用热核上确界替代热核，再依赖一个普通相关系数来证明一致
界。R0.62 已证明全指标上界
`|R_{L,M,m}| <= 7.8343 (m/M)^2 sqrt(M)`；图中正性和尺度行为仍只是有限
证据，并未证明独立于 `M` 的界。
