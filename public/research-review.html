The source ratio is not an energy-transfer fraction. Gradient amplification, infinite regeneration, and finite-time singularity are not established.

01 / Formal statement

这个问题要求证明什么The problem

先固定方程、定义域、初值和解的概念；改变条件后得到的结果，只能作为相关问题讨论。The equation, domain, initial data and solution class must be fixed first. Results under different assumptions concern related problems.

∂tu + (u · ∇)u = −∇p + νΔu + f，　∇ · u = 0，　u(0) = u0

u 是三维速度，p 是压力，ν > 0 为黏性系数，f 是外力。Clay 正面命题 A、B 分别在全空间 ℝ³ 和周期空间 ℝ³/ℤ³ 中取 f = 0，要求对任意满足正式衰减或周期条件的光滑无散初值，存在全局光滑解。Here u is velocity, p is pressure, ν > 0 is viscosity and f is an external force. The positive Clay statements A and B concern ℝ³ and ℝ³/ℤ³ with f = 0, respectively. They require global smooth solutions for every smooth divergence-free initial datum satisfying the stated decay or periodicity conditions.

负面命题 C、D 允许符合正式光滑性及衰减条件的外力，要求给出不存在正式要求的全局光滑解的例子。弱解非唯一性、修改方程或特殊模型中的增长，不能直接替代这些要求。The negative statements C and D allow forcing satisfying the official smoothness and decay conditions, and ask for an example without a global smooth solution of the required kind. Weak nonuniqueness, modified equations and growth in special models do not by themselves meet those requirements.

Fefferman · Clay Mathematics Institute · Official statement

全空间命题 A 还要求全时间有界的总动能；周期命题 B 要求速度保持空间周期性。负面命题 C 使用全空间快速衰减条件，D 使用空间周期与相应时间衰减条件。准确量词及函数类别以官方表述为准。Statement A additionally requires uniformly bounded total kinetic energy for all time; B requires spatial periodicity of the velocity. C uses whole-space rapid-decay conditions, whereas D uses spatial periodicity and the prescribed time decay. The official statement specifies the exact quantifiers and function classes.

尺度与临界性Scaling and criticality

uλ(x,t) = λu(λx,λ²t)，　pλ(x,t) = λ²p(λx,λ²t)

上述全空间缩放保持无外力方程的形式。L³ 速度范数以及满足 2/q + 3/p = 1 的 LtqLxp 范数不变，因而是临界量。L² 能量范数在高频缩放下变小：能量有界并不排除更小尺度上的集中。固定环面并不具有任意连续空间缩放对称性。This whole-space scaling preserves the unforced equation. The velocity L³ norm and mixed norms with 2/q + 3/p = 1 are invariant and hence critical. The L² norm decreases under high-frequency scaling, so bounded energy alone does not exclude concentration at smaller scales. A fixed torus does not admit arbitrary continuous spatial rescaling.

02 / State of knowledge

目前已经知道什么State of knowledge

全局弱解存在Global weak solutions

Leray–Hopf 理论为有限能量初值给出全局弱解与能量不等式。弱解存在与一般光滑初值的全局光滑延拓是不同结论。Leray–Hopf theory gives global weak solutions and an energy inequality for finite-energy initial data. Weak existence is distinct from global smooth continuation for general smooth initial data.

光滑解局部存在Local smooth solutions

足够光滑的初值产生唯一的局部强解。要得到任意时间的光滑性，还必须排除有限时间失去正则性的可能。Sufficiently smooth data generate a unique local strong solution. Global smoothness additionally requires ruling out loss of regularity in finite time.

小临界数据的全局理论Small critical data

Fujita–Kato、Kato 和 Koch–Tataru 的理论分别在相应临界空间中给出小数据全局适定性。不能把适当临界范数的小量条件简化成“动能小”。The theories of Fujita–Kato, Kato and Koch–Tataru establish small-data global well-posedness in their respective critical spaces. Smallness in an appropriate critical norm cannot be replaced by small kinetic energy.

二维与特殊结构Two dimensions and special structure

二维全局光滑性已知成立；一些具有附加结构的三维数据也有全局结果。它们不覆盖所有允许的三维光滑初值。Global smoothness is known in two dimensions, and global results also exist for some three-dimensional data with additional structure. These do not cover all admissible smooth three-dimensional data.

Clay · Existence, smoothness and weak solutions

Koch–Tataru · Critical small-data theory

能量与涡量：一个成立，一个不闭合Energy and vorticity: an identity and an unresolved estimate

对无外力光滑解，在周期域或满足适当衰减的全空间中，基本能量恒等式为：For smooth unforced solutions on a periodic domain or with suitable whole-space decay, the energy identity is:

½‖u(t)‖₂² + ν∫₀ᵗ‖∇u(s)‖₂² ds = ½‖u₀‖₂²

令 ω = ∇ × u。涡量方程中的 (ω · ∇)u 是三维涡量拉伸项；在二维中这一项消失。Let ω = ∇ × u. The term (ω · ∇)u represents three-dimensional vortex stretching; it vanishes in two dimensions.

∂tω + (u · ∇)ω = (ω · ∇)u + νΔω
½ d‖ω‖₂²/dt + ν‖∇ω‖₂² = ∫(Sω) · ω dx，　S = (∇u + ∇uᵀ)/2

右侧没有固定符号，基本能量估计不能直接将它闭合。这是三维困难的重要来源。The right-hand side has no fixed sign and is not directly controlled by the basic energy estimate. This is a central three-dimensional difficulty.

部分正则性与条件正则性Partial and conditional regularity

CKN 部分正则性CKN partial regularity

适当弱解的奇异集具有零的一维抛物 Hausdorff 测度。这不等于证明奇异集为空。For suitable weak solutions, the singular set has zero one-dimensional parabolic Hausdorff measure. This does not prove that the singular set is empty.

Serrin 与 L³ 端点Serrin and the L³ endpoint

在标准弱解与初值假设下，满足 2/q + 3/p ≤ 1、p > 3 的速度时空可积条件给出正则性。ESS 处理关键 Lt∞Lx3 端点；这些条件不是能量不等式自动给出的。Under the standard weak-solution and initial-data assumptions, velocity integrability with 2/q + 3/p ≤ 1 and p > 3 yields regularity. ESS addresses the critical Lt∞Lx3 endpoint. The energy inequality does not supply these conditions automatically.

04 / Core difficulty

困难可以落到几个具体点上The main difficulties

主要困难不在于写出更多条件，而在于从原方程演化本身推出足够强的控制。The central difficulty is not merely to state more conditions, but to derive sufficiently strong control from the equation’s own evolution.

能量超临界Supercritical energy

总能量有界不排除向越来越小的尺度集中。临界正则性判据要求的控制通常强于能量估计。Bounded total energy does not exclude concentration at ever smaller scales. Critical regularity criteria generally require stronger control than the energy estimate provides.

涡量拉伸无定号Indefinite vortex stretching

应变可以放大涡量。粗略取绝对值可能丢失几何抵消，却仍不足以给出全局先验界。Strain can amplify vorticity. Taking absolute values may lose geometric cancellations while still failing to provide a global a priori bound.

压力非局部Nonlocal pressure

无外力无散情形中，−Δp = ∂ᵢ∂ⱼ(uᵢuⱼ)。局部化不能忽略压力的远场影响。In the unforced divergence-free setting, −Δp = ∂ᵢ∂ⱼ(uᵢuⱼ). Localization must account for the far-field contribution of pressure.

临界尺度的紧致性Compactness at critical scales

平移与缩放可能使极限过程失去紧致性。排除某一固定自相似形状，不等于排除所有潜在奇性。Translations and rescalings can destroy compactness in limiting arguments. Excluding a fixed self-similar shape is not the same as excluding every possible singularity.

平均模型是一个必要对照：若一个论证只使用能量抵消及对修改模型也成立的粗估计，还必须检查它是否错误地同时排除了已知的模型爆破。The averaged model is an important comparison: an argument using only energy cancellation and coarse estimates shared by that model must be checked against the model’s known blowup.
