# Figure contract

## Analytical question

After taking the soft positive part component by component - equivalently,
after forming the componentwise relaxed positive-entry measure - which part
of the R0.71P sum is paid by fixed spatial overlap, and which temporal
quantity remains? This measure is generally not the positive Jordan part of
a signed shell-cell aggregate.

## Takeaway

The componentwise segmented/soft ledger retains an even-order positive entry
that ordinary hard positive variation misses. Taking a positive Jordan part
only after signed aggregation can therefore delete mass and defines a
different observable. Bounded cutoff overlap batches every entry at one
common time without a cell-count loss. The remaining multiplicity is the
number of distinct entry times: on `[0,2*pi)`, with the left endpoint included
and the right endpoint excluded, an abstract smooth path has exactly `N`
positive atoms while ordinary first-time budgets stay bounded and denominator
mass decays. One exact smooth NSE initial jet saturates the one-cell
projection constant, but supplies no internal or repeated NSE face theorem.
