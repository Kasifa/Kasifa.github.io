# Figure R0.73Z-1 | Covariance separation, endpoint obstruction, and a pressure-active kernel

**Panel A.** For the exact one-mode shear
\(u^{(n)}=e^{-\nu n^2t}\sin(nx_2)e_1\), the normalized Leray energy is
\(4+2=6\), independently of \(n\), while the proved initial-endpoint
\(D_{ii,s}^{3/2}\) lower bound contains the unbounded frequency factor
\(n\).  The plotted ordinate is that factor after suppressing the fixed,
positive geometry constant \(c\); it is not the value of the integral.

**Panel B.** For \(N_j=8^j\) and \(a_j=2^{-j}=N_j^{-1/3}\), the exact
partial coefficient energy is
\(S_J=\sum_{j\le J}a_j^2=(1-4^{-J})/3\to1/3\), whereas every lower-bound
unit satisfies \(a_j^3N_j=1\), so the partial lower sum is exactly \(J\).

**Panel C.** For the crossed exact Navier--Stokes family at
\(A=B=n=1\), \(t=t_*\), and
\(x_1=x_2=\pi/3\), both production channels vanish for every \(s>0\):
\(\Pi_s=\mathscr S_s=0\).  The plotted analytic formulas are
\(D_s=(1-r^2)(1+r^2/2)\),
\(|Q_s|=(\sqrt6/8)(r^3-r^5)\), and
\(\nabla\cdot Q_s=(3/4)(r^3-r^5)\), with \(r=e^{-s}\); all three are
strictly positive for every finite \(s>0\).
