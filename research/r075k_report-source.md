### Positive off-diagonal semigroup methods

### Pathwise shear diffusion

Gardner, Liss, and Mattingly use pathwise stochastic techniques for
passive scalars advected by shear.  Their work confirms that stochastic
representations can retain dynamical information beyond a raw energy
identity.  Their geometry and estimate are different, and no theorem from
