精确尺度相消与收缩定位的冲突

热滤波给出非负带能量和精确的有符号尺度预算，但逐尺度缩小的空间球、移动中心、时间窗口与临界权重会产生明确失配。若强制非负热对偶测试完全零失配，它会向粗尺度扩散而不是收缩定位；纯振幅重标得到的“小量”也不能普适替代原同尺度移动能量。

本页只记录一个有边界的解析结果：不推进 R0 主序列，不是正则性证明，不声称经典 SGS 方法为新发现。合同 G 仍 OPEN。NOT CLAY.

02 / F.1--F.4

嵌套热滤波：全局望远镜与局部余项

令 Hs=exp(sΔ)，br=Hr²u，bR=HR²u，d=R²-r²。热半群嵌套给出 bR=Hdbr，并由 Jensen 不等式得到非负带能量

k_{r,R} = 1/2 ( H_d |b_r|² - |b_R|² ) ≥ 0. (F.1)

局部化并不保持这一差分的纯望远镜结构。对任意空间 cutoff χ，精确式多出

∫χ k_{r,R} = 1/2∫χ(|b_r|²-|b_R|²) + 1/2∫(H_dχ-χ)|b_r|². (F.2)

当 χ=1 时余项消失；沿 Rn=θⁿR0 的有限尺度链，

Σ_{n=0}^{N-1} ∫k_{R_{n+1},R_n} = 1/2 ( ||b_N||₂² - ||b_0||₂² ) ≤ 1/2 ||u||₂². (F.4)

但局部正则性门槛含 Rn-1 型临界归一化及依尺度变化的时间窗和中心。有限总和本身不能推出归一化后的好尺度。

03 / S.1--S.7

固定尺度的完整有符号带能量预算

对滤波速度 bρ、压力 pρ 与应力 τρ，采用标准 SGS 符号 Πρ 和完整输运 Jρ。精确滤波方程的能量式为

∂_t e_ρ + ∇·J_ρ = -D_ρ - Π_ρ. (S.3)

在两个嵌套热尺度之间定义 kr,R、qr,R≥0 和 Lr,R=HdJr-JR。相减得到

∂_t k_{r,R} + ∇·L_{r,R} = Π_R - H_d Π_r - q_{r,R}. (S.5)

对非负光滑时空测试 χ，在 [s,t] 上的完整预算是

⟨χ(t),k(t)⟩ + ∫⟨χ,q⟩ = ⟨χ(s),k(s)⟩ + ∫[⟨∂_tχ,k⟩ + ⟨∇χ,L⟩ + ⟨χ,Π_R-H_dΠ_r⟩]. (S.6)

这同时保留两个时间端点、时间 cutoff、空间运输、压力、应力、黏性运输和移动中心功。k 与 q 的非负性不赋予通量差固定符号。固定正平滑尺度的 Leray-Hopf 端点可由平滑场的强时间连续性处理，但没有未经证明的一致 R→0 极限。

04 / S.8--S.15

逐尺度相加后，内部失配以完整单尺度预算返回

每一层允许自己的时空测试 χn。定义内部失配

A_m = H_{m-1}χ_{m-1} - χ_m, 1 ≤ m < N. (S.8)

有限尺度层和中，端点、通量、运输、时间导数和耗散都产生相应 Am 项。它们恰好组合成单尺度能量恒等式

[⟨A_m,e_m⟩]_s^t + ∫⟨A_m,D_m⟩ = ∫[⟨∂_tA_m,e_m⟩ + ⟨∇A_m,J_m⟩ - ⟨A_m,Π_m⟩]. (S.14)

因此不能只取消有符号通量项，同时免费丢掉时间、空间运输、耗散和端点支付。Am 可变号，S.14 是固定平滑尺度等式，不是把变号测试塞入原始局部能量不等式。

加入临界权重 wn=Rn-1 后，真正的失配变成

A_m^(w) = w_{m-1} H_{m-1}χ_{m-1} - w_m χ_m. (S.15)

即使无权失配为零，加权失配也未必为零；空间形状、中心、时间窗口与临界权重是四个独立来源。

05 / T.1--T.3

非负零失配热对偶不能同时保持真实收缩定位

若从非负非零 χ0 出发并强制逐层零失配，

χ_{n+1} = H_{d_n}χ_n, χ_n = H_{R_0²-R_n²}χ_0, ∫χ_n = ∫χ_0 = M > 0. (T.1-T.2)

质量守恒立即排除 ∫χn≤CRn³。更强地，对任意中心 xn 与固定 c>0，

∫_{B_{cR_n}(x_n)} χ_n / M ≤ (||χ_0||∞/M) (4π/3)c³R_n³ → 0. (T.3)

概率形状向粗尺度扩散，并不聚焦到指定中心。若各层还要求支撑在收缩时间窗中，同一实际时刻的非零质量也与足够细层必须为零相冲突。这只否定指定的非负零失配设计，不否定显式支付 ∂tAm 的方法。

06 / T.4--T.6

振幅重标产生 θ²，但观测本身自动退化

将热权乘以 (Rn/R0)³，记 an=∫χ̂nen、ℓn=∫χ̂nkn≥0、Bn=Rn-1an，则

B_{n+1} = θ² B_n + θ² R_n^{-1}ℓ_n. (T.5)

θ² 不是单独的收缩上界，因为右侧仍有未支付的非负源项。并且对任意固定时刻的 L² 场，

B_n ≤ (||χ_0||∞ / (2R_0³)) R_n² ||u||₂² → 0. (T.6)

变小的是振幅，不是归一化后的空间形状。这种自动小性未经独立比较，不能被命名为原同尺度移动能量的小性。

07 / T.7--T.12

统一初始 L² 的真实光滑 NS 家族排除普适转移

固定非负非零 f∈Cc∞(B1⊂R²) 与 κ>0。对足够小 R，把二维热流周期化并作均值扣除，定义

u^(R)(t,x) = (0,0,V_R(t,x_1,x_2)), p^(R)=0, V_R = (κ/R) Σ_{m∈Z²} (e^{(t/R²)Δ}f)((x_h+2πm)/R) - κRM_f/(2π)². (T.7)

因为 (u·∇)u=VR∂3u=0，这是真实三维周期、无外力、黏性 1 的全时光滑 Navier-Stokes 解，而不是抽象能量序列。其初值满足

||u^(R)(0)||₂² ≤ 2πκ²||f||₂², ||∇u^(R)(0)||₂² = 2πκ²R^{-2}||∇f||₂². (T.8)

所以初始 L² 一致有界，H¹ 不一致。取普通光滑终点 TR=128R²，Gaussian 下界和移动路径的竖直平移不变性给出

E_R(T_R,0) ≥ κ²πc_f²/24 > 0, (T.11) B_R[u^(R)(T_R)] ≤ (πκ²||f||₂²/R_0³) R² → 0. (T.12)

因此不存在统一有限 C 使 ER≤CBR；也不能由 BR→0 普适推出 ER→0，或使用同一连续趋零模数。不同 R 对应不同解与不同 TR，这正是被检验全称量词的一部分。

Exact scale cancellation conflicts with shrinking localization

Heat filtering gives a nonnegative band energy and an exact signed scale budget, but scale-dependent balls, moving centres, time windows, and critical weights create explicit mismatches. If a nonnegative heat-dual test is forced to have zero mismatch, it diffuses toward the coarse scale instead of localizing at shrinking scales; smallness created only by amplitude rescaling cannot universally replace the original same-scale moving energy.

This page records one bounded analytic result. It does not advance the R0 series, is not a regularity proof, and makes no novelty claim for classical SGS methods. Contract G remains OPEN. NOT CLAY.

A family of true, unforced, globally smooth periodic three-dimensional Navier-Stokes solutions is also constructed. Its initial L2 norm is uniformly bounded but its initial H1 norm is not. At TR=128R² the rescaled heat observable tends to zero while the original same-scale moving energy stays bounded below. The excluded comparison is therefore one that is uniform over solutions, admissible times, and scales and depends only on the common initial-L2 bound.

02 / F.1--F.4

Nested heat filters: global telescoping and a local remainder

Let Hs=exp(sΔ), br=Hr²u, bR=HR²u, and d=R²-r². The semigroup relation bR=Hdbr and Jensen's inequality give

k_{r,R} = 1/2 ( H_d |b_r|² - |b_R|² ) ≥ 0. (F.1)

Localization adds an exact remainder:

∫χ k_{r,R} = 1/2∫χ(|b_r|²-|b_R|²) + 1/2∫(H_dχ-χ)|b_r|². (F.2)

For χ=1, a finite geometric scale chain satisfies

Σ_{n=0}^{N-1} ∫k_{R_{n+1},R_n} = 1/2 ( ||b_N||₂² - ||b_0||₂² ) ≤ 1/2 ||u||₂². (F.4)

The local regularity threshold, however, has inverse-scale critical normalization and scale-dependent times and centres. A finite unweighted sum alone cannot produce a normalized good scale.

03 / S.1--S.7

The complete signed band-energy budget at fixed scales

For filtered velocity, pressure, and stress, use the standard SGS sign for Πρ and the complete transport Jρ. The exact filtered energy equation is

∂_t e_ρ + ∇·J_ρ = -D_ρ - Π_ρ. (S.3)

With the nonnegative quantities kr,R and qr,R and transport difference Lr,R, subtracting the two scales gives

∂_t k_{r,R} + ∇·L_{r,R} = Π_R - H_d Π_r - q_{r,R}. (S.5)

For a nonnegative smooth spacetime test χ on [s,t], the complete identity is

⟨χ(t),k(t)⟩ + ∫⟨χ,q⟩ = ⟨χ(s),k(s)⟩ + ∫[⟨∂_tχ,k⟩ + ⟨∇χ,L⟩ + ⟨χ,Π_R-H_dΠ_r⟩]. (S.6)

Both time endpoints, time cutoff, spatial transport, pressure, stress, viscous transport, and moving-centre work remain. Positivity of k and q does not assign a sign to the flux difference. Fixed-positive-scale Leray-Hopf endpoints follow from strong time continuity of the smoothed fields, with no unproved uniform limit as R tends to zero.

04 / S.8--S.15

Layer mismatch returns as a complete single-scale budget

Allow each scale layer to use its own test χn, and define

A_m = H_{m-1}χ_{m-1} - χ_m, 1 ≤ m < N. (S.8)

The endpoint, flux, transport, time-derivative, and dissipation mismatches recombine as

[⟨A_m,e_m⟩]_s^t + ∫⟨A_m,D_m⟩ = ∫[⟨∂_tA_m,e_m⟩ + ⟨∇A_m,J_m⟩ - ⟨A_m,Π_m⟩]. (S.14)

One cannot cancel only the signed flux while discarding time, transport, dissipation, and endpoint payments. Since Am can change sign, S.14 is a fixed-smoothed-scale equality, not an insertion of a signed test into the original local energy inequality.

For critical weights wn=Rn-1, the actual mismatch is

A_m^(w) = w_{m-1} H_{m-1}χ_{m-1} - w_m χ_m. (S.15)

Shape, centre, time window, and critical weight are separate sources of mismatch.

05 / T.1--T.3

A nonnegative zero-mismatch heat dual cannot genuinely shrink-localize

Starting from nonzero χ0≥0, enforce zero layer mismatch:

χ_{n+1} = H_{d_n}χ_n, χ_n = H_{R_0²-R_n²}χ_0, ∫χ_n = ∫χ_0 = M > 0. (T.1-T.2)

Mass conservation rules out volume-scale mass. Uniformly over all centres xn,

∫_{B_{cR_n}(x_n)} χ_n / M ≤ (||χ_0||∞/M) (4π/3)c³R_n³ → 0. (T.3)

The normalized shape diffuses toward the coarse scale rather than concentrating at the prescribed centre. Shrinking time supports are likewise incompatible at one physical time. This rules out only the specified nonnegative zero-mismatch design, not methods that explicitly pay the time mismatch.

06 / T.4--T.6

Amplitude rescaling creates a theta-squared factor while the observable degenerates

After multiplying the heat weight by (Rn/R0)³ and defining Bn=Rn-1an,

B_{n+1} = θ² B_n + θ² R_n^{-1}ℓ_n. (T.5)

The second term is an unpaid nonnegative source. More basically, every L2 field obeys

B_n ≤ (||χ_0||∞ / (2R_0³)) R_n² ||u||₂² → 0. (T.6)

Only the amplitude becomes small; the normalized spatial shape does not improve. This automatic smallness cannot be renamed as smallness of the original same-scale moving energy without a separate comparison.

07 / T.7--T.12

A true smooth NS family with uniform initial L2 rules out universal transfer

For nonzero f≥0 compactly supported in the unit ball of R2 and κ>0, periodize a two-dimensional heat flow, subtract its mean, and set

u^(R)(t,x) = (0,0,V_R(t,x_1,x_2)), p^(R)=0, V_R = (κ/R) Σ_{m∈Z²} (e^{(t/R²)Δ}f)((x_h+2πm)/R) - κRM_f/(2π)². (T.7)

Because (u·∇)u=VR∂3u=0, this is a true globally smooth solution of the unforced three-dimensional periodic Navier-Stokes equations. Its initial norms satisfy

||u^(R)(0)||₂² ≤ 2πκ²||f||₂², ||∇u^(R)(0)||₂² = 2πκ²R^{-2}||∇f||₂². (T.8)

The initial L2 bound is uniform and the initial H1 bound is not. At the ordinary smooth time TR=128R², a Gaussian lower bound gives

E_R(T_R,0) ≥ κ²πc_f²/24 > 0, (T.11) B_R[u^(R)(T_R)] ≤ (πκ²||f||₂²/R_0³) R² → 0. (T.12)

There is therefore no uniform finite C with ER≤CBR, and no universal implication from BR→0 to ER→0 through one common vanishing modulus. Different R values use different smooth solutions and different terminal times, exactly as allowed by the universal claim under test.

08 / Strict limitations

The counterexample is not a singularity and does not refute regularity criteria

  • Every test solution is globally smooth; TR is an ordinary smooth time, not a first singular time.
  • The initial L2 norm is uniform but the initial H1 norm is not; comparisons depending on stronger initial norms are not ruled out.
  • TR=128R² tends to zero; time-dependent estimates allowed to degenerate with TR are not ruled out.
  • No comparison restricted to a first singular point or persistent bad scales at one centre is tested.
  • Paid mismatch methods, other filters or tests, G-P/G-C, and general regularity criteria are not refuted.
  • No good-scale source, critical summability, regularity statement, or singularity exclusion is obtained.
