## Fixed public sentences

- **Chinese:** `R0.73I 证明继承端点严格小于 1/450，并得到连续体上作用量与零窗口切向匹配；固定正窗口的匹配作用量仍为 OPEN。`
- **English:** `R0.73I proves that the inherited endpoint is strictly below 1/450 and obtains a continuum upper action and a matching zero-window tangent rate; the matching action at fixed positive window remains OPEN.`
- **Chinese:** `有限 WKB 一致性只用于选择下一条证明路线，不证明连续体两项绝热律。`
- **English:** `Finite WKB agreement selects the next proof route; it does not prove a continuum two-term adiabatic law.`
- **Chinese:** `FALSE AS INFERENCE 只否定从现有输入到目标结论的推断，不否定真实算子可能满足该结论。`
- **English:** `FALSE AS INFERENCE rejects only the implication from the present inputs; it does not deny that the exact operator may satisfy the target conclusion.`

## Banned equivalences

- `strict upper bound for d0` \(\ne\) `d0`;
- `zero-window tangent action` \(\ne\) `fixed-window action`;
- `action limit` \(\ne\) `bounded prefactor`;
- `finite top branch` \(\ne\) `continuum selected branch`;
- `finite WKB correction` \(\ne\) `continuum two-term asymptotic`;
- `gain-normalized departure` \(\ne\) `action-prescribed departure`;
- `planar parameter-dependent family` \(\ne\) `fixed-background 3D instability`;
- `transient amplification` \(\ne\) `finite-time singularity`;
- any R0.73I statement \(\ne\) a resolution of the Clay problem.
