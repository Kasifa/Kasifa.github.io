# Figure contract

## Analytical question and takeaway

**Question.** In the dissipative one-carrier chain, which parts of the
R0.72M action screen are rigorous, which are only finite diagnostics, and
which direct cubic estimate survives?

**Supported takeaway.** The rigorous moment barrier lowers the actual
enstrophy scale, but the proved first-layer action lower bound forces the
actual action-poor ratio to diverge and leaves the scalar screen active. A
project corollary derived from Coble–He Theorem 1.2 proves a sublinear
square-root true-cubic bound. The finite normalized proxies suggest a sharper
logarithmic behavior but do not prove it.

## Theorem/diagnostic distinction

- Rigorous curves use dark neutral, marker-free dash-dot lines and the route
  `analytic theorem`.
- Producer finite diagnostics use blue circles and solid lines.
- Independent finite diagnostics use ochre squares, dashed lines, and open
  marker fill.
- Within Panels B and D, observable identity is also encoded by filled/open
  markers and solid/dotted line style, so grayscale does not rely on color.
- The rigorous moment curve is exactly
  `max(1, (2 sigma)^(2/3))`; the scalar ceiling is exactly one.
- The constants in the action lower bound and in the project square-root
  corollary derived from Coble–He Theorem 1.2 are not numerical outputs of
  the proof and therefore are not plotted as arbitrary benchmark heights.
- No regression or extrapolated curve appears in the formal figure.

## Claim boundary

The theorem applies only to the declared fixed-band, row-aligned dissipative
one-carrier chain. The plotted producer and independent curves are finite
binary64 corroboration. They are not interval certificates, do not prove a
logarithmic cubic law, and do not establish a common-band multi-carrier
estimate, a continuation criterion, finite-time singularity, or global
three-dimensional Navier--Stokes regularity.
