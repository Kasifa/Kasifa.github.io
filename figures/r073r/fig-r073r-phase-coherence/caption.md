# Figure caption

**Matched Fourier data and phase-sensitive heat-flow scaling.**
**A,** The positive Fourier packet of the normalized fields

\[
W_{R,m}(x)=\frac{\sqrt2}{m}e_3\operatorname{Re}\!\left[
e^{iNx_1}R_m(e^{ix_1})R_m(e^{ix_2})\right],\qquad N=8m,
\]

for \(R_m=D_m\) and the Rudin--Shapiro polynomial \(P_m\), shown at
\(m=8\). The two fields have the same \(2m^2\) Fourier sites, coefficient
moduli \(1/(\sqrt2m)\), \(L^2\) norm, and every quadratic
Fourier-weighted Sobolev norm. Filled circles and open squares encode the
coefficient signs on the displayed positive packet; the negative packet is
its conjugate reflection. **B,** Analytic unscaled exponent guides,
normalized at \(m=1\):
\(\|W_{D,m}\|_{\mathfrak X}\asymp m^{1/6}\),
\(\|W_{P,m}\|_{\mathfrak X}\asymp m^{-1/2}\), and ratio
\(\asymp m^{2/3}\). **C,** After
\(\alpha_m=\sqrt8\,m^{-1/6}\), the common \(L^2\) guide is
\(m^{-1/6}\), the Dirichlet and Rudin--Shapiro heat-flow guides are
\(1\) and \(m^{-2/3}\), respectively, and the shared \(\dot H^{1/2}\)
guide is \(m^{1/3}\). Panels B and C display analytic powers with fixed
comparison constants suppressed; they are not fitted data or simulations.
The fields themselves have zero convection and evolve as globally smooth
heat flows, so failure of a small-norm entrance condition is not evidence of
singular dynamics.
