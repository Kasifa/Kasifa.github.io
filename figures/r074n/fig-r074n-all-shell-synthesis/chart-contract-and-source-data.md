  cancellation between shells or packets?

  range each obey a bound of order \(C\Gamma_jLR^5\); their exact disjoint sum

- **Data sufficiency:** `source-data.csv` contains the 20 exact constants and

- **Palette:** three restrained roots plus neutrals because the three shell
  ranges are semantic categories.  Direct labels, solid/dashed/dotted
  outlines, box shape, and panel position preserve all distinctions without
  color.
- **Boundary:** schematic, not to scale, no DNS, no sampled stochastic path,
  familywise only, **NOT CLAY**.
