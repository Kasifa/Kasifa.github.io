**Figure R0.73S. What the quadratic autocorrelation certificate sees, and
what it cannot remove.** **A**, for the bounded, asymptotically fixed-quartic
Dirichlet-spike family,
the exact normalized sixth moment \(\Theta\) and the rigorous
\(D_C\)-branch certificate \(\Gamma^{3/2}\sqrt{D_C}\) have the same
square-root support exponent; the dotted line is the exact asymptotic guide
\((11/40)\sqrt{D_C}\). **B**, for the matched R0.73R Fourier pair after its
common scaling, solid curves are the computable certificate
\(m^{-2/3}(AQ)^{1/6}\) and open-marker curves are the exact sixth-moment
guide \(m^{-2/3}\Theta^{1/6}\). The certificate keeps the Dirichlet branch
order one and sends the Rudin--Shapiro branch to zero. **C**, no-carry
lacunary products have identical support, coefficient moduli, \(L^2\), and
\(L^4\), but their \(L^6\)-norm ratio is exactly
\((323/311)^{r/6}\) at every integer depth; the shaded interval marks the
sealed rows \(r=1,\ldots,8\), while \(r=0\) is the empty-product identity.
The unbounded ratio is an exact \(r\to\infty\) consequence, not an inference
from the finite window. All curves are formulas or rigorous upper bounds,
not fitted data or a PDE simulation. The annular sharpness lifts are
zero-convection shear flows and do not indicate singular dynamics.
